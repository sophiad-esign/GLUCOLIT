## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do genetic and environmental factors interact to influence glucose homeostasis trajectories in children and young adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 8783 participants aged 7-24 years from the Avon Longitudinal Study of Parents and Children (ALSPAC).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Polygenic risk scores (PRSs) for type 2 diabetes, fasting glucose, insulin, and BMI, along with environmental factors such as lifestyle, diet, and maternal characteristics.
- **对照 / Comparison:** PRS-enhanced models vs. models using environmental factors alone.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glycaemic outcomes including insulin resistance, prediabetes (impaired fasting glucose), and type 2 diabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** PRS-enhanced models outperformed environmental factor models in predicting glycaemic outcomes. Gene-environment interactions, such as high genetic risk combined with increased screen time or younger maternal age, increased insulin resistance risk.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is limited to participants of European ancestry, which may affect the generalizability of the results. The observational nature of the study precludes causal inference.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While genetic factors are significant, modifiable environmental factors can influence glucose homeostasis, suggesting potential for early prevention strategies.

## 原文精华摘要

### 研究背景

越来越多的年轻人面临血糖控制问题，这可能导致糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病。 了解根本原因是预防和早期干预的关键。

### 为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

理解遗传与生活方式之间的相互作用对于制定有效的预防糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病的策略至关重要，尤其是在青少年中。

### 你可以带走的重点

- 遗传因素显著影响青少年的血糖平衡。
- 屏幕使用时间和母亲年龄等环境因素与遗传风险相互作用。
- 体力活动和健康意识可以减轻胰岛素抵抗的风险。
- 生活方式的修改是早期预防糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病的可行目标。

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background

Young people are increasingly facing issues with blood sugar control, which can lead to prediabetes and type 2 diabetes. Understanding the root causes is crucial for prevention and early intervention.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the interplay between genetics and lifestyle is crucial for developing effective prevention strategies against prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, especially in youth.

### Practical Takeaways

- Genetic factors significantly influence glucose homeostasis in youth.
- Environmental factors like screen time and maternal age interact with genetic risk.
- Physical activity and health awareness can mitigate the risk of insulin resistance.
- Lifestyle modifications are actionable targets for early prevention of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes.
