## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does oral magnesium supplementation impact glycemic regulation and cardiometabolic outcomes in prediabetic adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Prediabetic adults
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Oral magnesium supplementation
- **对照 / Comparison:** Placebo
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Fasting plasma glucose, 2-hour OGTT glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, blood pressure, BMI, waist circumference, lipid parameters, high-sensitivity C-reactive protein, and serum magnesium
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Non-significant reduction in FPG; significant improvements in 2-hour OGTT glucose, HOMA-IR, triglycerides, HDL-C, and serum magnesium
- **局限性 / Limits:** Small sample size across included trials; non-significant effect on fasting blood sugar; need for larger, standardized trials
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While oral magnesium supplementation showed some promise in improving certain cardiometabolic outcomes in prediabetic adults, its effect on fasting blood sugar was not significant. More research is needed to establish the role of magnesium in diabetes prevention strategies.

### 研究背景 糖尿病前期是一个警示信号，表明血糖水平高于正常但尚未达到糖尿病的诊断标准。 这是一个如果不加以处理可能会导致2型糖尿病和其他健康问题的状态。 镁是一种在许多身体过程中发挥作用的矿物质，包括胰岛素信号传导和葡萄糖代谢，可能有助于管理糖尿病前期。

### 核心发现 这项系统回顾和元分析着眼于比较口服镁补充与安慰剂在糖尿病前期成人中的效果的研究。 主要结果是空腹血浆葡萄糖，其他结果包括胰岛素敏感性和脂质谱。

### 你的解读与批判 该综述表明，尽管镁并没有显著降低空腹血糖，但它确实改善了其他指标，例如身体如何处理餐后血糖和胰岛素抵抗。 它还对脂质水平产生了积极影响，这对心脏健康很重要。 然而，对空腹血糖的影响并不显著，而且包括的研究相对较小。

### 临床/商业启发 对于糖尿病前期患者来说，这些发现表明镁可能是生活方式改变的有益补充。 需要注意的是，这并不能取代健康饮食和定期锻炼的需要，但可以作为预防糖尿病的更广泛策略的一部分。 需要更多的研究来确认这些效果，并确定镁的最佳形式和剂量。

### 要点 - 糖尿病前期是2型糖尿病的风险因素。 - 镁可能有助于管理糖尿病前期的某些方面。 - 对空腹血糖的影响并不显著。 - 需要更多的研究来完全理解镁的作用。

### 为何相关 了解如何管理糖尿病前期对于预防糖尿病及其并发症至关重要。 镁可能是该管理的一部分，但它不是单独的解决方案。

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background Prediabetes is a warning sign that blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet high enough to be diagnosed as diabetes. It's a state that can lead to type 2 diabetes and other health issues if not addressed.

Magnesium, a mineral that plays a role in many body processes, including insulin signaling and glucose metabolism, might help in managing prediabetes.

### Core Findings This systematic review and meta-analysis looked at studies comparing oral magnesium supplementation to a placebo in prediabetic adults.

The main outcome was fasting plasma glucose, with other outcomes including insulin sensitivity and lipid profiles.

### My Interpretation and Critique The review suggests that while magnesium didn't significantly lower fasting blood sugar, it did improve other markers such as how the body handles sugar after a meal and insulin resistance.

It also positively affected lipid levels, which is important for heart health. However, the effect on fasting blood sugar was not significant, and the studies included were relatively small.

### Clinical/Business Insights For those with prediabetes, these findings suggest that magnesium could be a helpful addition to lifestyle changes.

It's important to note that this doesn't replace the need for a healthy diet and regular exercise but could be a part of a broader strategy to prevent diabetes.

More research is needed to confirm these effects and to determine the best forms and doses of magnesium.

### Takeaways - Prediabetes is a risk factor for type 2 diabetes. - Magnesium might help manage some aspects of prediabetes. - Effects on fasting blood sugar were not significant.

- More research is needed to fully understand magnesium's role.

### Why Relevant Understanding how to manage prediabetes is crucial for preventing diabetes and its complications. Magnesium could be a part of that management, but it's not a standalone solution.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how to manage prediabetes is crucial for preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes and its associated complications.

This systematic review provides insights into a potential dietary supplement that could aid in prediabetes management, which is relevant for both clinical practice and public health strategies.

### Practical Takeaways

- Prediabetes is a condition that, if not managed, can progress to type 2 diabetes.
- Magnesium supplementation may offer some benefits for managing prediabetes, though not all effects were significant.
- Larger, more standardized trials are needed to confirm the role of magnesium in prediabetes management.
- Magnesium could be a cost-effective addition to lifestyle interventions for diabetes prevention.
