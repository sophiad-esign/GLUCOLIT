## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can glutathione supplementation alleviate oxidative stress and restore metabolic balance in insulin-resistant cells?
- **研究对象 / Population:** L6 skeletal myotubes, a model for muscle cells, were used to study the effects of glutathione on insulin resistance induced by palmitic acid.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Glutathione (0.1 and 1 mM) was preconditioned and supplemented to observe its effects on oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction.
- **对照 / Comparison:** The metabolic perturbations were compared before and after glutathione supplementation in the presence of palmitic acid.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Levels of nucleotides, GSH metabolism intermediates, amino acids, and other metabolites were measured using 1H NMR spectroscopy.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Glutathione supplementation at 1 mM led to the partial restoration of metabolic imbalances observed in various metabolites.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in vitro using a cell model, and the results may not directly translate to human metabolism.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study suggests that glutathione may play a role in managing insulin resistance, it is important to consider it as part of a broader approach to diet and lifestyle modifications.
