## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of a healthy lifestyle on all-cause and cardiovascular disease mortality in individuals with cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome?
- **研究对象 / Population:** The study analyzed 306,831 and 9,823 CKM individuals from UK Biobank and NHANES (2007-2018).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A healthy lifestyle score was created based on seven factors: no current smoking, moderate drinking, healthy diet, regular physical activity, adequate sleep, low sedentary behavior, and appropriate social connection.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons were made between individuals with higher and lower lifestyle scores.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Primary outcomes were all-cause and cardiovascular disease mortality from linked health records and death registries.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Higher lifestyle scores were associated with reduced risks of all-cause (HR: 0.81, 95% CI: 0.80-0.82) and CVD mortality (HR: 0.82, 95% CI: 0.80-0.84) in UK Biobank. In NHANES, higher scores were associated with 15% (95% CI: 0.80-0.90) and 11% (95% CI: 0.81-0.99) lower mortality risks.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is observational and does not establish causation. The sample is limited to CKM individuals from UK Biobank and NHANES, and results may not be generalizable to all populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with CKM syndrome, adopting a healthy lifestyle, particularly regular physical activity and low sedentary behavior, can significantly reduce the risk of all-cause and cardiovascular disease mortality. However, it's important to discuss specific lifestyle changes with a healthcare provider as part of a personalized health plan.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up one day and realizing that by making a few changes to your daily routine, you could significantly reduce your risk of dying from heart disease or other health complications. This isn't just a dream;

it's a reality backed by scientific evidence.

A recent study has shown that individuals with cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome can lower their risk of all-cause and cardiovascular disease (CVD) mortality by adopting a healthy lifestyle.

This includes regular physical activity, a healthy diet, adequate sleep, and maintaining social connections.

The study analyzed data from over 306,000 CKM individuals and found that those with higher lifestyle scores had a reduced risk of mortality.

The benefits were even more pronounced in the early stages of CKM syndrome, emphasizing the importance of early intervention.

While this study doesn't prescribe a specific exercise regimen, it does highlight the importance of regular physical activity and low sedentary behavior in reducing mortality risks.

It's a reminder that taking care of your health is not just about treating diseases but also about making lifestyle choices that can prevent them.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome is a major health concern that affects a significant portion of the population.

Understanding how lifestyle factors can influence mortality risks is crucial for individuals looking to manage their health and prevent disease progression.

Practical Takeaways

- Adopting a healthy lifestyle can significantly reduce the risk of mortality for individuals with CKM syndrome.
- Regular physical activity and maintaining low sedentary behavior are key components of a healthy lifestyle that can lower mortality risks.
- The benefits of a healthy lifestyle are more pronounced in the early stages of CKM syndrome, emphasizing the importance of early intervention.
- Making lifestyle changes is not just about treating diseases but also about preventing them through proactive health choices.
