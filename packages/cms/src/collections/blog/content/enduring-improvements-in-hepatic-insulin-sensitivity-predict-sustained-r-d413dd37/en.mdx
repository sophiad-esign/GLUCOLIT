## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What factors are associated with sustained remission of prediabetes during a 3-year lifestyle intervention?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with prediabetes and overweight/obesity
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** 8-week rapid weight loss followed by 148-week lifestyle intervention for weight loss maintenance
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of metabolic markers and outcomes between participants who achieved sustained remission and those who did not
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Sustained remission of prediabetes, weight loss, fat mass loss, hepatic insulin sensitivity
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Only 12% of participants achieved sustained remission. Those who did had greater improvements in hepatic insulin sensitivity, regardless of weight change.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study had a low rate of sustained remission, which limits the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While weight loss is important, improving liver insulin sensitivity may also be crucial for managing prediabetes and preventing type 2 diabetes.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the factors that contribute to the remission of prediabetes is crucial for developing effective prevention strategies and improving health outcomes for those at risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

### Practical Takeaways

- Sustained remission of prediabetes is rare but achievable with lifestyle interventions.
- Improvements in hepatic insulin sensitivity are linked to better outcomes, independent of weight loss.
- Weight loss is beneficial, but other factors, such as liver health, also play a crucial role.
- More research is needed to understand the full spectrum of factors influencing prediabetes remission.
