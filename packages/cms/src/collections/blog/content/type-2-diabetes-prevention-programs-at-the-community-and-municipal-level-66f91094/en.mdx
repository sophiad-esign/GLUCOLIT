## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do community-based lifestyle interventions, particularly those focusing on diet, contribute to the prevention of type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Healthy adults and adults at increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes, including individuals with prediabetes or metabolic syndrome.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle-based interventions targeting physical activity, nutrition, or broader behavioral change.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Controlled study designs including (cluster-)randomized controlled trials, non-randomized controlled studies, and controlled clinical trials.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prevention of type 2 diabetes or delay of progression from prediabetes to manifest disease.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Of 464 records identified, 26 studies were included, showing varied approaches to community-based interventions, with a focus on group-based education and limited reporting of theoretical frameworks.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The evidence base was dominated by studies conducted in high-income countries, with limited representation from lower-resource settings. Definitions and operationalization of 'community-based' delivery varied across studies.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While community-based interventions show promise in preventing type 2 diabetes, there is a need for more diverse and rigorously designed studies, especially in lower-resource settings. Readers should be cautious in generalizing these findings and consider the variability in intervention approaches.
