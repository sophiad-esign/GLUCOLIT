## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do combined diet and physical activity interventions outperform diet-only or PA-only approaches for health-related outcomes in people with overweight or obesity?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with overweight or obesity, including those with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic risk.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Combined physical activity and diet interventions.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Diet-only or physical activity-only interventions.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Anthropometric outcomes (weight, BMI, waist circumference, fat mass), cardiometabolic outcomes (glucose control, insulin resistance, lipids, inflammation), and physical fitness outcomes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Combined interventions generally outperformed single-component comparators for reducing weight, BMI, waist circumference, and fat mass, and improving cardiorespiratory fitness. Benefits were more consistent and durable in programs lasting ≥6-12 months with frequent, structured contact and supervised, resistance-inclusive exercise.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Review quality varied, with 69% rated as low or critically low. Overlap between systematic reviews was minimal.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, combining diet and physical activity in a structured program lasting 6-12 months, with regular supervision and resistance training, may offer the best chance to improve health outcomes and reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

## English Plain-Language Version

Balancing diet and physical activity is key to better health. For prediabetes, this balance is crucial. Studies show combining diet and exercise is more effective than focusing on one alone.

In adults, this approach leads to greater weight loss and improved fitness. Programs lasting 6-12 months with regular, supervised exercise, including resistance training, are particularly effective.

For children, family involvement is key, but maintaining changes is a challenge. In older adults, these programs help maintain muscle while losing fat.

Across various settings, including digital delivery, combined interventions improve glucose control and insulin resistance, cutting the risk of developing type 2 diabetes by about half.

Despite varying review quality, structured lifestyle programs, including digital options, can be powerful tools in managing prediabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical stage where lifestyle interventions can prevent or delay the onset of type 2 diabetes.

Understanding the effectiveness of combined diet and exercise interventions is essential for developing personalized and sustainable health plans.

Practical Takeaways

- Combined diet and exercise interventions are more effective than single-component approaches for managing prediabetes.
- Programs lasting 6-12 months with regular, supervised exercise, including resistance training, show the most consistent benefits.
- Family engagement is crucial for children and adolescents to maintain improvements in BMI.
- Digital delivery models can be a scalable solution for structured lifestyle programs.
- Long-term lifestyle programs can reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes by about half.
