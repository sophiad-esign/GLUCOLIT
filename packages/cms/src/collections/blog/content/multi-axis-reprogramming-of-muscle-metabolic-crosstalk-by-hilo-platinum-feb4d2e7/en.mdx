## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can HiLo Platinum™ supplementation mitigate muscle strength decline in prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Male Sprague Dawley rats assigned to normal diet or prediabetic conditions induced by a cholesterol- and fat-enriched diet with fructose.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** HiLo Platinum™ supplementation at two different doses.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between prediabetic rats with and without HiLo Platinum™ supplementation.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Muscle strength, blood glucose, lipid profiles, inflammatory markers, mitochondrial function, anabolic signaling, and gut microbiome composition.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** HiLo Platinum™ improved muscle strength, reduced blood glucose, improved lipid profiles, showed anti-inflammatory effects, upregulated mitochondrial and metabolic regulators, and altered gut microbiome composition.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was preclinical and conducted in rats, limiting direct applicability to humans. The short duration of the study also limits understanding of long-term effects.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests potential benefits of HiLo Platinum™ in managing prediabetes-induced muscle decline, further research is needed to confirm these effects in humans and over longer periods.
