## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does self-reflection influence the sustainability of physical activity among individuals who have participated in a Diabetes Prevention Program?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Participants in the Small Steps for Big Changes Diabetes Prevention Program, consisting of 86 individuals who logged daily physical activity and recorded reflections via a mobile app for one year.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Daily physical activity logging and optional reflection on exercise experiences.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of self-reflection frequencies with physical activity engagement, analyzed over a one-year period.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Physical activity engagement, self-reflection frequency, and the presence of facilitators and barriers over time.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Higher self-reflection frequencies correlated with increased physical activity engagement. Self-monitoring was present in 73% of reflections, and facilitators were mentioned more frequently than barriers, with the latter declining over time.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on self-reported data and reflections from a specific Diabetes Prevention Program, which may limit the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, incorporating self-reflection into daily routines may support the sustainability of physical activity, which is crucial for diabetes prevention and metabolic health management.

## 原文精华摘要

开始一个新的锻炼习惯，但随着时间推移，坚持下去变得困难。 对于许多糖尿病前期患者来说，这是常见的情况，他们试图预防2型糖尿病。 最近的一项研究观察了糖尿病预防计划中的人们如何保持锻炼，重点关注自我反思的作用。

研究发现，那些经常反思自己锻炼习惯的人更有可能保持活跃。 他们跟踪自己的活动并考虑他们的环境，这帮助他们克服障碍，使锻炼成为他们生活的一部分。 这种洞见至关重要，因为保持规律的体力活动是降低2型糖尿病风险和维持体重减轻的关键。

自我反思不仅仅是记录你的锻炼； 它是关于理解什么帮助你，什么阻碍你的锻炼习惯。 在一年的过程中，研究中的参与者提到了比障碍更多的促进因素，表明随着时间的推移，他们找到了更多让锻炼适应他们的方法。 这种向积极策略的转变可以是维持长期体力活动的强大工具。

虽然研究提供了宝贵的见解，但重要的是要记住每个人的情况都是不同的。 关键的收获是，自我反思可以是一种个性化的方法，以理解和克服我们在维持积极生活方式时面临的独特挑战。

将自我反思纳入糖尿病预防计划可以提供重要的支持。 它允许参与者意识到、记录和响应体力活动的实时障碍和促进因素。 这种方法可以帮助根据个人需求定制干预措施，可能导致更有效和可持续的行为改变。

总之，如果你想开始或维持一个锻炼习惯，考虑自我反思如何帮助你保持正轨。 反思什么激励你，什么阻碍你，以及你如何调整你的环境来支持你的目标。 这种主动的方法可以是迈向更好健康和降低2型糖尿病风险的一步。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

体力活动是糖尿病预防的基石，但保持规律锻炼可能具有挑战性。 这项研究强调了自我反思在帮助糖尿病前期个体坚持他们的锻炼习惯中的作用，这对于管理他们的健康和降低发展2型糖尿病的风险至关重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Starting an exercise routine is a common goal, but maintaining it over time can be difficult. For those with prediabetes aiming to prevent type 2 diabetes, regular physical activity is crucial.

A study analyzed how self-reflection can aid in sustaining exercise habits.

Participants who regularly reflected on their exercise were more likely to stay active. They tracked their activities and considered their environment, which helped them overcome barriers and integrate exercise into their lives.

This is significant because regular physical activity is key to reducing the risk of type 2 diabetes and maintaining weight loss.

Understanding what helps and hinders one's exercise routine is vital. Over the year, participants mentioned more facilitators than barriers, indicating a shift towards positive strategies for sustaining physical activity.

This insight suggests that self-reflection can be a powerful tool for personalizing exercise routines.

While the study offers valuable insights, it's important to remember that individual circumstances vary. The key takeaway is that self-reflection can help understand and overcome unique challenges in maintaining an active lifestyle.

Incorporating self-reflection into diabetes prevention programs could provide significant support. It allows participants to be aware, document, and respond to real-time barriers and facilitators of physical activity.

This approach can help tailor interventions to individual needs, potentially leading to more effective and sustainable behavior changes.

Reflecting on what motivates you and what gets in your way can help you stay on track with your exercise routine. This proactive approach can contribute to better health and a reduced risk of type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Physical activity is a cornerstone of diabetes prevention, but maintaining regular exercise can be challenging.

This study highlights the role of self-reflection in helping individuals with prediabetes adhere to their exercise routines, which is essential for managing their health and reducing the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular self-reflection can help sustain physical activity, which is crucial for diabetes prevention.
- Understanding personal facilitators and barriers to exercise can lead to more effective and sustainable behavior changes.
- Self-monitoring of physical activity is a common facilitator that can be integrated into daily routines.
- As time progresses, individuals often find more ways to facilitate their exercise routines, indicating a positive adaptation.
- Incorporating self-reflection tools in diabetes prevention programs may enhance adherence to physical activity.
