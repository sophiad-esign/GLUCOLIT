## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can an app-based digital therapeutics program integrating continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) with lifestyle coaching improve glycemic control in individuals with type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults aged ≥18 years with confirmed type 2 diabetes and baseline hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) between 7.5% and 9.5%.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Use of the Goodflip mobile app, which includes a 15-day CGM period, personalized diet, exercise, and cognitive behavioral therapy plans, supported by multidisciplinary coaching.
- **对照 / Comparison:** No specific comparison group mentioned, but the study evaluates changes from baseline measures.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Changes in HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose, postprandial glucose, and CGM metrics; lipid profile, anthropometric indices, quality of life, and behavioral determinants of glycemic improvement.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Mean HbA1c decreased by 0.59%, postprandial glucose decreased by 25.42 mg/dL, and time in range increased by 9.98%. Quality of life improved significantly for sleep quality. Modest reductions in low-density lipoprotein cholesterol, body fat, and waist circumference were observed.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study had a small sample size and short duration, which limits the generalizability and long-term applicability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that an integrated app-based program can improve glycemic control and quality of life in individuals with type 2 diabetes, readers should be cautious in overinterpreting the results due to the study's limitations. It highlights the potential of digital therapeutics and lifestyle coaching but indicates the need for further research.

管理糖尿病就像驾驶一艘船，需要指南针来指引方向，舵来控制。

一项研究显示，一个结合CGM和生活方式指导的应用程序GLIDE-C可以帮助降低2型糖尿病患者的血糖水平。

该应用程序提供个性化的饮食、运动和认知行为疗法计划。

## English Plain-Language Version

Managing diabetes is like steering a ship, needing a compass for direction and a rudder for control. CGM provides the compass by offering real-time glucose levels, while lifestyle changes act as the rudder.

A study presented evidence that an app integrating CGM with lifestyle coaching, known as GLIDE-C, can assist in lowering blood sugar levels in individuals with type 2 diabetes.

The app offers personalized diet, exercise, and cognitive behavioral therapy plans. Participants experienced a reduction in HbA1c levels, indicating improved long-term blood sugar control.

They also reported better sleep quality and improvements in motivation and stress management. These improvements were achieved without altering medications, underscoring the significant impact of lifestyle changes on diabetes management.

However, given the study's small scale and short duration, further research is necessary to confirm these findings.

For now, it serves as a reminder of the power of lifestyle changes in conjunction with healthcare team support in managing diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Effective diabetes management is essential for preventing complications and enhancing the quality of life.

The study sheds light on how digital tools combined with personalized coaching can support lifestyle changes critical to glycemic control.

Practical Takeaways

- An app integrating CGM with lifestyle coaching can assist in managing type 2 diabetes.
- The study's participants showed a decrease in HbA1c levels.
- Lifestyle changes can significantly impact diabetes management without medication alterations.
- Participants reported improvements in sleep quality and stress management.
- Further research is needed to confirm the long-term effects of such interventions.
