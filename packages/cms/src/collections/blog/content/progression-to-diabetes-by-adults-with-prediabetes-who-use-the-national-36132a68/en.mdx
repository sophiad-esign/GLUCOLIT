## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the real-world effectiveness of the National Diabetes Prevention Program (NDPP) in preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes among adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 3.73 million adults with diagnosed prediabetes, 245,896 of whom had at least one claim for lifestyle treatment.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Participation in the NDPP, an intensive lifestyle intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison with adults who received lower intensity lifestyle services.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Progression to type 2 diabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** NDPP-treated patients had 1.94 fewer cases of diabetes at 12 months and 1.58 fewer cases at 24 months compared to those who did not participate in NDPP.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study followed patients for up to two years, which may not capture long-term outcomes, and potential biases in the data.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For adults with prediabetes, participating in the NDPP may reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes in the short term.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the effectiveness of lifestyle interventions like the NDPP is crucial for individuals with prediabetes and for the healthcare industry, as it can guide the development of more effective prevention strategies.

### Practical Takeaways

- NDPP can help reduce the progression to diabetes among adults with prediabetes.
- Participation in NDPP is associated with lower rates of diabetes development within 1-2 years.
- The study's design helps to control for individual risk factors, offering a clearer picture of NDPP's benefits.
- The potential limitations include biases in the data and the short follow-up period.
