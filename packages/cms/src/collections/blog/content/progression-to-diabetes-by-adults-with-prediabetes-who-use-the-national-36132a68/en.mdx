> 原文精华摘要
> 随着生活方式的改变，糖尿病前期的人数不断增加，这不仅增加了个人健康风险，也给医疗系统带来了压力。糖尿病前期意味着血糖水平高于正常但尚未达到2型糖尿病的诊断标准，这是一个预防或延缓糖尿病发作的关键时期。
> 研究表明，参与国家糖尿病预防计划（NDPP）的糖尿病前期成人在1-2年内发展为糖尿病的风险较低。这一发现强调了强化生活方式干预在糖尿病前期管理中的重要性。
> 该研究采用工具变量方法，为NDPP的影响提供了稳健估计，同时考虑了个体风险因素。然而，短暂的跟踪期限制了对长期效果的评估。此外，数据中的潜在偏差也可能影响结果的解释。尽管如此，这项研究为糖尿病前期个体提供了有价值的见解，并为医疗保健行业提供了开发更有效预防策略的指导。
> 对于糖尿病前期的个体来说，参与像NDPP这样的强化生活方式干预措施可能是减少发展为2型糖尿病风险的有效途径。对于健康科技行业而言，增强此类项目，提高其可访问性和参与度，将是一个重要的发展方向。这不仅有助于改善个体健康，也可能减轻医疗系统的负担。

- 国家糖尿病预防计划（NDPP）可能帮助减少糖尿病前期成人发展为2型糖尿病的风险。
- 参与NDPP与1-2年内糖尿病发展率较低相关。
- 研究的设计有助于控制个体风险因素，提供了NDPP益处的更清晰画面。
- 潜在的局限性包括数据中的偏差和短暂的跟踪期。
  English Plain-Language Version

Prediabetes is a growing concern as it signals a higher risk of developing type 2 diabetes. The National Diabetes Prevention Program (NDPP) offers a structured approach to lifestyle changes that can help delay or prevent the onset of diabetes. This study provides robust evidence that participation in NDPP is associated with a lower risk of progression to diabetes within 1-2 years among adults with prediabetes. However, the short follow-up period and potential biases in the data are limitations that should be considered. Despite these, the findings offer valuable insights for individuals with prediabetes and guide the healthcare industry in developing more effective prevention strategies. For the health tech industry, enhancing such programs to increase accessibility and engagement is a promising direction, benefiting both individual health and reducing the burden on healthcare systems.

- The National Diabetes Prevention Program (NDPP) may help reduce the risk of adults with prediabetes developing type 2 diabetes.
- Participation in NDPP is associated with lower rates of diabetes development within 1-2 years.
- The study's design helps to control for individual risk factors, offering a clearer picture of NDPP's benefits.
- Potential limitations include biases in the data and the short follow-up period.
