> 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of diabetes education on prediabetes and early metabolic risk?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adult patients with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, including racial/ethnic minority groups.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Structured diabetes education programs that emphasize lifestyle modifications.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Standard care without structured education.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Blood glucose, HbA1c, lipids, blood pressure, and body weight.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Structured diabetes education can improve diabetes outcomes as measured by blood glucose, time in glucose range, HbA1c, lipids, blood pressure, and body weight.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The long-term effects of these programs are not fully known, and cost-effectiveness is rarely estimated.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, engaging in structured diabetes education may help improve their health outcomes, but the long-term benefits and cost implications require further investigation.

  English Plain-Language Version

Why it matters

Diabetes management involves more than medication; it requires lifestyle changes.

For prediabetes or early metabolic risk individuals, integrating diabetes management into daily life is crucial.

What the evidence says

Structured diabetes education improves blood glucose, HbA1c, lipids, blood pressure, and body weight.

It emphasizes physical activity, mental health, and healthy eating.

What this means

Diabetes education must be accessible and tailored to individual needs.

Considering social status, education, age, language, and cultural background is essential for personalized empowerment.

What readers can do

Regular physical activity and monitoring diet are advised for prediabetes readers.

Developing personalized diabetes management tools can improve health outcomes and adherence.
Understanding the importance of diabetes education is relevant for anyone with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, as it can significantly impact their ability to manage their condition and prevent the onset of diabetes.

- Diabetes education plays a critical role in managing prediabetes and early metabolic risk.
- Structured education can lead to improvements in blood glucose and other health parameters.
- Personalized empowerment is key to integrating diabetes management into daily life.
- The long-term effects and cost-effectiveness of diabetes education programs are not fully understood.
