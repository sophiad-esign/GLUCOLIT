## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can including social partners in a telehealth intervention improve diabetes prevention and management among South Asian Americans?
- **研究对象 / Population:** South Asian Americans with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes and their social partners.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Culturally tailored telehealth intervention involving health education sessions.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Preintervention and postintervention surveys.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Changes in HbA1c, BMI, blood pressure, physical activity, and dietary choices.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Participants showed improvements in HbA1c and systolic blood pressure. More participants exercised at least 150 minutes weekly. Social partners increased fruit and vegetable intake and daily moderate exercise.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was a single-arm pilot without a control group, limiting the strength of the conclusions.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that social support can enhance diabetes management, further research is needed to confirm these findings.

## 原文精华摘要

### 研究背景

南亚裔美国人中，糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病患病率较高，他们通常生活在关系紧密的社区中。 这种环境为利用社交网络进行健康干预提供了独特的机会。

### 核心发现

研究发现，在远程医疗干预中包括社会伴侣可以改善糖尿病前期或2型糖尿病患者的糖化血红蛋白水平和体力活动。

### 你的解读与批判

研究结果令人鼓舞，表明社会支持可以增强糖尿病管理。 然而，缺少对照组限制了结论的力度。 需要更大规模的研究来验证这些发现。

### 临床/商业启发

将家庭成员纳入健康干预可能会改善结果。 整合家庭支持的健康科技解决方案可能产生更广泛的影响，尤其是当针对不同人群量身定制时。

### 为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

这项研究之所以相关，是因为它探索了社会支持网络在改善糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病健康结果方面的潜力，这是重要的公共卫生问题。

### 你可以带走的重点

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background

Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes are prevalent among South Asian Americans, who often live in close-knit communities. This setting offers a unique opportunity to leverage social networks for health interventions.

### Core Findings

The pilot study showed improvements in HbA1c levels and physical activity among participants with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes when social partners were included in the telehealth intervention.

### Your Interpretation and Critique

The study's results are encouraging, indicating that social support can enhance diabetes management. However, the lack of a control group limits the conclusions' strength.

Further research with larger samples is needed to validate these findings.

### Clinical/Business Insights

Involving family in health interventions may improve outcomes. Health tech solutions that integrate family support could have a broader impact, especially when tailored to diverse populations.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant because it explores the potential of social support networks in improving health outcomes for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, which are significant public health concerns.

### Practical Takeaways

- Social support can play a significant role in diabetes prevention and management.
- Involving family members in health interventions may improve outcomes.
- Larger, controlled studies are needed to validate these preliminary findings.
- Health tech solutions should consider integrating social network support.
