## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the research hotspots, trends, and molecular mechanisms of diabetes and sarcopenia, and how do they relate to lifestyle interventions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** The study analyzed literature from the Web of Science database, focusing on genes associated with both diseases obtained from GeneCards and OMIM databases.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** The study conducted bibliometric visualization and bioinformatics analysis to identify research trends and molecular mechanisms.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Bibliometric findings were cross-validated with molecular mechanisms to identify convergent evidence.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** The study found a significant upward trend in publications and citation frequency, with 'insulin resistance' being a key keyword and INS as the primary hub gene among 97 shared targets.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Insulin signaling, chronic inflammation, and nutritional status were identified as common mechanisms linking diabetes and sarcopenia, supporting the potential of lifestyle interventions.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a bibliometric and bioinformatics analysis, which means it does not provide direct clinical evidence but rather identifies research trends and potential areas for future study.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes can understand the importance of lifestyle interventions, particularly nutrition and resistance exercise, in managing their condition. However, they should not overclaim the findings as direct medical advice and should consult with healthcare providers for personalized treatment plans.

## 原文精华摘要

饮食在管理糖尿病前期中扮演着关键角色。 像烤三文鱼搭配藜麦和蔬菜这样的餐食可以帮助调节血糖水平。

最近的研究强调了生活方式改变，尤其是营养和阻力运动，在解决糖尿病和肌肉减少症共病中的重要性。

研究中“胰岛素抵抗”被识别为一个关键词，将其与97个共享靶标中的主枢纽基因INS联系起来，表明饮食和运动对胰岛素敏感性的影响。

然而，重要的是要记住，虽然这些发现很有希望，但它们并不是个人治疗的处方。 在对饮食或运动习惯做出重大改变之前，总是要咨询医疗保健提供者。

文献计量和生物信息学结果的整合强调了胰岛素信号和慢性炎症在糖尿病和肌肉减少症发展中的重要性。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

理解饮食和运动在管理糖尿病前期中的作用对于希望预防发展为2型糖尿病的个体至关重要。 这项研究提供了糖尿病和肌肉减少症之间分子机制的见解，强调了生活方式干预的重要性。

你可以带走的重点

- 饮食和运动是管理糖尿病前期的关键组成部分。
- 烤三文鱼搭配藜麦和蔬菜是有助于管理血糖水平的一餐的例子。
- 胰岛素抵抗是糖尿病和肌肉减少症发展中的一个重要因素。
- 生活方式的改变有可能显著影响胰岛素敏感性和整体健康。

## English Plain-Language Version

Diet plays a crucial role in managing prediabetes. A meal like grilled salmon with quinoa and vegetables can help regulate blood sugar levels.

Recent research underscores the importance of lifestyle changes, especially nutrition and resistance exercise, in addressing the co-occurrence of diabetes mellitus and sarcopenia.

The study identified 'insulin resistance' as a key keyword, linking it to the primary hub gene INS among 97 shared targets, suggesting the impact of diet and exercise on insulin sensitivity.

However, it's important to remember that while these findings are promising, they are not a prescription for individual treatment. Always consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet or exercise routine.

The integration of bibliometric and bioinformatics results highlights the importance of insulin signaling and chronic inflammation in the development of diabetes and sarcopenia.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the role of diet and exercise in managing prediabetes is crucial for individuals looking to prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

This research provides insights into the molecular mechanisms linking diabetes and sarcopenia, emphasizing the importance of lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Diet and exercise are key components in managing prediabetes.
- Grilled salmon with quinoa and vegetables is an example of a meal that can help manage blood sugar levels.
- Insulin resistance is a significant factor in the development of diabetes and sarcopenia.
- Lifestyle modifications have the potential to significantly impact insulin sensitivity and overall health.
