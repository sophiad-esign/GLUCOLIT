## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does olanzapine-induced insulin resistance affect the hypothalamus-liver-skeletal muscle axis in male mice, and what are the potential mechanisms?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Wild-type male mice
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Olanzapine treatment (10 mg/kg, intraperitoneal) for 8 weeks or a single intrahypothalamic injection (15 nmol)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Vagotomized mice, mice lacking JNK in either the hypothalamus or liver, mice overexpressing hepatic FGF21, and PTP1B-deficient mice (PTP1B-KO)
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Systemic insulin resistance, pyruvate intolerance, reduced insulin signaling in liver and skeletal muscle, increased hepatic JNK phosphorylation and IRS1 serine phosphorylation
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Olanzapine treatment induced systemic insulin resistance and reduced insulin signaling in liver and skeletal muscle, which was prevented by deletion of hypothalamic or hepatic JNK1, or vagotomy. Hepatic FGF21 overexpression prevented insulin resistance in skeletal muscle but not in the liver.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and while it provides valuable insights, the applicability to humans may vary. More research is needed to confirm these findings in clinical settings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Understanding the mechanisms by which olanzapine affects insulin sensitivity can help in developing strategies to counteract insulin resistance in patients undergoing treatment with this medication. However, it's important to note that these findings are preliminary and require further investigation before they can be applied to clinical practice.
