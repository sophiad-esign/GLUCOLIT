## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does the macronutrient content of different diets affect cardiometabolic health in individuals with metabolically unhealthy obesity?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with metabolically unhealthy obesity, including those with prediabetes and hepatic steatosis.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Three different diets: very-low-carbohydrate ketogenic, Mediterranean, and very-low-fat plant-forward diets.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of cardiometabolic effects following moderate weight loss (∼10%) induced by each diet.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Insulin sensitivity, intrahepatic triglyceride content, hepatic de novo lipogenesis, glycated hemoglobin, and 24-hour serial plasma glucose and insulin levels.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Weight loss increased muscle insulin sensitivity by ∼50% in all groups, with the very-low-carb group showing a two-to-three-fold greater increase in hepatic insulin sensitivity. The very-low-carb group also showed the most significant decreases in intrahepatic triglyceride content, hepatic de novo lipogenesis, glycated hemoglobin, and 24-hour serial plasma glucose and insulin levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study did not find significant differences in LDL-cholesterol, apolipoprotein B, or 24-hour plasma triglyceride concentrations among the groups. Individual responses to diets can vary, and the study's findings may not be generalizable to all populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes and metabolically unhealthy obesity, a very-low-carb diet may offer greater benefits in improving hepatic insulin sensitivity and other cardiometabolic markers compared to a Mediterranean or very-low-fat diet. However, it's important to consider personal factors and consult with a healthcare provider before making significant dietary changes.

这很重要，因为肝脏在葡萄糖代谢中扮演着关键角色。

虽然所有饮食都导致了肝内甘油三酯含量和其他指标的降低，但极低碳水化合物饮食尤为突出，特别是在降低糖化血红蛋白和24小时连续血浆葡萄糖和胰岛素水平方面。 需要注意的是，各组之间在低密度脂蛋白胆固醇或甘油三酯浓度上没有显著差异。

这项研究表明，对于代谢不健康的肥胖个体来说，极低碳水化合物饮食可能提供更大的代谢健康益处，特别是在肝脏功能方面，与地中海饮食或极低脂肪饮食相比。 然而，至关重要的是记住个体对饮食的反应可能不同，最有效的方法可能取决于个人因素和医疗建议。

## English Plain-Language Version

Choosing a diet can be pivotal for heart and metabolic health. A recent study compared three diets: very-low-carbohydrate ketogenic, Mediterranean, and very-low-fat plant-forward diets.

Weight loss, about 10%, improved muscle insulin sensitivity by around 50% across all diet groups. However, the very-low-carb group saw a much greater increase in liver insulin sensitivity, up to two to three times more than the other diets.

This is significant because the liver plays a crucial role in glucose metabolism.

While all diets led to a decrease in intrahepatic triglyceride content and other markers, the very-low-carb diet stood out, particularly in reducing glycated hemoglobin and 24-hour serial plasma glucose and insulin levels.

It's important to note that no significant differences were found in LDL-cholesterol or triglyceride concentrations among the groups.

This study suggests that for individuals with metabolically unhealthy obesity, a very-low-carb diet might offer greater cardiometabolic benefits, especially in liver function, compared to a Mediterranean or very-low-fat diet.

However, it's crucial to remember that individual responses to diets can vary, and what works best may depend on personal factors and medical advice.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how different diets affect metabolic health is essential for individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, as it can guide them towards making dietary choices that could improve their condition and reduce the risk of

developing type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- A very-low-carb diet may enhance liver insulin sensitivity more than other diets, which is crucial for glucose metabolism.
- Weight loss, regardless of diet type, can improve muscle insulin sensitivity.
- Liver function and glucose metabolism are key areas where diet can have a significant impact.
- It's important to discuss dietary choices with a healthcare provider to find the best fit for individual health needs.
