## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the effects of time-restricted eating (TRE) on glycemic control and variability in adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A subset of 46 participants with prediabetes from the RESET randomized controlled trial.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A 10-hour TRE intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Control group without TRE restrictions.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Continuous glucose monitoring outcomes, fasting glucose, and glycosylated hemoglobin (HbA1c) levels at 6 weeks and 3 months.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** No significant differences in fasting glucose or HbA1c between groups. TRE group showed reduced blood sugar variability and lower nighttime glucose levels after 6 weeks, but not after 3 months.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Small sample size, short study duration, and lack of long-term data.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While TRE may have some short-term effects on glycemic control, its long-term impact on prediabetes management remains uncertain and requires further investigation.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你的一天从限时进食开始，所有餐食必须在10小时内完成。 这种被称为限时进食（TRE）的饮食模式最近在糖尿病前期患者中进行了研究。 研究的目的是观察TRE是否有助于管理血糖水平，这对糖尿病前期患者来说是一个关键因素。

参与者被分为两组：一组遵循10小时TRE饮食方案，另一组作为对照组，没有这样的限制。 研究人员连续监测他们的血糖水平，并在6周和3个月时检查空腹血糖和糖化血红蛋白（HbA1c）水平。

有趣的是，尽管两组之间的空腹血糖或HbA1c没有显著差异，TRE在6周时确实显示出暂时减少血糖水平的变异性，并在夜间血糖水平略有下降，尽管这些效果在3个月时没有持续。 这表明TRE可能提供一些短期益处，但需要更多的研究来了解其长期影响。

对于糖尿病前期患者来说，这项研究暗示了TRE作为管理血糖的工具的潜力。 然而，重要的是要记住，这不是一个放之四海而皆准的解决方案。 与医疗保健提供者讨论任何饮食变化至关重要。 应该将这项研究的发现视为对话的起点，而不是行动的明确指南。

与此同时，如果你正在考虑TRE，思考一下它可能如何适应你的日常生活。 你能否调整你的用餐时间以适应10小时的窗口？ 这将如何影响你的睡眠和活动水平？ 这些问题值得与你的医疗团队讨论，因为你探索管理糖尿病前期的选项。

这项研究的局限性也值得注意。 样本量相对较小，持续时间较短，因此应谨慎解释结果。 缺乏长期数据意味着我们还不能说出TRE可能如何随时间影响糖尿病前期。 像往常一样，用开放的心态和批判的眼光来对待新的饮食策略很重要，同时考虑潜在的好处和不确定性。

## English Plain-Language Version

Consider a daily routine where meals are confined to a 10-hour window. This pattern, known as time-restricted eating (TRE), was examined in a study involving individuals with prediabetes.

The study aimed to assess whether TRE could assist in managing blood sugar levels, a significant concern for those with prediabetes.

Participants were split into two groups: one following a 10-hour TRE regimen and another serving as a control without restrictions.

Continuous glucose monitoring, along with fasting glucose and HbA1c levels, were evaluated at 6 weeks and 3 months. Notably, no significant differences in fasting glucose or HbA1c were observed between the groups.

However, TRE temporarily reduced blood sugar variability and slightly lowered nighttime glucose levels after 6 weeks, effects that did not persist at 3 months.

This suggests TRE might offer short-term benefits, but further research is needed to understand its long-term impact.

For individuals with prediabetes, TRE could be a potential tool for blood sugar management. It's important to discuss dietary changes with a healthcare provider, as what works for one may not work for all.

The study's findings should be a starting point for conversation, not a definitive guide.

If considering TRE, think about how it fits into your daily routine and its potential effects on sleep and activity levels. These are important considerations when discussing options for managing prediabetes with your healthcare team.

The study's limitations include a small sample size and short duration, which means results should be interpreted with caution. The absence of long-term data prevents us from understanding how TRE might affect prediabetes over time.

It's crucial to approach new dietary strategies with both an open mind and critical thinking, weighing potential benefits against uncertainties.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical health condition that, if left unmanaged, can progress to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding and exploring various lifestyle interventions, such as TRE, is essential for individuals with prediabetes to manage their condition and prevent disease progression.

Practical Takeaways

- Time-restricted eating (TRE) may offer short-term benefits in managing blood sugar levels for individuals with prediabetes.
- The study did not find significant differences in fasting glucose or HbA1c between the TRE and control groups.
- TRE showed a temporary reduction in blood sugar variability and a slight decrease in nighttime glucose levels after 6 weeks.
- More extensive and long-term studies are needed to understand the full impact of TRE on prediabetes management.
