## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the physiologic and metabolic differences between insulin-sensitive and insulin-resistant individuals with impaired glucose tolerance?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Patients from the STOP DIABETES trial with impaired glucose tolerance.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Comparison of physiologic and metabolic features between insulin-sensitive (IS) and insulin-resistant (IR) patients with impaired glucose tolerance.
- **对照 / Comparison:** IS-IGT patients compared to IR-IGT patients and IS patients with normal glucose tolerance.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Metabolic health markers, including HDL, BMI, triglycerides, triglyceride/HDL ratio, hs-CRP, blood pressure, disposition index, and early insulinogenic index.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** IS-IGT patients had better metabolic health markers and a higher disposition index but a lower early insulinogenic index compared to IR-IGT patients.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on a specific trial population and may not be generalizable to all individuals with prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes can discuss their insulin sensitivity with healthcare providers to understand their metabolic profile and make informed lifestyle choices.

这意味着他们仍然可以很好地响应胰岛素，这对于管理血糖水平至关重要。 这些人往往有更好的代谢健康指标，比如更高的高密度脂蛋白（好胆固醇）和更低的BMI、甘油三酯和炎症标志物。

他们的处置指数也更高，表明对胰岛素有更好的反应，但他们的早期胰岛素原指数可能比IR组低，这表明他们的胰腺可能最初没有释放足够的胰岛素。 了解这些差异可以帮助定制生活方式干预措施，更好地管理糖尿病前期，防止发展成2型糖尿病。 这不仅仅是关于你吃什么，还关于你的身体如何响应你摄入的食物。

这项研究比较了两组：一组是胰岛素敏感和糖耐量受损（IS-IGT），另一组是胰岛素抵抗和糖耐量受损（IR-IGT）。 与IR-IGT组相比，IS-IGT组的代谢性血脂异常明显较少，高血压较少，胰岛β细胞功能更好。 这表明即使有糖尿病前期，保持良好的胰岛素敏感性也可以有积极的健康状况。

需要注意的是，这不是治疗处方，而是了解可以指导生活方式选择的潜在机制。

在管理糖尿病前期时，关键是找到平衡。 就像你会选择更健康的沙拉调料一样，你可以做出支持你的代谢健康的选择。 这可能包括定期的体育活动、均衡饮食和监测你的血糖水平。 认识到睡眠障碍的迹象并确保你获得高质量的休息也很重要，因为睡眠在代谢健康中扮演着关键角色。

总之，了解你的代谢概况可以帮助你做出关于健康的明智决定。 无论你是胰岛素敏感还是抵抗，都有一些步骤可以帮助你管理你的糖尿病前期，降低发展成2型糖尿病的风险。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a restaurant, and the waiter brings you a menu with two types of salads: one with a dressing that's high in sugar and the other with a healthier, low-sugar dressing.

Choosing the healthier option is like choosing a lifestyle that can help manage your prediabetes. Recent research has shown that not all prediabetes is the same;

some people have normal insulin sensitivity despite having impaired glucose tolerance. This means they can still respond well to insulin, which is crucial for managing blood sugar levels.

These individuals tend to have better metabolic health markers, such as higher HDL (good cholesterol) and lower BMI, triglycerides, and inflammation markers.

They also have a higher disposition index, indicating a better response to insulin, but they may have a lower early insulinogenic index, suggesting their pancreas might not be releasing enough insulin initially.

Understanding these differences can help tailor lifestyle interventions to better manage prediabetes and prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

It's not just about what you eat, but also about how your body responds to the food you consume.

The study compared two groups: one with normal insulin sensitivity and impaired glucose tolerance (IS-IGT), and another with insulin resistance and impaired glucose tolerance (IR-IGT).

The IS-IGT group had significantly less metabolic dyslipidaemia, less hypertension, and better beta cell function compared to the IR-IGT group.

This suggests that even with prediabetes, maintaining good insulin sensitivity can have positive health outcomes.

It's important to note that this is not a prescription for treatment but rather a guide to understanding the underlying mechanisms that can inform lifestyle choices.

When it comes to managing prediabetes, it's about finding a balance. Just as you would choose a healthier dressing for your salad, you can make choices that support your metabolic health.

This might include regular physical activity, a balanced diet, and monitoring your blood sugar levels.

It's also important to recognize the signs of sleep disorders and ensure you're getting quality rest, as sleep plays a crucial role in metabolic health.

In conclusion, understanding your metabolic profile can help you make informed decisions about your health.

Whether you're insulin sensitive or resistant, there are steps you can take to manage your prediabetes and reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the nuances of insulin sensitivity and resistance in prediabetes is crucial for individuals looking to manage their condition and prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes.

This research provides insights into the metabolic differences between insulin-sensitive and insulin-resistant individuals with impaired glucose tolerance, which can inform personalized lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Understanding your insulin sensitivity can help tailor lifestyle changes to manage prediabetes.
- Even with prediabetes, maintaining good insulin sensitivity can lead to better metabolic health markers.
- Lifestyle choices, including diet and physical activity, play a crucial role in managing insulin sensitivity and prediabetes.
- Recognizing the importance of sleep in metabolic health can help prevent the progression of prediabetes to type 2 diabetes.
- Regular monitoring of blood sugar levels is essential for individuals with prediabetes to manage their condition effectively.
