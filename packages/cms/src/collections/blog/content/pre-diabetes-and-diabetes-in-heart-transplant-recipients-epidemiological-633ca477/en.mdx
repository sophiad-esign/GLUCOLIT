## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the prevalence of prediabetes and diabetes in heart transplant recipients, and what are the associated risks?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 250 heart transplant recipients at Dr. Masih Daneshvari Hospital, Tehran, Iran, between 2010 and 2020.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Prediabetes and diabetes diagnosis according to the American Diabetes Association criteria.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of glucose metabolism status and associated health outcomes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** 36.5% had established DM, 29.1% had Pre-DM, and 34.4% had normal glucose metabolism at transplantation.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Pre-transplant metabolic dysfunction was highly prevalent, and 58.4% of diabetic recipients were unaware of their diagnosis. New-onset PTDM occurred in 6.8% of previously normoglycemic patients by 6 months.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is retrospective and from a single center, which may limit generalizability.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Heart transplant recipients with prediabetes should be aware of the high prevalence of undiagnosed diabetes and the importance of pre-transplant screening and post-transplant glucose monitoring.

想象你刚做了心脏移植手术，现在需要管理糖尿病前期。 这就像用新食材学做菜； 你需要调整生活方式以保持健康。 饮食扮演着关键角色，小小的改变可以带来巨大的差异。 例如，将白米饭换成糙米可以帮助调节血糖水平。 控制分量也很关键；

想象你的盘子是一个画布，一半应该用蔬菜填满，四分之一用精益蛋白质，四分之一用全谷物。 运动是另一个重要组成部分，目标是每周至少进行150分钟的中等强度活动。 记住，持续性比强度更重要。 最后，睡眠经常被忽视，但同样重要。 目标是每晚7-9小时，并建立一个例行公事以提高睡眠质量。

这些调整不仅仅是关于管理糖尿病前期； 它们是关于拥抱一个更健康的生活方式，这可以显著提高你的生活质量。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you've just had a heart transplant and are now managing prediabetes. It's like learning to cook with new ingredients; you need to adjust your lifestyle to maintain your health.

Diet plays a crucial role, and small changes can make a big difference. For instance, swapping white rice for brown can help regulate blood sugar levels. Portion control is also key;

think of your plate as a canvas where half should be filled with vegetables, a quarter with lean protein, and a quarter with whole grains. Exercise is another vital component, aiming for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.

Remember, consistency is more important than intensity. Lastly, sleep is often overlooked but equally important. Aim for 7-9 hours per night and establish a routine to improve sleep quality.

These adjustments are not just about managing prediabetes; they're about embracing a healthier lifestyle that can significantly improve your quality of life.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Heart transplant recipients with prediabetes face unique challenges in managing their health.

Understanding how diet, exercise, and sleep can impact glucose levels is crucial for preventing the progression to diabetes and improving overall health.

Practical Takeaways

- Diet changes, like choosing whole grains over refined, can help manage blood sugar.
- Portion control is essential; fill half your plate with vegetables for balanced meals.
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week to support health.
- Prioritize sleep with 7-9 hours per night for overall well-being.
- Consult with healthcare providers to tailor these suggestions to your specific needs.
