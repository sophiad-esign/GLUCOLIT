## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does Nr4a3 deficiency affect glucose intolerance and body weight in mice?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Male and female mice with full body Nr4a3 deletion fed a standard chow diet.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Full body Nr4a3 deletion.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons made between Nr4a3 knock out mice and control mice.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Elevated non-fasting blood glucose, impaired glucose tolerance, increased body weight, and increased adipose depot weight.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Male Nr4a3 knock out mice had increased body weight, elevated blood glucose, and impaired glucose tolerance. They also had larger adipose cells and impaired adipose mitochondrial respiration.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and results may not directly translate to humans. The study did not provide long-term follow-up data or interventions.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While Nr4a3 deficiency in mice leads to weight gain and glucose intolerance, further research is needed to understand its implications in humans, especially for those with prediabetes or insulin resistance.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're trying to lose weight, but your body seems to be fighting against you. This is what happened to male mice without Nr4a3, a protein that helps regulate how our bodies use energy.

These mice, even on a normal diet, gained more weight and had higher blood sugar levels than their counterparts with Nr4a3.

This suggests that Nr4a3 plays a crucial role in how our bodies respond to the food we eat and how we manage our blood sugar.

The study found that without Nr4a3, mice had larger fat cells and trouble with their fat cells' energy production, which could be a reason for their weight gain and glucose intolerance.

It's like a car engine that isn't running efficiently, causing the car to use more fuel and not perform as well. Understanding how Nr4a3 works can help us figure out better ways to manage our diets and blood sugar levels.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the role of Nr4a3 in glucose intolerance and weight management is crucial for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, as it can inform dietary and lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Nr4a3 deficiency can lead to increased body weight and glucose intolerance.
- Proper Nr4a3 function is crucial for adipocyte health and energy metabolism.
- Understanding Nr4a3's role can help in developing dietary and therapeutic strategies for glucose intolerance.
- The study highlights the importance of considering genetic factors in diet and glucose management.
