## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does Aurantio-obtusin have an effect on prediabetes and what are the underlying mechanisms?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Mice subjected to prediabetes by a combination of a high-fat diet and streptozotocin.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Administration of Aurantio-obtusin.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons made between prediabetic mice treated with Aurantio-obtusin and those without.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glucose tolerance, lipid profiles, insulin sensitivity, liver function, oxidative stress, inflammatory responses, insulin signaling, and gut microbiota composition.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Aurantio-obtusin improved glucose tolerance, lipid profiles, and insulin sensitivity. It also protected the liver, reduced oxidative stress, preserved intestinal barrier integrity, reduced endotoxemia, and suppressed inflammatory signaling, with the effects being dependent on gut microbiota.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and the results may not be directly applicable to humans. The study did not include a human clinical trial component.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests potential benefits of Aurantio-obtusin in managing prediabetes, readers should be cautious about applying these findings to human health directly. Further research in humans is necessary to confirm these effects.
