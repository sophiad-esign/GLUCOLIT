## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does Ramadan fasting affect the gut microbiome and metabolic outcomes in prediabetic individuals?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Seventeen adults diagnosed with prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Participation in Ramadan fasting.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Baseline measurements before Ramadan, end of Ramadan, and 3 months post-fasting.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Gut microbiome profiled using 16S rRNA gene sequencing, metabolic and anthropometric measurements.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Ramadan fasting was associated with distinct, time-dependent shifts in the gut microbiome. Alpha diversity increased significantly at the end of Ramadan and partially reverted by 3 months post-fasting. Despite these microbial changes, glycemic and lipid markers remained largely unchanged, while insulin resistance and adiposity indices increased over time.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on a small sample size of 17 individuals, and the findings may not be generalizable to a larger population. The study duration was limited to 3 months post-fasting, and long-term effects are unknown.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While Ramadan fasting can lead to changes in the gut microbiome, these changes do not necessarily result in short-term improvements in metabolic health for individuals with prediabetes. Larger, diet-controlled studies are needed to understand the relationship between gut microbiome changes and metabolic outcomes in the context of prediabetes.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine starting your day without breakfast or lunch, only to eat a large meal in the evening. This is what Ramadan fasting entails, and a recent study looked into how this affects people with prediabetes.

The study involved 17 adults with prediabetes who were monitored before, during, and after Ramadan. The researchers found that fasting led to changes in the gut microbiome, the community of bacteria in the gut.

However, these changes did not lead to improvements in blood sugar or lipid levels. It's like rearranging the furniture in a room but not necessarily making the room cleaner or more organized.

The study suggests that while fasting can alter the gut microbiome, it may not be enough to improve metabolic health in the short term for those with prediabetes.

It's important to remember that this was a small study and more research is needed to understand the long-term effects and how different diets might interact with the gut microbiome.

For now, if you have prediabetes, it's still crucial to focus on a balanced diet and regular exercise to manage your condition.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how dietary patterns like Ramadan fasting affect the gut microbiome and metabolic health is crucial for individuals with prediabetes who are looking for ways to manage their condition.

This study provides insights into the potential effects of fasting on gut health and its limitations in improving metabolic markers.

Practical Takeaways

- Ramadan fasting can lead to changes in the gut microbiome, but these changes do not necessarily translate into short-term metabolic improvements.
- Despite alterations in gut bacteria, blood sugar and lipid levels remained largely unchanged in the study.
- More research is needed to understand how diet and lifestyle changes can effectively manage prediabetes.
- It's important for individuals with prediabetes to continue focusing on a balanced diet and regular exercise.
