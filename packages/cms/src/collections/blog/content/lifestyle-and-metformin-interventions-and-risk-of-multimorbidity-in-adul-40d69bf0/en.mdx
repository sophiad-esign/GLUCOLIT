## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，您被诊断出处于糖尿病前期，这意味着您的血糖水平高于正常，但还没有达到糖尿病的标准。面对这样的诊断，您可能会感到焦虑，担心未来可能会发展成多种慢性疾病。但最新的研究给我们带来了希望，它表明通过改变生活方式，可以有效降低这种风险。

这项研究追踪了1173名糖尿病前期成人长达21年，结果显示，与服用安慰剂的对照组相比，那些接受生活方式干预的人，多重共病的风险更低。这意味着，通过改善饮食习惯、增加体育活动等健康生活方式的改变，可能有助于预防未来的健康问题。

这项研究的重要性在于，它提供了长期的数据支持，显示生活方式的改变对于预防多重共病是有效的。然而，我们也应该注意到，研究的普遍性可能受到参与者的特定人口特征和研究中考虑的特定疾病条件的限制。这意味着，虽然结果令人鼓舞，但在将这些发现应用到更广泛的人群之前，还需要更多的研究来验证。

此外，这项研究并没有提供关于二甲双胍在降低多重共病风险方面的明确证据，这可能对那些考虑药物治疗作为预防措施的人有重要影响。

这项研究强调了公共卫生干预策略中生活方式改变的重要性，特别是在糖尿病前期这一关键时期。对于个人来说，这意味着采取健康的饮食、规律的体育活动和良好的生活习惯可能有助于减少未来健康问题的风险。

对于健康科技行业，这提供了开发和推广支持健康生活方式项目的机会。例如，开发更多工具和应用程序，帮助人们跟踪和改善他们的生活方式，特别是在糖尿病前期这一关键时期。

如果您处于糖尿病前期，考虑采取更健康的生活方式，比如改善饮食习惯和增加体育活动。同时，与医生讨论适合您的预防措施。健康科技行业可以助力推广支持健康生活方式的项目，帮助更多人预防未来的健康问题。

这项研究提醒我们，虽然生活方式的改变可能有助于降低多重共病的风险，但这种关联并不意味着因果关系。在采取任何健康干预措施之前，了解个人的健康状况和风险因素是非常重要的。

总的来说，这项研究为我们提供了一个重要的启示：在糖尿病前期这一关键时期，通过改变生活方式来预防多重共病是可行的，但也需要更多的研究来验证这些发现，并为不同人群提供个性化的建议。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine being diagnosed with prediabetes, a condition where your blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet at the level of diabetes. This diagnosis might leave you feeling anxious about the possibility of developing multiple chronic diseases in the future. Fortunately, a recent study offers hope by suggesting that adopting a healthier lifestyle can effectively reduce this risk.

This long-term study followed 1173 adults with prediabetes for 21 years, revealing that those who received lifestyle interventions had a lower risk of developing multiple chronic conditions compared to those who took a placebo. This indicates that making changes such as improving dietary habits and increasing physical activity could help prevent future health issues.

The significance of this study lies in its long-term data supporting the effectiveness of lifestyle changes in preventing multimorbidity. However, it's important to note that the generalizability of these results may be limited by the demographic composition of the study participants and the specific conditions considered in the study. While the results are encouraging, more research is needed to validate these findings before applying them to a broader population.

Additionally, the study did not provide clear evidence about the role of metformin in reducing the risk of multimorbidity, which could be significant for those considering medication as a preventive measure.

This study emphasizes the importance of lifestyle changes in public health intervention strategies, especially during the critical period of prediabetes. For individuals, this means adopting healthier eating habits, regular physical activity, and good lifestyle practices could help reduce the risk of future health issues.

For the health tech industry, this provides an opportunity to develop and promote programs that support these lifestyle changes. For example, developing more tools and applications to help people track and improve their lifestyles, especially during the critical period of prediabetes.

If you are in the prediabetes stage, consider adopting a healthier lifestyle, such as improving your eating habits and increasing physical activity. Also, discuss suitable preventive measures with your doctor. The health tech industry can help promote programs that support healthy lifestyle changes, assisting more people in preventing future health issues.

This study reminds us that while lifestyle changes may help reduce the risk of multimorbidity, this association does not imply causation. It is important to understand individual health conditions and risk factors before undertaking any health intervention measures.

Overall, this study provides an important insight: during the critical period of prediabetes, preventing multimorbidity through lifestyle changes is feasible, but more research is needed to validate these findings and provide personalized recommendations for different populations.

Practical Takeaways

- Adults with prediabetes can reduce the risk of multimorbidity by changing their lifestyle.
- A 21-year study shows that lifestyle interventions are associated with a lower risk of multimorbidity.
- Metformin did not show significant differences in reducing multimorbidity risk compared to placebo.
- The health tech industry can help promote programs that support healthy lifestyle changes.
