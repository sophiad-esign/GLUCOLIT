## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the associations of lifestyle and metformin interventions compared with placebo on the occurrence of multimorbidity in adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1173 adults with prediabetes enrolled in Medicare and followed up for 21 years after enrolling in a randomized clinical trial.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Participants were randomly assigned to intensive lifestyle intervention, metformin, or placebo.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Lifestyle intervention and metformin compared to placebo.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Multimorbidity (presence of ≥2 of 15 prevalent conditions).
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Lifestyle intervention was associated with a lower risk of multimorbidity compared to placebo, while metformin showed no significant difference.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's demographic composition and the specific conditions considered may limit the generalizability of the results.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For adults with prediabetes, adopting a healthy lifestyle can significantly reduce the risk of developing multiple chronic conditions, suggesting the importance of lifestyle interventions in public health strategies.

对于糖尿病前期患者来说，预防多重慢性疾病的发生对公共健康至关重要。 这项研究提供了关于生活方式改变和二甲双胍在减少多重共病风险方面的有效性见解。

## English Plain-Language Version

### Research Background

Preventing the onset of multiple chronic conditions is crucial for public health, especially for those with prediabetes. This study provides insights into the effectiveness of lifestyle changes and metformin in reducing multimorbidity.

### Core Findings

In a 21-year follow-up of 1173 adults with prediabetes, lifestyle intervention was associated with a lower risk of multimorbidity compared to placebo, while metformin showed no significant difference.

### Your Interpretation and Critique

The long-term follow-up offers valuable insights into the enduring effects of lifestyle changes on health. However, the study's demographic composition and specific conditions considered may limit the generalizability of these results.

### Clinical/Business Insights

Adopting a healthy lifestyle can significantly reduce the risk of developing multiple chronic conditions for those with prediabetes. The health tech industry can play a significant role in developing programs that support lifestyle changes.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant as it provides long-term data on the effects of lifestyle changes and metformin on multimorbidity in adults with prediabetes, offering insights into potential strategies for public health interventions.

### Practical Takeaways

- Lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of multimorbidity in adults with prediabetes.
- Metformin did not show a significant difference in reducing multimorbidity compared to placebo.
- Long-term studies provide valuable insights into the enduring effects of interventions on health.
- The health tech industry can play a significant role in developing programs that support lifestyle changes.
