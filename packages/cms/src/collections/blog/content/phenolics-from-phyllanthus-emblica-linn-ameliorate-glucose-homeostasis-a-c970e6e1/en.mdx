## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do phenolics from Phyllanthus emblica have potential antidiabetic effects and what are the underlying mechanisms?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Insulin-resistant HepG2 cells and a high-fat diet/streptozotocin-induced type 2 diabetes mellitus mouse model.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Treatment with phenolic extracts from Phyllanthus emblica (PEFP and PEBP).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of glucose consumption, glycogen synthesis, and gene expression in treated and untreated cells and mice.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Enhanced glucose consumption, glycogen synthesis, and hexokinase and pyruvate kinase activities in IR-HepG2 cells. Improved glucose homeostasis, insulin sensitivity, dyslipidemia, oxidative stress, and inflammatory responses in T2DM mice.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** PEFP and PEBP significantly improved glucose homeostasis and insulin sensitivity in T2DM mice, with high-dose PEFP showing stronger associations with restoration of insulin signaling and inhibition of gluconeogenesis.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in a mouse model and cell lines, and more research is needed to confirm these effects in humans.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While these findings are promising, readers with prediabetes or insulin resistance should not overclaim the effects of Phyllanthus emblica. It's important to approach dietary changes with caution and under the guidance of a healthcare professional.
