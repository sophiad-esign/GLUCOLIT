## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of Time-Restricted Eating (TRE) on insulin sensitivity, glycemic control, and metabolic outcomes in low- and middle-income countries?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults in low- and middle-income countries with metabolic conditions such as Type 2 Diabetes, Metabolic Syndrome, Obesity, Polycystic Ovary Syndrome, or Metabolic-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Time-Restricted Eating (TRE) with a defined daily eating window of 6 to 10 hours.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Standard care, ad libitum eating, or daily caloric restriction.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Primary outcomes were HbA1c, fasting blood sugar (FBS), and HOMA-IR. Secondary outcomes were body weight, waist-to-hip ratio, visceral fat area (VFA), and AMPK/mTOR expression.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** TRE was associated with reductions in insulin resistance, fasting blood sugar, and body weight across most included studies. A persistent HbA1c reduction of -0.78% at 18 months in one longitudinal cohort suggested that glycemic benefits may compound with sustained adherence.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The review included studies with varying methodologies and populations, and further evidence from larger and longer trials is needed to confirm the findings and evaluate the superiority of TRE over caloric restriction.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, TRE may offer a feasible, low-cost dietary strategy for improving metabolic health. However, the evidence is not yet definitive, and larger studies are needed to fully understand the long-term effects and compare TRE with other dietary approaches.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，醒来后决定午餐前不吃东西。 这个简单的改变可能是管理血糖和体重的强大工具，特别是如果你有患糖尿病的风险。 最近的一项系统综述研究了来自印度和中国等国家的研究报告，那里的许多人负担不起昂贵的饮食，但可以尝试一个简单的改变：每天将所有餐食限制在一个6到10小时的窗口内。

大多数研究表明这有助于降低胰岛素抵抗、空腹血糖和体重。 甚至有一项研究在18个月后看到血糖控制的持续改善。 这不仅仅是关于你吃什么，而是你什么时候吃。 这可能是迈向更好代谢健康的实用步骤，特别是对于那些资源有限的人。

时间限制性饮食，或TRE，不是一个告诉你吃什么的饮食，而是告诉你什么时候吃。 它基于我们身体的自然24小时周期，或昼夜节律。 通过在特定时间窗口内进食，我们可能能够重置身体的内部时钟，这可能会因为深夜进食而变得混乱。 这可以导致更好的血糖控制和更少的胰岛素抵抗。

这就像是给我们的新陈代谢按下重置按钮。

然而，需要注意的是，尽管TRE看起来很有前景，但我们仍然需要更多的、更大规模的研究来确定。 一些研究的结果存在差异，我们还不知道TRE是否比简单地少吃更好。 但就目前而言，TRE提供了一个低成本、简单的策略，可能是管理代谢健康更大计划的一部分。

综述还观察到一些早期迹象，表明TRE可能会改变我们的细胞使用能量的方式，这对于管理糖尿病等疾病是好事。 但请记住，这只是开始，每个人都是不同的。 对一个人有效的方法可能对另一个人无效。 在对你的饮食习惯做出大的改变之前，最好与医疗服务提供者交谈。

总之，TRE可能是迈向更好健康的简单一步，但它不是灵丹妙药。 它是更大图景的一部分，包括定期检查、了解你的身体，并与医疗服务提供者合作管理你的健康。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

对于糖尿病前期或早期代谢风险的个体来说，管理血糖和体重至关重要。 时间限制性饮食提供了一个潜在的解决方案，既低成本又易于实施，使其特别适用于传统饮食干预可能负担不起的低收入和中等收入国家。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up and deciding not to eat until lunchtime. This simple change might be a powerful tool for managing blood sugar and weight, especially if you're at risk of diabetes.

A recent systematic review looked at studies from countries like India and China, where many people can't afford fancy diets but can try a simple change: eating all meals within a 6 to 10-hour window each day.

Most studies showed this helped lower insulin resistance, fasting blood sugar, and body weight. One study even saw lasting improvements in blood sugar control 18 months later. It's not just about what you eat, but when you eat it.

This could be a practical step towards better metabolic health, especially for those with limited resources.

Time-Restricted Eating, or TRE, is not a diet that tells you what to eat, but when to eat it. It's based on our body's natural 24-hour cycle, or circadian rhythm.

By eating during a specific window, we might be able to reset our body's internal clock, which can get messed up by late-night eating. This can lead to better blood sugar control and less insulin resistance.

It's like hitting the reset button on our metabolism.

However, it's important to note that while TRE seems promising, we still need more and larger studies to be sure. Some studies had differences in results, and we don't know yet if TRE is better than simply eating less.

But for now, TRE offers a low-cost, simple strategy that could be a part of a bigger plan to manage metabolic health.

The review also looked at some early signs that TRE might change how our cells use energy, which is a good thing for managing diseases like diabetes. But remember, this is just the start, and everyone is different.

What works for one person might not work for another. It's always best to talk to a healthcare provider before making big changes to your eating habits.

In the end, TRE might be a simple step towards better health, but it's not a magic bullet. It's part of a bigger picture that includes regular check-ups, understanding your body, and working with healthcare providers to manage your health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Managing blood sugar and weight is crucial for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Time-Restricted Eating offers a potential solution that is both low-cost and easy to implement, making it particularly relevant for those in low- and middle-income countries where traditional dietary interventions may be unaffordable.

Practical Takeaways

- Time-Restricted Eating (TRE) is a simple strategy that might help manage blood sugar and weight.
- TRE involves eating all meals within a 6 to 10-hour window each day.
- Studies from countries like India and China show promising results with TRE.
- More research is needed to confirm the benefits of TRE and how it compares to other dietary strategies.
- Always consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet.
