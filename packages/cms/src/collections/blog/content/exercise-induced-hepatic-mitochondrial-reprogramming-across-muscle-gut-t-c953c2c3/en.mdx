## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does exercise influence hepatic mitochondrial function and MASLD/MASH progression?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Exercise as a lifestyle intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Pharmacologic therapies and lifestyle modifications.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvements in hepatic mitochondrial function, insulin sensitivity, and reduction in hepatic steatosis and inflammation.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exercise activates key metabolic regulators promoting mitochondrial biogenesis, fatty acid oxidation, and mitophagy, while suppressing hepatic lipogenesis and oxidative injury.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a review and does not provide new experimental data. The effects of exercise are complex and multifactorial, involving multiple organ systems.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, regular exercise may offer a non-pharmacological approach to improve liver health and metabolic function, but it should be integrated with other lifestyle modifications and medical advice.

## English Plain-Language Version

Feeling sluggish after a long day? It might be more than just fatigue. Prediabetes can悄悄影响你的肝脏健康，but regular exercise can help. Exercise isn’t just about burning calories;

it triggers a complex response in your body that can improve liver function and insulin sensitivity. How? Exercise activates metabolic regulators like AMPK and PGC-1α, promoting healthier mitochondria in the liver.

This means better fat burning, less liver damage, and improved insulin response. But it’s not just about individual workouts; your muscles, gut, and thyroid all work together to support this process.

Exercise might be the key to unlocking a healthier liver and better metabolic health. It’s not a quick fix, but a consistent routine can make a real difference.

Remember, it’s always best to consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a silent health threat that can lead to serious complications if left unaddressed.

Understanding how lifestyle changes, particularly exercise, can impact liver health and insulin sensitivity is crucial for individuals looking to manage their prediabetes and prevent progression to type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Exercise can improve liver function and insulin sensitivity in individuals with prediabetes.
- Regular physical activity triggers metabolic adaptations that support healthier liver mitochondria.
- Muscle, gut, and thyroid all play a role in the exercise-induced mitochondrial adaptations.
- Exercise is not a quick fix but can be a sustainable part of managing prediabetes.
- Consult with a healthcare provider before starting a new exercise regimen.
