## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the effects of pharmacological treatment and lifestyle modification on prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Pharmacological treatment and lifestyle modifications
- **对照 / Comparison:** Control or lower intensity/alternative interventions
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incidence of diabetes, incidence of normoglycemia, weight change, waist circumference, fasting plasma glucose
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Pharmaceutical intervention alongside lifestyle adjustments decreased diabetes incidence and enhanced the incidence of normoglycemia.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Substantial heterogeneity and variability in secondary metabolic outcomes, indicating that the magnitude of benefit may differ across populations and intervention types.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that a combined approach can be beneficial, it's important for individuals with prediabetes to work with healthcare providers to determine the most suitable intervention based on personal factors and the potential variability in outcomes.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant because it provides a systematic review of interventions for prediabetes, a condition that affects a significant portion of the population and can lead to diabetes if not managed properly.

### Practical Takeaways

- Combining medication with lifestyle changes can reduce the risk of developing diabetes.
- Lifestyle modifications are the most effective strategy for reversing prediabetes.
- The effectiveness of interventions may vary based on the population and type of intervention.
- Personalized strategies are needed for managing prediabetes.
