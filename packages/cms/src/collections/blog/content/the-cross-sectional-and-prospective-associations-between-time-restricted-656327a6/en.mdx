## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between time-restricted eating and cardiometabolic health in adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Community-dwelling adults
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Time-restricted eating (TRE; ≤12 hours daily eating window)
- **对照 / Comparison:** No specific comparison group mentioned, as the review includes cross-sectional and cohort studies
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Cardiometabolic markers including abdominal obesity, metabolic syndrome, elevated blood pressure/hypertension, reduced high-density lipoprotein cholesterol, elevated triglycerides, and prediabetes/type 2 diabetes mellitus
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** No significant association found between TRE and the mentioned cardiometabolic markers in cross-sectional studies. Insufficient data for prospective associations.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Inconclusive results due to insufficient data and heterogeneity in the studies. No strong evidence to support TRE as a standalone strategy for cardiometabolic health.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While time-restricted eating may be part of a healthy lifestyle, the evidence does not consistently demonstrate its effectiveness in improving cardiometabolic health. Focus should remain on established prevention strategies including regular physical activity.
