## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the long-term effects of sleeve gastrectomy on cardiometabolic parameters and the resolution of obstructive sleep apnea?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Patients aged 18 to 60 years who underwent sleeve gastrectomy.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Sleeve gastrectomy as a metabolic and bariatric surgical intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of anthropometric, glycemic, blood pressure, lipid profile, and sleep apnea parameters at baseline and 5 years after surgery.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant improvements in body mass index, waist and neck circumferences, blood pressure, fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, HOMA-IR, triglyceride, and HDL. The STOP-BANG questionnaire score and Apnea-Hypopnea Index significantly decreased.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Sleeve gastrectomy led to significant long-term improvements in cardiometabolic health and resolution of obstructive sleep apnea.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study had a relatively small sample size and focused on a specific surgical intervention, limiting generalizability.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk considering surgical options, sleeve gastrectomy may offer long-term benefits in managing weight, improving metabolic health, and resolving sleep apnea.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，每天醒来都精神焕发，不再有睡眠呼吸暂停的困扰，这是肥胖人士常见的问题。 最近的一项研究表明，袖状胃切除术，一种减肥手术，不仅有助于减轻体重，还显著改善睡眠质量。 患者的体重指数和腰围显著减少，血压、血糖和胆固醇水平也有所改善。

最值得注意的是，研究发现睡眠呼吸暂停的缓解非常显著，呼吸暂停低通气指数显著下降。 即使是那些体重有所回升的人，仍然享受到了这些好处。 这表明，通过手术解决体重问题可以长期改善睡眠和代谢健康。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

肥胖和糖尿病前期等相关代谢疾病常伴有睡眠障碍，严重影响生活质量。 了解外科手术对体重和睡眠的影响，为寻求全面健康管理的个体提供了宝贵的见解。

- 即使体重有所回升，对心血管代谢健康和睡眠呼吸暂停的好处仍然存在。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up refreshed every day without the struggle of sleep apnea, a common problem for those with obesity.

A recent study shows that sleeve gastrectomy, a type of weight loss surgery, not only helps with weight but also significantly improves sleep quality.

Patients saw a remarkable reduction in body mass index and waist size, along with improvements in blood pressure, blood sugar, and cholesterol levels.

Most notably, the study found that the resolution of sleep apnea was substantial, with a significant drop in the Apnea-Hypopnea Index. Even those who gained some weight back still enjoyed the benefits.

This suggests that addressing weight through surgery can lead to better sleep and metabolic health in the long run.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Obesity and related metabolic conditions like prediabetes often come with sleep disturbances, significantly affecting quality of life.

Understanding the impact of surgical interventions on both weight and sleep provides valuable insights for individuals seeking comprehensive health management.

Practical Takeaways

- Sleeve gastrectomy can lead to significant long-term improvements in both weight and sleep quality.
- Metabolic parameters such as blood pressure and blood sugar show substantial improvements post-surgery.
- Even with some weight regain, the benefits to cardiometabolic health and sleep apnea persist.
- Discuss with your healthcare provider the potential benefits and considerations of sleeve gastrectomy for managing obesity and related conditions.
