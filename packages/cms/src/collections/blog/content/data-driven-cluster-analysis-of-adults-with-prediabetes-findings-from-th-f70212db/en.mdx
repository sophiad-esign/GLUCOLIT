## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the distinct metabolic subtypes among adults with prediabetes and how do they respond to lifestyle interventions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 295 Japanese adults with impaired glucose tolerance, mean age 50.4 ± 14.6 years.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle interventions in the context of a randomized controlled trial.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of incident type 2 diabetes risk and response to lifestyle interventions among identified subtypes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incident type 2 diabetes defined as a repeat 75-g OGTT.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Three clusters emerged: Metabolically Resilient Prediabetes (MRP), Insulin-Insufficient Prediabetes (IIP), and Severe Insulin-Resistant Prediabetes (SIRP), differing in diabetes risk and intervention responsiveness.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on a specific Japanese population and may not be generalizable to other ethnicities or regions.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Understanding these subtypes can help in developing more targeted interventions to reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes, but the applicability of these findings needs further investigation in diverse populations.
