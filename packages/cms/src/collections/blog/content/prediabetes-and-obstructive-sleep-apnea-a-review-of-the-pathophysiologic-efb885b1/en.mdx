## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the relationship between prediabetes and obstructive sleep apnea, and can treating OSA improve glucose metabolism?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes and obstructive sleep apnea.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Obstructive sleep apnea and its treatment with CPAP.
- **对照 / Comparison:** The bidirectional relationship between prediabetes and OSA.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** There is a bidirectional relationship between prediabetes and OSA, and treating OSA with CPAP may improve glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The evidence is limited, and more research is needed to confirm the effects of CPAP on the progression from prediabetes to type 2 diabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While there is a clear connection between prediabetes and sleep apnea, and treating sleep apnea might help with glucose metabolism, individuals should not consider this a definitive treatment for prediabetes without consulting a healthcare provider.
