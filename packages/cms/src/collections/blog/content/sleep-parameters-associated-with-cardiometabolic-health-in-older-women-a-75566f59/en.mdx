## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do sleep parameters, particularly consistency, affect cardiometabolic health in older women with prediabetes or diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Women aged 50 and above with type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, or at moderate to high risk for diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Measurement of sleep quantity and quality using Fitbit Inspire 2 devices worn for 3-7 days.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of cardiometabolic risk factors between good and poor sleepers.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Homeostasis model assessment of insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), Metabolic Z-score (MetS), Cardiometabolic Risk Score, and Framingham Risk Score.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Sleep duration variability and wake time variability were significantly associated with elevated cardiometabolic risk scores.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study had a relatively small sample size and only included women, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For women with prediabetes, maintaining a consistent sleep schedule may be as important as getting enough sleep for managing cardiometabolic health.

优质睡眠对健康至关重要。 在老年女性中，睡眠一致性可能会影响心血管代谢健康。 一项研究检查了患有糖尿病或糖尿病前期的50岁以上女性的睡眠参数。 研究人员使用Fitbit设备发现，睡眠时间的变异性和清醒时间的变异性与更高的心血管代谢风险评分相关。 这表明，保持一致的睡眠时间表可能对健康有益。

## English Plain-Language Version

Quality sleep is essential for health. In older women, sleep consistency may impact cardiometabolic health. A study examined sleep parameters in women aged 50+ with diabetes or prediabetes.

Using Fitbit devices, researchers found that sleep duration variability and wake time variability were linked to higher cardiometabolic risk scores. This suggests that maintaining a consistent sleep schedule could be beneficial for health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Sleep is a fundamental aspect of health that can significantly influence how our bodies manage conditions like prediabetes.

This study provides valuable insights into how sleep parameters, particularly consistency, can impact cardiometabolic health, which is crucial for women over 50 who are at risk of diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Good sleep hygiene is crucial for managing prediabetes and metabolic health.
- Consistent sleep patterns may be as important as sleep duration for cardiometabolic health.
- Variability in sleep duration and wake times is associated with higher cardiometabolic risk scores.
- Setting a regular sleep schedule and aiming for 7-9 hours of sleep each night could improve health outcomes.
- Discussing sleep patterns with a healthcare provider can be beneficial for personalized health management.
