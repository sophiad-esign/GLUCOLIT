## English Plain-Language Version

Prediabetes, a condition characterized by impaired glucose regulation, is gaining attention for its broader health implications. In orthognathic surgery, it may affect bone healing.

Emerging data suggest that prediabetes could disrupt bone homeostasis, potentially delaying post-surgical bone healing. However, the evidence is limited and requires further research.

Managing prediabetes is crucial for improving bone health and surgical outcomes. A balanced approach to diet, exercise, and lifestyle can help manage prediabetes and support bone health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the impact of prediabetes on bone healing is crucial for individuals considering orthognathic surgery, as it can influence surgical planning and postoperative care.
