## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can a community-implemented diabetes prevention program improve metabolic outcomes in individuals at risk of developing type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults at risk of developing type 2 diabetes with HbA1c levels between 39-46 mmol/mol (5.7-6.4%).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Participation in the Small Steps for Big Changes (SSBC) program, which consists of 6 one-hour sessions involving behavioral counseling and exercise.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of metabolic outcomes before and 12 months after the program.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Primary outcome was HbA1c at 12-month post-program; secondary outcomes included weight, waist circumference, functional exercise capacity, diet, and physical activity behavior.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Significant reductions in HbA1c, weight, and waist circumference, and improvements in functional exercise capacity were observed at 12 months post-program.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a single-arm trial without a control group, and not all participants completed the 12-month assessments.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the results suggest that community-based programs like SSBC can lead to improvements in key diabetes risk factors, individual results may vary, and it's important to discuss such programs with healthcare providers for personalized advice.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你刚刚被诊断出糖尿病前期，正在寻找管理健康的方法。 最近的一项研究提供了希望：完成一个为期4周的社区计划的参与者在一年后显示出他们的糖化血红蛋白水平、体重和腰围有显著改善。 这个项目被称为“小步骤大变化”（SSBC），涉及行为咨询和运动，由非医疗人员在社区环境中提供。

这不仅仅是去健身房； 而是在社区支持下做出可持续的生活方式改变。 研究表明，这样的计划可以有效改善关键的糖尿病风险因素。 需要注意的是，虽然结果充满希望，但并不能保证每个个体都会看到同样的好处。 计划的成功可能会因个人因素和对推荐生活方式改变的坚持程度而异。

如果你正在考虑类似的计划，与你的医疗服务提供者讨论是个好主意，以了解什么可能最适合你。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

运动是管理糖尿病前期和预防2型糖尿病发病的关键组成部分。 这项研究强调了社区支持如何增强运动干预的有效性，使其对有风险的个体更加易于接受和持续。

你可以带走的重点

- 社区基础项目可以支持糖尿病前期的管理生活方式改变。
- 在项目结束后一年观察到糖化血红蛋白、体重和腰围的持续改善。
- 运动和行为咨询是项目的关键组成部分。
- 与医疗服务提供者讨论这些项目以获得个性化建议至关重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you've just been diagnosed with prediabetes, and you're looking for ways to manage your health.

A recent study offers hope: participants who completed a 4-week community program showed significant improvements in their HbA1c levels, weight, and waist circumference a year later.

This program, called Small Steps for Big Changes (SSBC), involved behavioral counseling and exercise, and it was delivered by non-medical personnel in community settings. It's not just about going to the gym;

it's about making sustainable lifestyle changes with support from your community. The study suggests that such programs can be effective in improving key diabetes risk factors.

It's important to note that while the results are promising, they don't guarantee that every individual will see the same benefits. The program's success may vary based on personal factors and adherence to the lifestyle changes recommended.

If you're considering a similar program, it's a good idea to discuss it with your healthcare provider to understand what might work best for you.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise is a critical component of managing prediabetes and preventing the onset of type 2 diabetes.

This study highlights how community support can enhance the effectiveness of exercise interventions, making them more accessible and sustainable for individuals at risk.

Practical Takeaways

- Community-based programs can support lifestyle changes for prediabetes management.
- Sustained improvements in HbA1c, weight, and waist circumference were observed a year after the program.
- Exercise and behavioral counseling are key components of the program.
- It's crucial to discuss such programs with healthcare providers for personalized advice.
