## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the distinct metabolic subtypes among Japanese men with prediabetes and their association with the future risk of type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 2,172 Japanese men with prediabetes, defined as a fasting plasma glucose concentration of 100-125 mg/dL (5.6-6.9 mmol/L) from the JDOIT-1 cohort.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Data-driven cluster k-means cluster analysis using four biochemical indicators: BMI, fasting plasma glucose, alanine aminotransferase, and non-HDL cholesterol.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of 5-year diabetes-free survival between three identified metabolic phenotypes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Three metabolic phenotypes identified: metabolically resilient, obesity- and insulin-resistant, and non-obese hyperglycemic. Annual diabetes incidence rates and adjusted hazard ratios for developing diabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Three distinct metabolic subtypes were identified with varying annual diabetes incidence rates: 0.70%, 2.73%, and 6.73% in the metabolically resilient, obesity- and insulin-resistant, and non-obese hyperglycemic groups, respectively. Lifestyle interventions significantly reduced diabetes risk only in the obesity- and insulin-resistant group.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in a Japanese male cohort, which may limit the generalizability of the findings to other populations. The study's design does not establish causation but rather identifies associations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes should be aware of the potential existence of different metabolic subtypes that may require personalized lifestyle interventions. Understanding one's metabolic subtype can help in developing a more effective diabetes prevention plan in collaboration with healthcare providers.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你在一个满是糖尿病前期患者的房间里。 不是每个人都处于发展成2型糖尿病的相同风险中。 日本最近的一项研究在糖尿病前期的男性群体中识别出三种不同的代谢亚型，每种亚型发展成糖尿病的风险各不相同。 了解这些亚型可以帮助我们更有针对性地制定生活方式干预措施，更有效地降低糖尿病风险。

该研究分析了2,172名日本男性糖尿病前期患者，重点关注四个生化指标：BMI、空腹血糖、丙氨酸氨基转移酶和非HDL胆固醇。 他们发现了三个群体：代谢弹性型、肥胖和胰岛素抵抗型以及非肥胖高血糖型。 这些群体中年度糖尿病发病率存在显著差异，非肥胖高血糖型群体中最高。

重要的是，研究发现生活方式干预措施只在肥胖和胰岛素抵抗型群体中显著降低了糖尿病风险。

这意味着并非所有糖尿病前期干预措施都适用所有人。 对于那些与肥胖和血脂异常相关的胰岛素抵抗个体，有针对性的生活方式改变可能特别有益。 与医疗保健提供者合作，了解自己的代谢亚型并制定个性化计划至关重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're in a room full of people with prediabetes. Not everyone is at the same risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

A recent study from Japan identified three distinct metabolic subtypes among men with prediabetes, each with varying risks of progressing to diabetes.

Understanding these subtypes can help tailor lifestyle interventions to reduce the risk of diabetes more effectively.

The study analyzed 2,172 Japanese men with prediabetes, focusing on four biochemical indicators: BMI, fasting plasma glucose, alanine aminotransferase, and non-HDL cholesterol.

They found three clusters: metabolically resilient, obesity- and insulin-resistant, and non-obese hyperglycemic.

The annual diabetes incidence rates were significantly different across these clusters, with the highest in the non-obese hyperglycemic group.

Importantly, lifestyle interventions were found to significantly reduce diabetes risk only in the obesity- and insulin-resistant group.

This means that not all prediabetes interventions are one-size-fits-all. For those with obesity- and dyslipidemia-related insulin resistance, targeted lifestyle changes could be particularly beneficial.

It's crucial to work with healthcare providers to understand one's metabolic subtype and develop a personalized plan.

GLUCOLIT insight: As we move towards more personalized medicine, understanding metabolic subtypes can help in designing targeted interventions. This could lead to more effective diabetes prevention strategies and better health outcomes.

Takeaways: - Recognize that prediabetes subtypes exist with different risks of diabetes. - Understand the importance of personalized lifestyle interventions. - Work with healthcare providers to identify your metabolic subtype.

- Targeted lifestyle changes can significantly reduce diabetes risk in specific subtypes. - Embrace a more personalized approach to diabetes prevention.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the distinct metabolic subtypes among individuals with prediabetes is crucial for developing personalized lifestyle interventions that can effectively reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.

This approach can lead to more targeted and successful diabetes prevention strategies.

Practical Takeaways

- Recognize the existence of different prediabetes subtypes with varying risks of developing diabetes.
- Understand the importance of personalized lifestyle interventions tailored to individual metabolic subtypes.
- Collaborate with healthcare providers to identify your specific metabolic subtype.
- Targeted lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of diabetes in certain subtypes.
- Adopt a more personalized approach to diabetes prevention for better health outcomes.
