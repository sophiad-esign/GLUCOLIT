## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do physical activity and dietary live microbe intake have independent and combined effects on the risk of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), and does the triglyceride-glucose (TyG) index mediate these effects?
- **研究对象 / Population:** The study included 10,387 participants from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey 2007-2018.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Physical activity was assessed using the self-reported Global Physical Activity Questionnaire, and dietary live microbe intake was assessed through 24-hour dietary recall data.
- **对照 / Comparison:** The study compared individuals with high physical activity and high dietary live microbe intake to those with lower levels.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** The primary outcome was the risk of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), defined by the US fatty liver index.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Multivariate-adjusted odds ratios (ORs) were 0.84 (95% CI = 0.72-0.99; P = 0.040) for NAFLD patients with sufficiently high PA and 0.78 (95% CI = 0.66-0.92; P = 0.003) for those with relatively high dietary live microbe intake. The TyG index mediated 40.80% of the association between PA and NAFLD, and 28.44% of the association between dietary live microbe intake and NAFLD.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's limitations include its observational nature, which limits the ability to establish causation, and the reliance on self-reported data for physical activity and dietary intake, which may introduce bias.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that both physical activity and dietary live microbe intake are associated with a lower risk of NAFLD, and that the TyG index plays a mediating role, readers should be cautious about overclaiming causation. It's important to consider these findings as part of a broader approach to managing metabolic health, rather than as a definitive prescription for NAFLD prevention.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你在健康旅程的十字路口，通向更好代谢健康的道路是由体力活动和富含活菌的饮食共同铺就的。 最近的一项研究揭示了这两个生活方式因素如何显著降低你发展非酒精性脂肪肝（NAFLD）的风险，特别是如果你还不到65岁。

该研究分析了国家健康和营养调查的数据，使用自我报告的体力活动水平和饮食回忆来评估对NAFLD风险的影响。 研究发现，那些体力活动高和饮食中富含活菌的人风险最低。 甘油三酯-葡萄糖指数，一个胰岛素抵抗的标志，被发现可以部分介导这种保护效应。

这表明，将定期运动和富含活菌的饮食（如发酵食品）结合起来，可能是预防NAFLD的强大策略。 然而，重要的是要记住，虽然这些关联是显著的，但它们并不能证明因果关系，个体对饮食和运动的反应可能会有所不同。 任何重大的生活方式改变最好与医疗保健提供者讨论。

最终，关键的要点是保持活跃，考虑在你的饮食中添加更多的活菌，并与专业人士讨论以针对你的个人健康需求定制这些策略。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

非酒精性脂肪肝（NAFLD）是一个日益关注的问题，尤其是在糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗的个体中。 了解饮食和运动等生活方式因素如何影响NAFLD风险对于制定有效的预防策略至关重要。

这项研究提供了有关结合体力活动和富含活菌饮食的潜在益处的有价值见解，这可能有助于个体管理他们的代谢健康并降低他们患NAFLD的风险。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads in your health journey, where the path to better metabolic health is paved with both physical activity and a diet rich in live microbes.

A recent study sheds light on how these two lifestyle factors can significantly reduce your risk of developing non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), especially if you're under 65.

The study analyzed data from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey, using self-reported physical activity levels and dietary recall to assess the effects on NAFLD risk.

It found that those with high physical activity and a diet rich in live microbes had the lowest risk. The triglyceride-glucose index, a marker of insulin resistance, was found to mediate a part of this protective effect.

This suggests that incorporating regular exercise and a diet with live microbes, such as fermented foods, could be a powerful strategy for preventing NAFLD.

However, it's important to remember that while these associations are significant, they don't prove causation, and individual responses to diet and exercise can vary.

It's always best to discuss any major lifestyle changes with a healthcare provider.

In the end, the key takeaways are to stay active, consider adding more live microbes to your diet, and consult with a professional to tailor these strategies to your personal health needs.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) is a growing concern, especially among individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance.

Understanding how lifestyle factors like diet and exercise can influence NAFLD risk is crucial for developing effective prevention strategies.

This study provides valuable insights into the potential benefits of combining physical activity with a diet rich in live microbes, which could help individuals manage their metabolic health and reduce their risk of NAFLD.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular physical activity can significantly reduce the risk of NAFLD.
- A diet rich in live microbes, such as fermented foods, may also play a role in NAFLD prevention.
- The combination of high physical activity and a diet rich in live microbes offers a synergistic approach to reducing NAFLD risk.
- The triglyceride-glucose index, a marker of insulin resistance, mediates part of the protective effect of physical activity and live microbe intake on NAFLD.
- It's important to consult with a healthcare provider before making significant lifestyle changes.
