## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does gut microbiota mediate the impact of periodontitis on glucose homeostasis?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Germ-free mice colonized with microbiota from donor mice with ligature-induced periodontitis or healthy controls.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Colonization with microbiota from mice with periodontitis versus healthy controls.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of fasting blood glucose, serum insulin, HOMA-IR, HOMA-β, and glucose tolerance between the two groups.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Higher fasting blood glucose, serum insulin, HOMA-IR, HOMA-β, and impaired glucose tolerance in mice with periodontitis-derived microbiota.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Mice with periodontitis-derived microbiota showed significant disruptions in glucose homeostasis, and supplementation with SCFA-producing bacteria reduced blood glucose levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and the findings may not directly translate to humans. The sample size was limited, and more research is needed to establish causation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study in mice suggests a link between periodontitis, gut microbiota, and glucose homeostasis, it highlights the potential importance of dietary fiber and oral health in managing blood sugar levels. However, it's important to discuss any lifestyle changes with a healthcare provider rather than making assumptions about causation.

## English Plain-Language Version

Dinner time might hold clues to managing blood sugar levels. Research indicates that gut microbiota, influenced by periodontitis, can disrupt glucose balance.

This could be due to a decrease in short-chain fatty acids and increased inflammation.

In the study, germ-free mice were colonized with microbiota from mice with periodontitis, showing higher fasting blood glucose and insulin resistance. Certain bacteria that produce SCFAs were found to reduce blood glucose levels.

Though the study doesn't prove causation and was only in mice, it hints at the importance of a fiber-rich diet for SCFA production and the role of good oral hygiene in血糖管理.
