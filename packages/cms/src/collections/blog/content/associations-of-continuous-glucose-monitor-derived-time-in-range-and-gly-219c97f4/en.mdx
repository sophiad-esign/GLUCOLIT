## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the relationship between diet, lifestyle factors, and glucose control in individuals without diabetes or prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 3,634 individuals without diabetes or prediabetes, aged 46 ± 12 years, 83% female, BMI 27 ± 6 kg/m².
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Continuous glucose monitor (CGM) data analysis.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of time in range (TIR) and glycemic variability with demographic, diet, lifestyle, cardiometabolic markers, and predicted cardiovascular risk.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Higher TIR 3.9-5.6 associated with lower HbA1c, OGTT glucose, carbohydrate intake, and higher protein intake. Sleep duration inversely correlated with mean glucose.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Higher TIR is linked to better dietary habits and lower HbA1c levels. Sleep duration affects glucose levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is observational and does not establish causation. Longer-term health outcomes are needed to confirm the utility of CGM monitoring.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study shows associations, it does not provide a direct prescription for diet or lifestyle changes. It suggests areas for further exploration and discussion with healthcare providers.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，醒来后你的血糖水平像你的早晨例行公事一样稳定。 最近的研究揭示了饮食和生活方式如何影响血糖控制，即使是在没有糖尿病的个体中也是如此。

在涉及3600多名个体的研究中，较高的时间在范围内（TIR），一个血糖控制的指标，与较低的糖化血红蛋白水平和更好的饮食习惯相关，例如较低的碳水化合物摄入和较高的蛋白质摄入。 有趣的是，睡眠时间与平均血糖水平呈负相关，这表明获得足够的休息可能和你的饮食习惯一样重要。

这些发现强调了CGMs提供对血糖生理学见解的潜力，以及我们日常行为对健康的潜在影响。 虽然研究没有规定饮食或生活方式计划，但它确实突出了糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗个体可能考虑进行改变以改善血糖控制的领域。

重要的是要注意，虽然关联是明确的，但因果关系尚未得到证明，对一个人有效的方法可能对另一个人无效。 在对饮食或生活方式进行重大改变之前，请务必咨询医疗保健提供者。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

了解饮食和生活方式如何影响血糖控制对于糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗个体至关重要，因为这可以帮助他们就自己的健康做出明智的决策。

- 血糖控制较好的个体蛋白质摄入量更高，碳水化合物摄入量更低。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up to a day where your glucose levels are as stable as your morning routine. Recent research sheds light on how diet and lifestyle can influence glucose control, even in those without diabetes.

In a study involving over 3,600 individuals, higher time in range (TIR), a measure of glucose control, was linked to lower HbA1c levels and better dietary habits, such as lower carbohydrate intake and higher protein intake.

Interestingly, sleep duration was found to have an inverse relationship with mean glucose levels, suggesting that getting enough rest might be as important as what you eat.

These findings underscore the potential of CGMs to offer insights into glucose physiology and the impact of everyday behaviors on our health.

While the study doesn't prescribe a diet or lifestyle plan, it does highlight areas where individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance might consider making changes to improve their glucose control.

It's important to note that while associations are clear, causation isn't proven, and what works for one person might not work for another.

Always consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet or lifestyle.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how diet and lifestyle impact glucose control is crucial for individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, as it can help them make informed decisions about their health.

Practical Takeaways

- Higher TIR is associated with lower HbA1c and better dietary habits.
- Protein intake is higher, and carbohydrate intake is lower in individuals with better glucose control.
- Sleep duration inversely correlates with mean glucose levels.
- CGMs offer potential insights into glucose physiology and the impact of lifestyle on health.
