## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does exercise impact islet inflammation and beta-cell dysfunction in prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Male C57BL/6J mice fed a high-fat diet to induce obesity-associated type 2 diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Treadmill exercise intervention while maintaining a high-fat diet.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Sedentary high-fat diet group versus exercise intervention group.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glucose tolerance, insulin sensitivity, beta-cell function, islet inflammation, and circulating SPARC protein levels.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exercise improved glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity, mitigated beta-cell dysfunction, reduced islet inflammation, and was associated with lower circulating SPARC levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in a murine model and may not fully replicate human metabolic responses. The findings are associative and require further clinical research to establish causation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study in mice suggests that exercise can have protective effects on beta-cell function and reduce inflammation, it is important to approach these findings with caution and discuss any exercise plans with a healthcare provider.
