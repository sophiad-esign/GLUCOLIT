## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the relationship between free-living physical activity and glucose tolerance in non-underweight, non-overweight young adult women without regular exercise habits?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 28 healthy young adult women (22 ± 2 years; body mass index, 20.9 ± 2.3 kg/m2) without regular exercise habits.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of daily energy expenditure, PA intensity distribution, sedentary behavior, and daily step count using doubly labelled water and an accelerometer.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Correlation analysis between muscle insulin sensitivity index (MISI), hepatic insulin resistance index (HIRI), and whole-body insulin sensitivity with physical activity indices.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** MISI demonstrated significant negative correlation with %SB and positive correlation with step counts. HIRI showed no association with PA indices.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Lower time spent in sedentary behavior and a greater number of daily steps were associated with higher muscle insulin sensitivity.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was cross-sectional and involved a specific group of women, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For young women without regular exercise habits, increasing daily steps and reducing sedentary time may improve muscle insulin sensitivity, which is important for managing blood sugar levels.

## 原文精华摘要

保持胰岛素敏感性对管理血糖水平至关重要。 最近的一项研究揭示了日常步数如何发挥作用。 该研究涉及28位没有规律运动习惯的健康年轻女性。 研究发现，步数越多的女性肌肉胰岛素敏感性越好。 这表明，增加日常活动量可能有益于健康，即使没有正式的运动习惯。 减少久坐时间也与更高的胰岛素敏感性相关。

将更多运动融入日常例程，如走楼梯或休息时散步，可以产生影响。 需要注意的是，该研究针对特定群体，因此结果可能不具有普遍适用性。 然而，对于年轻女性，尤其是那些不经常锻炼的人，这些发现为提高胰岛素敏感性提供了实用建议。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

## English Plain-Language Version

Maintaining insulin sensitivity is key for managing blood sugar levels. A recent study sheds light on how daily steps can play a role. It involved 28 healthy young women who didn't exercise regularly.

The study found that those who took more steps had better muscle insulin sensitivity. This suggests that increasing daily activity could benefit health, even without formal exercise.

Spending less time sedentary also关联 with higher insulin sensitivity. Incorporating more movement into daily routines, like taking the stairs or walking during breaks, can make a difference.

It's important to note that the study focused on a specific group, so results may not apply broadly.

However, for young women, especially those who don't exercise regularly, these findings offer practical advice for improving insulin sensitivity.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Insulin sensitivity is crucial for managing blood sugar levels, which is a key concern for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Understanding how daily physical activity, such as step count, can influence insulin sensitivity provides practical insights for lifestyle modifications that can help prevent the progression to diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Daily steps and muscle insulin sensitivity are linked in young women.
- Reducing sedentary time can improve insulin sensitivity.
- Not all types of physical activity may have the same effect on insulin sensitivity.
- Simple changes in daily activity can have a significant impact on health.
