## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does a high-fat diet affect insulin sensitivity and sleep in women with normal BMI?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 18 normally cycling women aged 18-38 with BMI 18-24 kg/m2
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** One-month eucaloric high-fat diet (48% calories from fat)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Pre- and post-diet measurements
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Insulin sensitivity, body composition, lipidome, microbiome, and sleep
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Decreased whole-body insulin sensitivity, decreased body fat, increased microbial diversity, stable sleep patterns except for WASO
- **局限性 / Limits:** Sample size was small and specific to a certain demographic; results may not be generalizable
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While a high-fat diet may affect insulin sensitivity and body composition, its impact on sleep appears limited. However, individual responses may vary, and further research is needed to understand these effects fully.

研究发现，即使没有体重增加，高脂饮食也会导致全身胰岛素敏感性降低，这可能表明胰岛素抵抗的潜力。

有趣的是，尽管饮食中脂肪高，但体脂减少，微生物多样性增加，这表明饮食有特定的影响。

## English Plain-Language Version

Diet plays a pivotal role in managing blood sugar levels. A recent study provides insights into how a high-fat diet might affect insulin sensitivity and sleep.

Researchers found that a high-fat diet can lead to decreased whole-body insulin sensitivity, even without weight gain, indicating a potential for insulin resistance.

Interestingly, despite the high-fat diet, body fat decreased, and microbial diversity increased, suggesting specific dietary impacts.

While metabolic health was affected, sleep patterns remained stable, except for waking after sleep onset, indicating limited sleep disruption.

Understanding these findings is crucial for managing prediabetes and early metabolic risks, but individual responses may vary, and further research is needed.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the impact of diet on insulin sensitivity and sleep is crucial for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, as it can guide lifestyle modifications to manage these conditions.

Practical Takeaways

- High-fat diets can impact insulin sensitivity without weight gain.
- The study noted a decrease in whole-body insulin sensitivity.
- Unexpectedly, body fat decreased and microbial diversity increased.
- Sleep patterns remained stable, except for waking after sleep onset.
- Discuss dietary changes with healthcare providers for personalized advice.
