## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up one day and realizing your body's ability to process sugar is not as efficient as it used to be. This is the reality for those with prediabetes, a condition that often leads to type 2 diabetes if not managed properly.

Exercise is a powerful tool in this management, as it can help the body use insulin more effectively and improve glucose metabolism.

According to a recent review, glucose metabolic reprogramming is a key factor in the development of type 2 diabetes.

This shift from oxidative phosphorylation to aerobic glycolysis in cells can be mitigated by early interventions, such as regular physical activity.

Exercise can help restore balance in mitochondrial energy metabolism, reduce oxidative stress, and improve calcium homeostasis—all of which are disrupted in prediabetes. It's not just about going to the gym;

even simple activities like walking can make a difference. The key is to find an exercise routine that fits into your daily life and is sustainable in the long term.

While exercise is beneficial, it's important to remember that it's part of a broader management plan that may also include diet and medication.

Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have prediabetes or other health concerns.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise is a crucial component of lifestyle changes that can help individuals with prediabetes prevent or delay the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding the mechanisms by which exercise can be beneficial is key to making informed decisions about one's health.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular physical activity can help manage prediabetes and prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes.
- Exercise can improve insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism, which are disrupted in prediabetes.
- It's important to find an exercise routine that fits into your daily life and is sustainable over time.
- Consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have prediabetes.
