## 原文精华摘要

为什么值得关注

糖尿病前期是一个影响许多成年人的健康问题，尤其在某些种族/族裔群体和低社会经济地位人群中更为普遍。对于这些高风险人群，及时获得有效的预防措施至关重要。国家糖尿病预防项目（DPP）旨在帮助预防或延缓2型糖尿病的发病，但其效果很大程度上取决于项目的可达性。

证据告诉我们什么

研究发现，84.3%的纽约州糖尿病前期居民居住在DPP场所30分钟车程内，显示了广泛的可达性。然而，这也突出了不可用区域的存在，特别是在一些糖尿病前期高发的县。

应该怎样理解

这项研究强调了地理可达性对DPP的重要性，但并未涉及这些项目的质量或效果。它还指出了基于种族和民族的可达性差异，表明需要有针对性的扩展努力。例如，黑人、亚裔和西班牙裔居民的可达性大于白人居民，这可能与社会经济地位和居住区域有关。此外，研究并未考虑个体参与DPP的障碍，如时间、费用和文化差异。因此，尽管地理可达性是一个重要因素，但提高DPP的整体参与度还需要综合考虑其他因素。

可以怎么做

研究结果表明，尽管大多数糖尿病前期居民可以获得DPP，但在一些地区，特别是在糖尿病前期高发的县，需要增加项目可用性。这为健康科技公司与社区合作以提高可访问性提供了机会。例如，通过开发移动健康应用或在线平台，可以为那些居住在偏远地区或有交通障碍的居民提供更便捷的DPP服务。同时，针对不同种族和民族群体的文化敏感性培训，也有助于提高DPP的参与度和效果。

你可以带走的重点

- 纽约州大多数糖尿病前期居民可以获得DPP。
- 仍有一些地区对DPP的可达性有限，特别是在糖尿病前期高发的县。
- DPP的可达性因种族和民族而异，黑人、亚裔和西班牙裔居民的可达性大于白人居民。
- 需要进一步扩展DPP场所，以增加可达性。

## English Plain-Language Version

Prediabetes is a health condition that affects many adults, particularly among certain racial/ethnic groups and those with lower socioeconomic status. Timely access to effective prevention measures is crucial for these high-risk populations. The National Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP) aims to help prevent or delay the onset of type 2 diabetes, but its effectiveness largely depends on the accessibility of the program.

The study reveals that 84.3% of residents with prediabetes in New York State live within a 30-minute drive of a DPP site, indicating widespread access. However, this also highlights areas of unavailability, especially in counties with high prediabetes rates.

The study underscores the importance of geographical access to DPP, yet it does not address the quality or effectiveness of these programs. It also points to disparities in access based on race and ethnicity, suggesting a need for targeted expansion efforts. For instance, greater access among Black, Asian, and Hispanic residents compared to White residents may be related to socioeconomic status and residential areas. Moreover, the study does not consider individual barriers to participation in DPP, such as time, cost, and cultural differences. Therefore, while geographical accessibility is an important factor, improving overall participation in DPP also requires a comprehensive consideration of other factors.

The study's findings suggest that while most residents with prediabetes have access to DPP, there are areas, particularly in high prediabetes prevalence counties, that require increased program availability. This presents an opportunity for health tech companies to partner with communities to enhance accessibility. For example, by developing mobile health apps or online platforms, more convenient DPP services can be provided for residents living in remote areas or with transportation barriers. At the same time, cultural sensitivity training targeted at different racial and ethnic groups can also help increase participation and effectiveness of DPP.

Practical Takeaways

- Most residents with prediabetes in New York State have access to the DPP.
- There are still areas with limited access to DPP, particularly in counties with high prediabetes rates.
- Access to DPP varies by race and ethnicity, with greater access among Black, Asian, and Hispanic residents compared to White residents.
- Further expansion of DPP sites is needed to increase accessibility.
