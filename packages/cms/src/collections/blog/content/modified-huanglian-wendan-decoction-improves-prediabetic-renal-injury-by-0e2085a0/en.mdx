## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can Modified Huanglian Wendan Decoction (MHWD) improve prediabetic renal injury and metabolic disorders?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Prediabetic rat model induced by a high-fat diet and streptozotocin
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Treatment with Modified Huanglian Wendan Decoction (MHWD)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison with metformin, an AMPK activator, and the effects of chloroquine and Compound C on MHWD's effects
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Metabolic abnormalities, renal injury, autophagy restoration, and inflammation suppression
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** MHWD alleviated hyperglycemia, insulin resistance, dyslipidemia, and weight gain in prediabetic rats, while also reducing renal injury and inflammation. MHWD restored autophagy and suppressed inflammation via the AMPK/ULK1 pathway.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in an animal model, and further research is needed to confirm these effects in humans.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that MHWD may have beneficial effects on prediabetes and renal injury, it is important to approach these findings with caution as they are based on an animal model. More research is necessary to understand the applicability of these results to human health.
