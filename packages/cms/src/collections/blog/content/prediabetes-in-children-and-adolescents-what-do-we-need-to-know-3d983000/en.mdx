## 原文精华摘要

想象一个青少年在课堂上努力保持清醒。 这不仅仅是因为缺乏兴趣； 睡眠不足可能会影响他们的健康，特别是如果他们有糖尿病前期。 睡眠障碍与胰岛素抵抗有关，这是糖尿病前期的一个关键因素。 就像一个运转良好的机器一样，一个休息良好的身体可以更好地管理血糖水平。

研究表明，虽然青春期可能会暂时降低胰岛素敏感性，但大多数青少年在这一时期之后会有所改善。 然而，这也意味着识别和管理青少年的糖尿病前期需要一个谨慎的方法，与成人不同。 生活方式的改变，包括更好的睡眠习惯，对于管理儿童和青少年的糖尿病前期至关重要。 这不仅仅是关于获得更多的睡眠；

而是关于睡眠的质量和一致性。 实际步骤可以包括设定规律的睡眠时间表，创造一个宁静的环境，以及避免睡前的刺激活动。 这些变化可以与医疗保健提供者讨论，以制定个性化的计划。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

睡眠是健康的基本方面，在讨论糖尿病前期管理时经常被忽视。 本文强调了睡眠在儿童和青少年糖尿病前期背景下的重要性，为读者提供了可考虑的实用见解。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine a teenager struggling to stay awake in class. It's not just about lack of interest; poor sleep can affect their health, especially if they have prediabetes.

Sleep disturbances are linked to insulin resistance, a key factor in prediabetes. Just as a well-oiled machine runs smoothly, a well-rested body can better manage blood sugar levels.

The study emphasizes that while puberty can temporarily reduce insulin sensitivity, most youth will see an improvement after this period.

However, this also means that identifying and managing prediabetes in youth requires a careful approach, different from adults.

Lifestyle modifications, including better sleep habits, are crucial for managing prediabetes in children and adolescents. It's not just about getting more sleep; it's about the quality and consistency of sleep that matters.

Practical steps can include setting a regular sleep schedule, creating a restful environment, and avoiding stimulating activities before bed. These changes can be discussed with healthcare providers to develop a personalized plan.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Sleep is a fundamental aspect of health that is often overlooked in discussions about prediabetes management.

This article highlights the importance of sleep in the context of prediabetes in children and adolescents, providing actionable insights for readers to consider.

Practical Takeaways

- Sleep quality is crucial for managing prediabetes in youth.
- Lifestyle modifications, including sleep habits, are key interventions.
- A regular sleep schedule and a restful environment can improve sleep quality.
- Avoid stimulating activities before bed to enhance sleep.
- Discuss sleep habits with healthcare providers for personalized management plans.
