## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the prevalence of metabolic syndrome in SLE patients and what are the clinical and demographic correlates?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 131 adult SLE patients aged 18 years and above, diagnosed per 2019 European League Against Rheumatism/American College of Rheumatology classification criteria.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Observational cross-sectional study with data collection including history, anthropometry, examination, and laboratory investigations.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of clinical features and laboratory parameters between SLE patients with high SLEDAI scores (≥10) and those with low SLEDAI scores (<10).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prevalence of metabolic syndrome, BMI, blood pressure, fasting glucose, triglycerides, and HDL levels.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** 38.5% of SLE patients met the criteria for metabolic syndrome. High SLEDAI patients had significantly higher BMI, blood pressure, fasting glucose, and triglycerides, and lower HDL levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is cross-sectional and thus cannot establish causation. The sample size is relatively small and may not be representative of all SLE patients.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with SLE and prediabetes, managing diet and lifestyle is crucial to reduce the risk of metabolic syndrome and related complications. Regular screening and integrated management strategies are recommended.

最近的研究显示，对于SLE患者来说，管理饮食和生活方式对于降低代谢综合征及相关并发症的风险至关重要。

就像保养良好的汽车运行得更好一样，管理良好的饮食和生活方式可以让你的身体保持更好的状态。

这项研究涉及131名成年SLE患者，发现38. 5%符合代谢综合征的标准。

SLEDAI评分较高的患者BMI、血压、空腹血糖和甘油三酯显著更高，而HDL水平更低。

这并不是说要完全避免某些食物，而是要进行聪明的替换和控制分量。

定期锻炼，比如快步走，也可以帮助管理体重和胰岛素抵抗。

研究结果强调了需要常规筛查和综合管理策略，以减轻这一高危群体的心血管并发症。

## English Plain-Language Version

At a family gathering, surrounded by delicious food, how can you enjoy the meal without risking your health if you have SLE and prediabetes?

Recent research underscores the importance of diet and lifestyle management for SLE patients to mitigate the risk of metabolic syndrome.

A well-maintained diet and lifestyle can keep your body in better shape, similar to a well-maintained car running better.

The study involved 131 adult SLE patients and found that 38. 5% met the criteria for metabolic syndrome.

High SLEDAI score patients had significantly higher BMI, blood pressure, fasting glucose, and triglycerides, and lower HDL levels.

Making smart food swaps and portion control can help manage metabolic risks.

Regular exercise, like a brisk walk, can aid in weight and insulin resistance management.

Consult with healthcare providers to create a sustainable and personalized routine.

The findings highlight the need for routine screening and integrated management strategies to reduce cardiovascular complications in high-risk SLE patients.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Metabolic syndrome increases the risk of cardiovascular disease and diabetes, which are particularly concerning for individuals with SLE.

Understanding how diet and lifestyle modifications can impact these risks is essential for managing prediabetes and improving overall health.

Practical Takeaways

- Diet and lifestyle management is crucial for SLE patients with prediabetes.
- Smart food swaps and portion control can help manage metabolic risks.
- Regular exercise can aid in weight and insulin resistance management.
- Consult with healthcare providers to create a sustainable and personalized routine.
- Routine screening and integrated management strategies are essential for high-risk SLE patients.
