## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the differences in biochemical and molecular markers between prediabetic and type 2 diabetes mellitus patients, and how can these inform early identification and intervention?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A study of 90 participants, including healthy controls, individuals with prediabetes, and patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus, conducted in Sulaymaniyah, Iraq.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Analysis of glycated hemoglobin (HbA1C), random blood glucose (RBG), C-peptide, lipid profile, urea, creatinine, and molecular markers miRNA-126 and miRNA-132.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Glycemic markers and molecular marker expressions were compared among the three groups.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Type 2 diabetes mellitus patients showed the highest values in glycemic markers and molecular marker expressions.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Glycemic markers were significantly higher in T2DM patients. Molecular markers miRNA-132 and miRNA-126 showed distinct expression patterns across groups, suggesting their potential as diagnostic markers.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The cross-sectional nature of the study and the limited sample size restrict the ability to establish causation and generalize findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** The study emphasizes the importance of early identification and comprehensive biochemical evaluation during the prediabetes phase to prevent progression to overt diabetes. Monitoring glycemic and molecular markers could aid in risk stratification and timely intervention.

## English Plain-Language Version

Prediabetes is a warning sign that you may develop type 2 diabetes. Making changes in diet and exercise can help manage this condition. For instance, choosing whole grains over refined ones can lower your glycemic index.

Smaller portions can help control food intake. Regular physical activity, such as walking, can improve insulin sensitivity. Quality sleep, aiming for 7-8 hours per night, supports metabolic health.

Discuss these steps with your doctor to prevent the onset of diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Early identification of prediabetes can prevent or delay the onset of type 2 diabetes. Understanding molecular markers can aid in early diagnosis and intervention.

Practical Takeaways

- Recognizing prediabetes is crucial for early intervention.
- Diet changes, such as opting for whole grains, can help manage blood sugar levels.
- Portion control and regular exercise are essential for prediabetes management.
- Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep per night to support metabolic health.
- Consult with healthcare providers to develop personalized lifestyle plans.
