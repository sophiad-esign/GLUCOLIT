- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do social determinants like gender and social class influence adherence to lifestyle modifications among people with prediabetes?

## English Plain-Language Version

Prediabetes is like a current drawing one towards type 2 diabetes. Lifestyle changes are the paddles that can steer clear of this waterfall. Gender and social class can significantly affect one's ability to paddle effectively.

Women often face work overload and caregiving, hindering their efforts, while men may benefit from stronger family support.

Lower socioeconomic backgrounds bring additional challenges, such as financial constraints and lack of time for healthy food choices and regular exercise.

Trust-based relationships with healthcare professionals can help sustain lifestyle changes. Finding a personalized approach that considers these social determinants is crucial for improving prevention strategies.

Diet changes, like choosing low-glycemic foods over high ones and portion control, are key. Exercise, aiming for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly, is also vital.

Gradually increase intensity and duration to make sustainable changes. Sleep, aiming for 7-9 hours per night, is equally important. Consistent sleep struggles may indicate a sleep disorder, warranting professional consultation.

Managing prediabetes requires understanding personal barriers and leveraging support systems. Discuss these with healthcare providers to develop a tailored plan.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the barriers and facilitators to lifestyle changes in prediabetes is relevant because it provides a roadmap for individuals to navigate the complexities of behavior modification, which is essential in preventing the

progression to type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Lifestyle changes are crucial in managing prediabetes and preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes.
- Social factors like gender and socioeconomic status can significantly influence one's ability to adopt and maintain healthy behaviors.
- Trust-based relationships with healthcare professionals can facilitate sustained lifestyle changes.
- A personalized approach that considers social determinants is essential for effective prediabetes management.
- Discuss your personal barriers and support systems with healthcare providers to develop a tailored plan.
