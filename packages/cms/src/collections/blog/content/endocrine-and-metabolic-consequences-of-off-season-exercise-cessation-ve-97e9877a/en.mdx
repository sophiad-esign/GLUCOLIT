## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does exercise cessation versus reduction during the off-season affect metabolic health in highly trained female basketball players?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 34 highly trained Division I female basketball players
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Randomized allocation to either an exercise cessation (EC) group or an exercise reduction (ER) group during a four-week off-season
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of circulating myonectin, free fatty acids (FFA), high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP), and glucose-insulin homeostasis between the EC and ER groups
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Circulating myonectin, FFA, hs-CRP, and glucose-insulin homeostasis assessed using a standard 75-g oral glucose tolerance test
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A significant group × time interaction for circulating myonectin with a greater decline in the EC group. Exercise cessation was associated with larger increases in FFA and insulin area under the curve, and a greater reduction in whole-body insulin sensitivity.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted over a short period of four weeks and only included highly trained Division I female basketball players, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, maintaining some level of physical activity during breaks from intense training may help attenuate early detraining-related alterations in myonectin and insulin sensitivity.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你是一位顶尖运动员，正在从训练中休息。 你的身体处理糖分的能力会改变吗？ 这项研究观察了两种不同的休赛期训练策略如何影响精英女性篮球运动员的代谢健康。 一组完全停止了运动，而另一组减少了训练。

仅仅四周后，完全停止运动的组观察到与体力活动水平相关的肌肉蛋白——肌凝蛋白(myonectin)显著下降。 这一组还有更高的游离脂肪酸水平和较低的胰岛素敏感性，胰岛素有助于调节血糖。 另一方面，即使在减少训练量的情况下，保持运动的组保持了更好的代谢健康。

看来，在高强度训练的休息期间保持一定水平的体力活动可以帮助维持代谢健康和胰岛素敏感性。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

了解休赛期运动习惯如何影响代谢健康对于运动员以及有糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗的个体至关重要。 这些知识可以指导他们做出关于运动计划的明智决定，以维持或改善他们的代谢健康。

你可以带走的重点

- 即使是短暂的运动中断也可能影响胰岛素敏感性和葡萄糖代谢。
- 在休赛期保持一定水平的体力活动可以帮助保持代谢健康。
- 精英运动员在休息期间可能从减少而不是停止运动中受益。
- 肌凝蛋白水平的变化可能是代谢健康变化的早期指标。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're a top athlete taking a break from training. Would your body's ability to handle sugar change?

This study looked at how two different off-season training strategies affected the metabolic health of elite female basketball players. One group stopped exercising altogether, while the other reduced their training.

After just four weeks, the group that stopped exercising saw a significant drop in a muscle protein called myonectin, which is linked to physical activity levels.

This group also had higher levels of free fatty acids and less sensitivity to insulin, which helps regulate blood sugar. On the other hand, the group that kept exercising, even at a reduced level, maintained better metabolic health.

It seems that keeping some level of physical activity during breaks from intense training can help maintain metabolic health and insulin sensitivity.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how exercise habits during off-season periods affect metabolic health is crucial for athletes and individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance.

This knowledge can guide them in making informed decisions about their exercise routines to maintain or improve their metabolic health.

Practical Takeaways

- Even a short break from exercise can impact insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism.
- Maintaining some level of physical activity during off-season can help preserve metabolic health.
- Elite athletes may benefit from reduced, rather than ceased, exercise during breaks.
- Changes in myonectin levels could be an early indicator of metabolic health shifts.
