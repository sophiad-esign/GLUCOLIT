## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do novel oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) metrics provide incremental predictive value for prediabetes remission beyond conventional glycemic criteria during lifestyle interventions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with overweight/obesity and predominantly impaired fasting glucose (IFG).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Long-term lifestyle intervention including an 8-week weight loss period followed by 148-week lifestyle-based weight maintenance.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Conventional glycemic metrics (fasting and 2-h glucose, HbA1c) versus novel OGTT metrics (1-h glucose, time to glucose peak, etc.).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prediabetes remission at 1, 2, and 3 years.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Novel OGTT metrics did not provide additional predictive value for prediabetes remission beyond conventional metrics.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was a post-hoc analysis and may have been subject to limitations inherent in such analyses. The population was predominantly IFG, limiting generalizability.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, particularly those with IFG, traditional glycemic measures may be sufficient to predict the likelihood of remission without the need for more complex OGTT metrics.

为什么值得关注

糖尿病前期是 2 型糖尿病发病前的关键阶段，生活方式的改变是预防其发展的关键。传统指标如空腹血糖和糖化血红蛋白用于诊断，但可能无法捕捉个体葡萄糖代谢的所有变化。

证据告诉我们什么

研究发现，新型 OGTT 指标并没有为糖尿病前期的缓解提供超出传统指标如空腹血糖和糖化血红蛋白的额外预测价值。

应该怎样理解

这项研究的重要性在于它阐明了糖尿病前期缓解的预测因素。尽管 OGTT 的新指标看似有前景，但它们并没有提供超出传统测试的预测信息，表明专注于简单措施可能足以指导治疗决策。

可以怎么做

医疗保健提供者可以依赖传统的血糖指标来评估糖尿病前期的风险，简化诊断过程。对于产品开发者来说，基于传统指标的干预措施可能比开发新测试更有效率。

了解糖尿病前期的发展对于早期干预和预防2型糖尿病至关重要。 这项研究提供了哪些指标对于预测结果最有用，这可以帮助设计有效的生活方式干预措施。

## English Plain-Language Version

Why it matters

Prediabetes is a critical window before type 2 diabetes onset, and lifestyle changes are key to its prevention. Traditional metrics like fasting glucose and HbA1c are used for diagnosis but may not capture all individual glucose metabolism variations.

What the evidence says

The study indicates that novel OGTT metrics do not offer extra predictive value for prediabetes remission over traditional metrics like fasting glucose and HbA1c.

What this means

This study is significant because it clarifies the predictive factors for prediabetes remission. Despite the promise of new OGTT metrics, they do not add predictive information beyond traditional tests, suggesting that focusing on simple measures may suffice for treatment decisions.

What readers can do

Healthcare providers can rely on traditional glycemic measures to assess prediabetes risk, simplifying diagnostics. For developers, focusing on interventions based on traditional metrics could be more efficient than developing new tests.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the progression of prediabetes is crucial for early intervention and prevention of type 2 diabetes.

This study provides insights into which metrics are most useful for predicting outcomes, which can help in designing effective lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Prediabetes is a critical phase for diabetes prevention.
- Traditional glycemic measures are still the best predictors of prediabetes remission.
- Novel OGTT metrics did not show additional predictive value.
- Lifestyle interventions are key in managing prediabetes.
