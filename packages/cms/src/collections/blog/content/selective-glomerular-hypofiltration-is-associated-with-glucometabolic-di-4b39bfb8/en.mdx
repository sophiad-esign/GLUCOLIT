## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between dysglycemia, insulin resistance, and the eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio in the general population?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 28,078 participants aged 50-64 years from the Swedish CArdioPulmonary bioImage Study.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of glycemic status, hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c), fasting glucose, and insulin resistance (HOMA-IR).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio across different glycemic categories.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Continuous eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio and SGHS defined as eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio <0.7.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Mean eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio declined across glycemic categories, and SGHS prevalence increased with higher HbA1c and fasting glucose levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is observational and does not establish causation. The eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio may be influenced by factors other than glomerular filtration rate.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** The findings suggest that individuals with higher blood sugar levels or insulin resistance may have a lower eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio, indicating potential early kidney vulnerability, which could be important for managing prediabetes and insulin resistance.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你在一家餐厅，服务员给你端来一道太咸的菜。 你可能会要求换一道不那么咸的。 同样，你的肾脏会过滤掉血液中的废物，如果它们工作不正常，可能会影响你的血糖水平。

最近的一项研究发现，血糖水平较高或有胰岛素抵抗的人肾小球滤过率比值（eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio）较低，这是衡量肾脏过滤功能的一个指标。 这可能意味着他们的肾脏工作效率较低，这可能是早期肾脏脆弱性的标志。

这项研究包括了来自瑞典CArdioPulmonary生物影像研究的超过28,000名50-64岁的参与者。 他们观察了不同的血糖水平，包括正常血糖、糖尿病前期和糖尿病。 结果显示，随着血糖水平的升高，eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio降低。

这可能很重要，因为它可能帮助我们理解肾功能和血糖之间的联系，并可能导致更好的管理这些状况的方法。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

你可以带走的重点

- 较高的血糖水平和胰岛素抵抗与较低的eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio相关联。
- 这种联系可能表明血糖较高或有胰岛素抵抗的人存在早期肾脏脆弱性。
- 理解这种联系可能有助于管理糖尿病前期和胰岛素抵抗。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a restaurant, and the waiter brings you a dish that's too salty. You might ask for a less salty version.

Similarly, your kidneys filter out waste from your blood, and if they're not working well, it can affect your blood sugar levels.

A recent study found that people with higher blood sugar levels or insulin resistance had a lower eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio, which is a measure of how well your kidneys are filtering.

This could mean that their kidneys are working less efficiently, which might be a sign of early kidney vulnerability. The study included over 28,000 participants aged 50-64 from the Swedish CArdioPulmonary bioImage Study.

They looked at different blood sugar levels, including those with normal blood sugar, prediabetes, and diabetes. The results showed that as blood sugar levels went up, the eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio went down.

This could be important because it might help us understand how kidney function and blood sugar are connected, and it could lead to better ways to manage these conditions.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between kidney function and glucose metabolism is crucial for individuals with prediabetes and insulin resistance, as it can provide insights into potential complications and guide treatment strategies.

Practical Takeaways

- Kidney function, as measured by the eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio, is associated with blood sugar levels.
- Higher blood sugar levels and insulin resistance are linked to a lower eGFRcys/eGFRcr-ratio.
- This connection could indicate early kidney vulnerability in people with higher blood sugar or insulin resistance.
- Understanding this link might help in managing prediabetes and insulin resistance.
