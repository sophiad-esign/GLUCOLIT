## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do diet, physical activity, sleep, metabolic health, and socioeconomic status influence cognitive function in school-aged children?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 170 school-aged children from a population-based cohort with a mean age of 6.33 years; 49% female.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of diet and physical activity using validated questionnaires, evaluation of cognitive function using NEPSY-II and TONI-2 subsets, and assessment of socioeconomic status.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Correlation and regression analyses to identify associations between cognitive function and various lifestyle and socioeconomic factors.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Cognitive function outcomes, particularly in attention, language, and memory.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Cognitive function was positively associated with Mediterranean diet adherence, sleep duration, socioeconomic status, and negatively associated with ultra-processed food intake, TV viewing, and metabolic markers including insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), C-reactive protein, and gamma-glutamyltransferase.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study shows associations but does not prove causation. The sample size is relatively small, and more research is needed to understand the complex relationships fully.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study does not provide a prescription for action, it suggests that a healthy diet, regular physical activity, and ensuring children get enough sleep could be practical steps parents can discuss with healthcare providers to support their child's cognitive development.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一个忙碌的家长试图在工作和照顾孩子之间找到平衡，并且希望为孩子的大脑健康提供最好的条件。 新研究揭示了孩子的饮食、体力活动、睡眠以及家庭的经济状况如何影响他们的认知发展。

研究评估了170名学龄儿童，发现坚持地中海饮食、较长的睡眠时间和更好的代谢健康状况与更好的认知功能相关，特别是在注意力、语言和记忆方面。 另一方面，过多的超加工食品、电视观看和较高的胰岛素抵抗水平与较差的认知结果相关。

这项研究提醒我们，健康的生活方式和社会公平不仅关乎身体健康——它们对大脑健康也至关重要。

地中海饮食富含水果、蔬菜和全谷物，与更好的认知表现相关。 这表明对心脏有益的东西也可能对大脑有益。 至于睡眠，研究发现较长的睡眠时间与更好的认知功能相关，强调了儿童充足睡眠的重要性。 经济状况也起了作用，较高的经济状况与更好的认知结果相关，突出了社会因素对大脑健康的影响。

需要注意的是，尽管这项研究显示了关联，但它并没有证明因果关系。 许多因素可以影响认知发展，需要更多的研究来完全理解这些复杂的关系。 然而，这些发现确实表明，健康的饮食、定期的体力活动和确保孩子获得足够的睡眠可能是家长可以与医疗保健提供者讨论的实用步骤，以支持孩子的认知发展。

总之，该研究强调了认知发展的多面性以及健康生活方式和社会公平对儿童认知健康的潜在益处。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine a busy parent trying to juggle work and kids, and wanting the best for their child's brain health.

A new study sheds light on how a child's diet, physical activity, sleep, and even their family's economic status can affect their cognitive development.

The research evaluated 170 school-aged children and found that adherence to a Mediterranean diet, longer sleep duration, and better metabolic health were linked to better cognitive function, particularly in attention, language, and memory.

On the flip side, too much ultra-processed food, TV viewing, and higher levels of insulin resistance were associated with poorer cognitive outcomes.

This study is a reminder that a healthy lifestyle and social equity are not just about physical health—they're also crucial for cognitive development.

The Mediterranean diet, rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, was associated with better cognitive performance. This suggests that what's good for the heart can also be good for the brain.

As for sleep, the study found that longer sleep duration was linked to better cognitive function, emphasizing the importance of restful nights for children.

Economic status also played a role, with higher status associated with better cognitive outcomes, highlighting the impact of social factors on brain health.

It's important to note that while this study shows associations, it doesn't prove causation. Many factors can influence cognitive development, and more research is needed to understand these complex relationships fully.

However, the findings do suggest that a healthy diet, regular physical activity, and ensuring children get enough sleep could be practical steps parents can discuss with healthcare providers to support their child's cognitive development.

In summary, the study underscores the multifaceted nature of cognitive development and the potential benefits of a healthy lifestyle and social equity for children's cognitive health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the factors that influence cognitive development is crucial for parents and policymakers aiming to support children's health and educational outcomes.

This study provides valuable insights into the multifaceted nature of cognitive development, emphasizing the importance of diet, physical activity, sleep, and socioeconomic status.

Practical Takeaways

- Adherence to a Mediterranean diet is associated with better cognitive function in children.
- Longer sleep duration is linked to improved cognitive performance, especially in attention, language, and memory.
- Higher socioeconomic status is associated with better cognitive outcomes in children.
- A healthy lifestyle and social equity are crucial for cognitive development.
- Parents can discuss practical steps like diet, activity, and sleep with healthcare providers to support their child's cognitive health.
