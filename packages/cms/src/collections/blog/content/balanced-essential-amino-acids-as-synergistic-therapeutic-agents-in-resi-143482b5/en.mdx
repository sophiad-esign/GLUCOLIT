## English Plain-Language Version

Skeletal muscle, crucial for body movement and metabolic homeostasis, is affected by declines in mass and function, which are linked to various diseases.

Resistance training (RT) and dietary essential amino acids (EAAs) are known to work synergistically to enhance muscle anabolism.

EAAs, as precursors and signaling molecules, stimulate muscle protein synthesis and neuromuscular junction remodeling, improving muscle strength and endurance.

The combination of EAAs and RT may offer a safe and effective strategy for preserving and enhancing muscle and metabolic health across different populations.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the synergistic effects of EAAs and resistance training is vital for developing nutritional strategies to combat muscle wasting and metabolic dysfunction.

Practical Takeaways

- Balanced EAAs combined with RT can lead to significant improvements in muscle protein synthesis.
- The synergistic effect of EAAs and RT may enhance muscle strength and endurance.
- Clinical populations, including those with sarcopenia and cachexia, may benefit from EAA and RT interventions.
- A mechanistic understanding of EAA-RT interactions is essential for interpreting clinical outcomes.
