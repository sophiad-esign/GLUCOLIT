## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between sleep duration and glycemic levels in adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A nationally representative sample of 1,090 adults with prediabetes from the 2017-2020 National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Sleep duration calculated using weekday and weekend sleep hours.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of glycemic levels (fasting plasma glucose and glycated hemoglobin) against sleep duration.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Fasting plasma glucose and glycated hemoglobin levels.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A nonlinear association was found with an inflection point at 6 hours of sleep per night, where additional sleep was associated with lower fasting plasma glucose levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's cross-sectional design limits the ability to establish causation, and the sample may not capture all demographic nuances.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For adults with prediabetes, ensuring at least 6 hours of sleep per night may help manage blood sugar levels, but individual responses may vary, and more research is needed.

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background For many, the struggle with prediabetes is a silent one, often unnoticed until it's too late. Sleep, a critical component of health, might play a more significant role in managing blood sugar levels than we realize.

### Core Findings The study found a nonlinear association between sleep duration and fasting plasma glucose levels, with an inflection point at 6 hours of sleep per night. Below this threshold, more sleep was linked to lower glucose levels.

### Your Interpretation and Critique While the study provides valuable insights, it's important to note its limitations. The sample size, though nationally representative, may not capture all nuances.

The findings suggest that short sleep duration could be a risk factor for elevated glucose levels in prediabetes, but more research is needed to establish causation.

The study's design does not allow for conclusions about the long-term effects of sleep on diabetes progression.

### Clinical/Business Insights #### A. Action Advice for Prediabetes Readers If you have prediabetes, consider the quality of your sleep. Aim for at least 6 hours per night, as it may help manage your blood sugar levels. #### B.

Insights for the Health Tech Industry There's an opportunity to develop tools that help track sleep patterns and provide personalized feedback to prediabetes patients to improve their sleep and, potentially, their glucose management.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between sleep and prediabetes is crucial for developing lifestyle interventions that can prevent the progression to full-blown diabetes.

### Practical Takeaways

- Sleep duration may influence blood sugar levels in prediabetes.
- A threshold of 6 hours of sleep per night is identified for its impact on glucose levels.
- More sleep than 6 hours did not show significant associations with glucose levels.
- The study highlights the need for further research on sleep and diabetes management.
