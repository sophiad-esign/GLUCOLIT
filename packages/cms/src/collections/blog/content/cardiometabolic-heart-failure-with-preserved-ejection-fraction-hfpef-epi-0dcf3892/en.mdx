## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the role of exercise in managing cardiometabolic heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and its associated metabolic dysfunctions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with cardiometabolic abnormalities such as obesity, insulin resistance, increased waist circumference, dyslipidemia, type 2 diabetes, and hypertension.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Exercise training, including a combination of aerobic and resistance exercises.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Lifestyle modifications including diet and exercise compared to pharmacologic interventions.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvement in exercise capacity, physical function, and quality of life.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exercise training, with or without calorie restriction, is an effective therapeutic management strategy for improving outcomes in patients with clinically stable HFpEF.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a review and does not provide new experimental data. The effectiveness of exercise may vary among individuals and depends on the specific exercise regimen and adherence.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, incorporating a combination of aerobic and resistance exercises into their routine can be beneficial. However, it's important to consult with a healthcare provider to tailor the exercise plan to personal needs and capabilities.

## English Plain-Language Version

Feeling tired after a long day at work, you decide to skip your evening walk. But taking those steps could be more than just a break from the desk—it might be a step towards better health.

Recent research highlights the importance of exercise in managing prediabetes and metabolic issues.

Individuals with cardiometabolic abnormalities, such as obesity and insulin resistance, can benefit from a combination of aerobic and resistance exercises.

This not only improves their physical function but also enhances their quality of life. Exercise, when paired with a healthy diet, can be a powerful tool in the fight against prediabetes and metabolic syndrome.

It's not about turning into an athlete overnight but finding a sustainable routine that fits into your lifestyle. Remember, every bit of movement counts.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise is a crucial component of lifestyle modification that can significantly impact prediabetes and metabolic health. Understanding its role is essential for individuals looking to prevent or manage these conditions.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular exercise can help manage prediabetes and improve metabolic health.

- Consult with a healthcare provider to develop a personalized exercise plan.
