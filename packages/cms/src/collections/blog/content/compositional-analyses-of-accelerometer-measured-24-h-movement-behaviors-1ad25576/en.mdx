## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do 24-hour movement behaviors, including sleep, sedentary behavior, light-intensity physical activity, and moderate-to-vigorous physical activity, associate with prediabetes phenotypes and diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1222 participants from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey 2005-2006, with a mean age of 41.02 years and comprising 47.22% males.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Time spent in sleep, sedentary behavior, light-intensity physical activity, and moderate-to-vigorous physical activity measured via accelerometer and self-report.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of movement behaviors with normoglycemia, pre-diabetes (i-IFG, i-IGT, or combined IFG + IGT), and diabetes based on the American Diabetes Association criteria.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Associations of movement behaviors with glycemic status.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** None of the movement behaviors were associated with i-IFG. SB was detrimentally associated with i-IGT, and MVPA was favorably associated with combined IFG + IGT. More time spent in sleep or MVPA, and less in SB, were associated with lower odds of diabetes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Cross-sectional study design limits causal inference. Self-reported sleep duration may introduce bias.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, managing daily activities by reducing sedentary time, optimizing sleep, and incorporating MVPA may help in diabetes prevention and management.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，如果把你的一天比作一个饼图，分为睡眠、静坐、轻度活动和锻炼。 最近的一项研究表明，这些部分如何影响你患糖尿病前期和糖尿病的风险。 对于有糖尿病前期的人来说，过多的静坐与餐后血糖较高有关，而更多的睡眠和剧烈运动则降低了这种风险。

这意味着你每减少一小时的静坐时间，增加到睡眠或锻炼上，都可能改善你的健康结果。 但请记住，每个人的身体都是独一无二的，适合别人的不一定适合你。 关键是找到适合你生活方式的平衡点。

睡眠不仅仅是数量问题，质量问题同样重要。 良好的夜间睡眠有助于调节血糖。 如果你经常感到疲劳或难以集中注意力，这可能是睡眠质量差的迹象。 尝试建立规律的睡眠时间表，并创造一个有助于休息的环境。

谈到体力活动，不仅仅是多少的问题，还有频率问题。 短暂的剧烈运动和较长时间的中等强度活动一样有益。 尝试在你的一天中加入更多的活动，比如走楼梯或散步会议。

研究还提醒我们，不同的人对同样的活动可能有不同的反应。 关键是找到一个你喜欢并能坚持的活动习惯。

总之，通过平衡睡眠、减少久坐时间以及增加体力活动来管理你的一天，可以是迈向更好糖尿病管理的一步。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病前期是一个关键阶段，生活方式的改变可以显著影响糖尿病的发展。 了解日常活动如睡眠和体力活动如何影响血糖水平，为希望管理自己健康的人提供了可行的见解。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine your day as a pie chart, split into sleep, sitting, light activity, and exercise. A recent study shows how these slices affect your risk of prediabetes and diabetes.

For those with prediabetes, too much sitting is linked to higher blood sugar after meals, while more sleep and vigorous exercise lower the risk.

This means for every hour you cut sitting, add to sleep or exercise, you may improve your health outcomes. But remember, every body is unique, so what works for one might not work for all.

It's about finding a balance that fits your lifestyle.

Sleep is not just about the quantity but also the quality. A good night's sleep can help regulate blood sugar. If you often feel tired or have trouble concentrating, it might be a sign of poor sleep quality.

Try to establish a regular sleep schedule and create a restful environment.

When it comes to physical activity, it's not just about how much but also how often. Short bursts of intense activity can be as beneficial as longer periods of moderate activity.

Try to incorporate more movement into your day, like taking the stairs or walking meetings.

The study also reminds us that different people may respond differently to the same activities. What's crucial is finding an activity routine that you enjoy and can stick to.

In summary, managing your day by balancing sleep, reducing sedentary time, and increasing physical activity can be a step towards better diabetes management.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical phase where lifestyle changes can significantly impact the progression to diabetes.

- Balance your daily activities to include less sitting, more sleep, and regular physical activity.

- Incorporate short bursts of intense activity into your day for health benefits.
- Different activities work for different people, find what you enjoy and can stick to.
