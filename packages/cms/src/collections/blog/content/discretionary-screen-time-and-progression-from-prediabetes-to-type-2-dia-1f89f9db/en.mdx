## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is discretionary screen time associated with the risk of type 2 diabetes among individuals with prediabetes, and do sleep and the triglyceride-glucose index mediate this relationship?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 41,978 participants with prediabetes from the UK Biobank
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Discretionary screen time, including television viewing and leisure computer use
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of screen time duration with risk of type 2 diabetes
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incident type 2 diabetes confirmed through hospital, primary care, and death registry records
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** More than 5 hours of discretionary screen time per day was associated with a 26% higher risk of type 2 diabetes. Television viewing showed a stronger association than leisure computer use.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study shows an association but does not prove causation. The sample is specific to the UK Biobank and may not be generalizable to all populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While managing prediabetes, consider the impact of screen time on sleep and insulin resistance, as reducing screen time may help in preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你正在努力管理自己的糖尿病前期，同时也在与睡眠问题作斗争。 最近的一项研究可能为你提供了一些见解。 结果表明，过多地花时间在屏幕前可能会增加你发展为2型糖尿病的风险，而这种风险部分可能是由于屏幕时间如何影响你的睡眠和胰岛素抵抗。

这项研究观察了来自英国生物银行的41,000多名糖尿病前期患者。 他们发现，那些每天花费超过5小时在随意屏幕时间上的人，发展为2型糖尿病的风险高出了26%。 对于经常看电视的人来说，这个风险更高，风险高出35%。

研究表明，减少屏幕时间可能是预防2型糖尿病的一个有益策略，尤其是当考虑到对睡眠和胰岛素抵抗的影响时。 重要的是要记住，虽然这项研究显示了一种联系，但它并不能证明屏幕时间直接导致糖尿病。 然而，它确实给了我们一个理由去思考我们如何能够平衡我们的屏幕时间与其他健康习惯，比如获得足够的睡眠。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病前期是一个关键阶段，生活方式的改变可以显著影响向2型糖尿病的进展。 了解日常习惯如屏幕时间和睡眠质量如何影响这种风险，对于希望主动管理自己健康的人来说至关重要。

- 改善睡眠和管理胰岛素抵抗可能是这种关系中的关键中介因素。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're trying to manage your prediabetes, and you're also struggling with sleep. A recent study might have some insights for you.

It turns out that spending too much time in front of screens could be increasing your risk of developing type 2 diabetes, and part of this risk might be due to how screen time affects your sleep and insulin resistance.

This study looked at over 41,000 people with prediabetes from the UK Biobank. They found that those who spent more than 5 hours a day on discretionary screen time had a 26% higher risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

This risk was even higher for those who watched a lot of TV, with a 35% higher risk.

The study suggests that reducing screen time could be a helpful strategy to prevent type 2 diabetes, especially when considering the impact on sleep and insulin resistance.

It's important to remember that while this study shows a link, it doesn't prove that screen time directly causes diabetes.

However, it does give us a reason to think about how we can balance our screen time with other healthy habits, like getting enough sleep.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical stage where lifestyle changes can significantly impact the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding how daily habits like screen time and sleep quality influence this risk is essential for individuals looking to manage their health proactively.

Practical Takeaways

- Reducing discretionary screen time may help prevent the progression from prediabetes to type 2 diabetes.
- More than 5 hours of screen time per day is associated with a higher risk of type 2 diabetes.
- The risk is particularly higher for those who watch a lot of TV.
- Improving sleep and managing insulin resistance could be key mediators in this relationship.
