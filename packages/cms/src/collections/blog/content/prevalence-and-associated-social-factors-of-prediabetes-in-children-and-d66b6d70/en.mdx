## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the prevalence of prediabetes and what are the associated social factors among children and adolescents in India?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Children and adolescents aged 6-19 years in India.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Prediabetes status and associated social factors.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between different age groups, sexes, and socioeconomic statuses.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Pooled prevalence of prediabetes and identification of key social correlates.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The pooled prevalence of prediabetes was 10% with high heterogeneity. Prevalence was higher in older adolescents, females, and those from urban high socioeconomic backgrounds. Key social correlates included sedentary lifestyle, overnutrition, and high sugar-sweetened beverage consumption.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study faced high heterogeneity in the prevalence of prediabetes, indicating wide regional variability. The prediction intervals indicated a wide range of possible prevalence rates.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study provides valuable insights into the prevalence of prediabetes and associated factors in Indian youth, it's important to recognize the limitations and variability in the data. This information can guide public health interventions and individual lifestyle modifications to manage prediabetes, but it should not be used to make definitive health claims for individual cases.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，印度的一个青少年，忙于学校和朋友，得知自己患有糖尿病前期。 这是一个警钟，但通过正确的改变，他们可以降低发展成2型糖尿病的风险。 最近的一项研究表明，印度十分之一的青少年患有糖尿病前期，受年龄、性别和生活方式等因素的影响。

这是针对生活方式改变和早期筛查的针对性公共卫生干预措施的行动号召。

糖尿病前期是一个警告信号，表明血糖水平高于正常但尚未达到糖尿病的阈值。 这是一个在为时已晚之前采取行动的机会。 研究发现，年龄较大的青少年、女性和来自城市高社会经济背景的人群风险更高。 与糖尿病前期相关的主要社会因素包括久坐不动的生活方式、过度营养和高糖饮料消费。

为了对抗这个问题，考虑均衡饮食，多吃蔬菜、全谷物和瘦蛋白。 用不含糖的茶或水代替含糖饮料。 控制分量是关键； 盘子应该一半是蔬菜，四分之一是全谷物，四分之一是蛋白质。 定期进行体力活动，如散步或骑自行车，也有助于控制体重和血糖水平。 目标是每周至少进行150分钟的适度运动。

重要的是要记住，尽管生活方式的改变可以显著降低发展成2型糖尿病的风险，但它们并不是保证。 每个人的情况都是独特的，与医疗保健提供者合作制定个性化计划至关重要。

该研究的结果强调了公共卫生工作需要解决健康社会决定因素的需要，并促进早期筛查和生活方式的改变。 通过这样做，我们可以帮助印度的年轻人控制自己的健康，预防2型糖尿病的发作。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病前期是一个关键的健康问题，如果管理不当，可能会导致2型糖尿病。 了解特定人群，如印度儿童和青少年的患病率和相关因素，对于制定针对性干预措施至关重要。 这项研究提供了有关糖尿病前期社会决定因素的宝贵见解，并强调了生活方式改变在管理这一状况中的重要性。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine a teenager in India, busy with school and friends, who learns they have prediabetes. It's a wake-up call, but with the right changes, they can reduce their risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

A recent study shows that one in ten Indian youth has prediabetes, influenced by factors like age, sex, and lifestyle.

This is a call to action for tailored public health interventions focusing on lifestyle modifications and early screening.

Prediabetes is a warning sign that blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet at the diabetes threshold. It's a chance to act before it's too late.

The study found that older adolescents, females, and those from urban high socioeconomic backgrounds are at higher risk.

Key social factors linked to prediabetes include a sedentary lifestyle, overnutrition, and high sugar-sweetened beverage consumption.

To combat this, consider a balanced diet with plenty of vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Swap sugary drinks for water or unsweetened tea. Portion control is key;

a plate should be half full of vegetables, a quarter of whole grains, and a quarter of protein. Regular physical activity, such as walking or cycling, can also help manage weight and blood sugar levels.

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week.

It's important to remember that while lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes, they are not a guarantee.

Each person's situation is unique, and it's crucial to work with healthcare providers to develop a personalized plan.

The study's findings underscore the need for public health efforts to address the social determinants of health and promote early screening and lifestyle modifications.

By doing so, we can help young people in India take control of their health and prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical health issue that can lead to type 2 diabetes if not managed properly.

Understanding the prevalence and associated factors in specific populations, such as Indian children and adolescents, is essential for developing targeted interventions.

This study provides valuable insights into the social determinants of prediabetes and emphasizes the importance of lifestyle modifications in managing this condition.

Practical Takeaways

- Prediabetes is a serious concern affecting one in ten Indian youth.
- Lifestyle modifications, including diet and exercise, can significantly reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.
- A balanced diet with controlled portions and regular physical activity are key components of managing prediabetes.
- It's crucial to work with healthcare providers to develop a personalized plan to manage prediabetes.
- Public health efforts are needed to address the social determinants of health and promote early screening and lifestyle modifications.
