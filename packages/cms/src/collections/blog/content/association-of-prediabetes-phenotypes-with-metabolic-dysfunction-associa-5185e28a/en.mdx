## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between different prediabetes phenotypes and metabolic dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease (MAFLD) and liver fibrosis?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A total of 1599 subjects, including those with normal glucose tolerance and prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Prediabetes stratified as isolated defects (impaired fasting glucose, impaired glucose tolerance, or impaired hemoglobin A1c), two defects, or all three defects.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between prediabetes phenotypes and normal glucose tolerance regarding the prevalence of MAFLD and liver fibrosis.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Odds of MAFLD and liver fibrosis associated with different prediabetes phenotypes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Prediabetes with two or three glucometabolic defects had increased odds of MAFLD compared to those with a single defect. Insulin resistance may mediate this association.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is cross-sectional and does not establish causation. The findings are specific to the population studied and may not be generalizable.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes should be aware of the potential increased risk of MAFLD based on their specific glucometabolic defects and consider lifestyle modifications to mitigate risks.

## English Plain-Language Version

Study Background

Prediabetes is a state of elevated blood sugar levels that precedes diabetes, signaling the need for lifestyle changes to prevent disease progression.

This study investigates the link between prediabetes phenotypes and metabolic dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease (MAFLD).

What the evidence says

The study reveals higher MAFLD prevalence in prediabetes, with increased odds associated with multiple glucometabolic defects, suggesting insulin resistance as a mediator.

What this means

The heterogeneity of prediabetes impacts liver health differently, underscoring the need for tailored management strategies. These findings emphasize the significance of early screening and intervention.

What readers can do

For individuals with prediabetes, monitoring liver health is essential. The health tech industry can leverage this knowledge to develop targeted screening and intervention tools.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant because it provides insights into the heterogeneity of prediabetes and its implications on liver health, which is crucial for early intervention and prevention strategies.

Practical Takeaways

- Different types of prediabetes have varying risks for fatty liver disease.
- Managing prediabetes requires a personalized approach considering liver health.
- Lifestyle changes are crucial for individuals with prediabetes to prevent progression to diabetes and related complications.
- Health tech can aid in early detection and personalized management of prediabetes and liver health.
