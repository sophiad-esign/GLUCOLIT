## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the intervention needs and ideas of young adults with prediabetes for diabetes prevention?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 24 publicly-insured adults aged 18-26 years with prediabetes in a US city.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Qualitative study with semistructured interviews exploring perspectives on intervention needs and ideas.
- **对照 / Comparison:** N/A
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Internal and external factors affecting dietary and exercise behaviors within the Capability-Opportunity-Motivation-Behavior framework.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Participants had good nutrition knowledge but faced significant Opportunity barriers such as the high cost of healthy food and time constraints. Motivation varied, with most wanting to avoid diabetes but mixed perceptions of risk and self-efficacy.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was qualitative and focused on a specific demographic, limiting generalizability.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While young adults with prediabetes have a good understanding of nutrition, practical barriers like cost and time significantly impact their ability to make healthy choices. Interventions should consider these real-life challenges to be more effective.
