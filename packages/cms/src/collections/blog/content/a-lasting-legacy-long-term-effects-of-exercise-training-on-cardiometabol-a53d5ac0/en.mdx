## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do brief structured exercise programs confer lasting cardiometabolic benefits in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 73 participants (71.3±7.2 years; 64% women; 77% White) who completed one of four 6-month interventions differing in exercise amount, intensity, and inclusion of diet-induced weight loss.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Four different 6-month exercise programs.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Longitudinal trajectories evaluated using linear mixed effects models; baseline-adjusted associations among short-term intervention response and Reunion outcomes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvement in abdominal adiposity, decline in cardiorespiratory fitness and fat-free mass, and continued fat mass reductions in women who did high-amount exercise.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exercise training produced several legacy health effects persisting more than a decade later, with effects differing by sex and exercise dose.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's limitations include a relatively small sample size and a specific demographic composition, which may limit the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, engaging in structured exercise programs can have long-lasting benefits on cardiometabolic health, emphasizing the importance of incorporating exercise into lifestyle changes to manage prediabetes.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你完成了一个为期6个月的运动计划，多年后仍在享受其带来的益处。 这就是STRRIDE-糖尿病前期重聚研究的启示，该研究发现运动可以为糖尿病前期患者留下持久的健康遗产。 这项研究涉及73名参与者，他们的平均年龄超过70岁。

他们在完成四种不同的为期6个月的运动计划后大约11年接受了评估。 结果显示，在随访期间，所有组别的腰围平均减少了约3厘米，表明腹部脂肪有所改善。 然而，心肺健康和无脂质量显著下降。 有趣的是，进行大量运动的女性继续减少了总脂肪量。

研究表明，对短期运动干预的反应可以预测长期结果，强调了针对性运动策略对于维持心血管代谢健康和随着年龄增长保持功能独立性的重要性。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you've just finished a 6-month exercise program, and years later, you're still reaping the benefits.

That's the takeaway from the STRRIDE-Prediabetes Reunion Study, which found that exercise can leave a lasting legacy on health, especially for those with prediabetes.

The study involved 73 participants who, on average, were over 70 years old. They were assessed about 11 years after completing one of four different 6-month exercise programs.

The results showed that waist circumference decreased by about 3 cm over the follow-up period, indicating improved abdominal adiposity across all groups. However, cardiorespiratory fitness and fat-free mass declined significantly.

Interestingly, women who did high-amount exercise saw continued reductions in total fat mass.

The study suggests that the response to short-term exercise interventions can predict long-term outcomes, emphasizing the importance of targeted exercise strategies for maintaining cardiometabolic health and functional independence as we

age.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise is a key component of managing prediabetes and reducing the risk of developing type 2 diabetes. Understanding the long-term effects of exercise can help individuals make informed decisions about their health and lifestyle choices.

Practical Takeaways

- Exercise can have a lasting positive impact on health, especially for those with prediabetes.
- Waist circumference decreased by about 3 cm over the follow-up period, indicating improved abdominal adiposity.
- Cardiorespiratory fitness and fat-free mass declined significantly, suggesting the need for ongoing exercise to maintain these aspects of health.
- Women who did high-amount exercise saw continued reductions in total fat mass, highlighting the importance of exercise dose.
- The response to short-term exercise interventions can predict long-term outcomes, emphasizing the importance of consistent exercise.
