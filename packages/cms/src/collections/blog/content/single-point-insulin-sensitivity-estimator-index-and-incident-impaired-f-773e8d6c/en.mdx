## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between the SPISE index and the risk of incident impaired fasting glucose (IFG) in Chinese adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 100,494 Chinese adults with normal fasting plasma glucose at baseline.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** SPISE index as a measure of insulin sensitivity.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Incident IFG during follow-up.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Hazard ratio for incident IFG associated with each one-unit increase in the SPISE index.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A higher SPISE index was independently associated with a lower risk of IFG. Each one-unit increase in the SPISE index was associated with a hazard ratio of 0.91 for incident IFG.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is retrospective and observational, limiting causal inferences. The SPISE index requires further validation in diverse populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, a higher SPISE index may indicate lower risk of progressing to IFG. This suggests that lifestyle interventions aimed at improving insulin sensitivity could be beneficial.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你站在一个十字路口，选择一条通往更健康的道路，还是一条可能导致糖尿病的道路。 SPISE指数就像一个路标，帮助你决定走哪条路。 它是一个简单的工具，可以在不需要胰岛素测量的情况下测量胰岛素敏感性。 这很重要，因为胰岛素抵抗是发展为糖尿病前期和糖尿病的关键因素。

最近在中国进行的一项研究发现，较高的SPISE指数与较低的IFG发展风险相关，IFG是糖尿病的预警信号。 这意味着通过提高胰岛素敏感性，我们可以降低糖尿病前期发展为完全糖尿病的风险。 研究表明，较低的SPISE指数是未来IFG风险的红旗。

通过了解这一点，我们可以做出饮食和生活方式的改变，以保持SPISE指数高，糖尿病风险低。 例如，富含全谷物、瘦蛋白和大量蔬菜的饮食可以帮助提高胰岛素敏感性。 定期锻炼，即使是快走，也可以有所作为。 这不仅仅是关于你吃什么，还关于你吃多少以及你多久移动一次。

记住，SPISE指数是一个指南，不是保证，而且最好与你的医疗服务提供者讨论变化。 研究结果提醒我们，饮食和锻炼上的小步骤可以对我们的健康产生重大影响。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

SPISE指数之所以相关，是因为它提供了一个简单的方法来评估胰岛素敏感性，这是发展为糖尿病前期和糖尿病的关键因素。 了解你的SPISE指数可以帮助你做出关于饮食和生活方式的明智决定，以降低发展这些状况的风险。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, deciding between a path that leads to better health or one that could lead to diabetes. The SPISE index is like a signpost, helping you know which way to go.

It's a simple tool that measures insulin sensitivity without needing insulin measurements. This is important because insulin resistance is a key factor in developing prediabetes and diabetes.

A recent study in China found that a higher SPISE index was linked to a lower risk of developing IFG, a warning sign for diabetes.

This means that by improving insulin sensitivity, we can reduce our risk of prediabetes progressing to full-blown diabetes. The study suggests that a lower SPISE index is a red flag for future IFG risk.

By understanding this, we can make dietary and lifestyle changes to keep our SPISE index high and our diabetes risk low. For example, a diet rich in whole grains, lean proteins, and plenty of vegetables can help improve insulin sensitivity.

Regular exercise, even just a brisk walk, can also make a difference. It's not just about what you eat, but also about how much and how often you move.

Remember, the SPISE index is a guide, not a guarantee, and it's always best to discuss changes with your healthcare provider. The study's results are a reminder that small steps in diet and exercise can have a big impact on our health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

The SPISE index is relevant because it provides a simple way to assess insulin sensitivity, which is a key factor in the development of prediabetes and diabetes.

Understanding your SPISE index can help you make informed decisions about your diet and lifestyle to reduce your risk of developing these conditions.

Practical Takeaways

- A higher SPISE index is associated with a lower risk of developing IFG.
- Improving insulin sensitivity through diet and exercise can reduce diabetes risk.
- Whole grains, lean proteins, and vegetables can help improve insulin sensitivity.
- Regular physical activity, like brisk walking, contributes to better insulin sensitivity.
- Discuss lifestyle changes with your healthcare provider to manage prediabetes effectively.
