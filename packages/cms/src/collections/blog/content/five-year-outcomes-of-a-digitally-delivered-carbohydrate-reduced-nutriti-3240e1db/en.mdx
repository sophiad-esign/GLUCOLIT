## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can a digitally delivered, low-carb diet help adults with prediabetes maintain weight loss and metabolic health over five years, potentially preventing the onset of type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults aged 21-65 with prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Continuous remote care intervention delivering individualized carbohydrate-reduced nutrition therapy.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Baseline measures and outcomes over a five-year period.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glycemic status, weight, insulin resistance, lipids, inflammatory markers, and liver and kidney function.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** At five years, 45 participants completed follow-up with 22% achieving normoglycemia and 13% progressing to T2D. Mean body weight was reduced by 5.8 kg (5.3%), with 47%, 29%, and 13% achieving ≥5%, ≥10%, and ≥15% weight loss, respectively. Liver and kidney function was stable.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was a single-arm longitudinal study without a control group, and not all participants saw the same benefits.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, a digitally supported, low-carb diet may help manage weight and metabolic health, but more research is needed to understand long-term effects and individual variability.

## 原文精华摘要

糖尿病前期是一条可能带你走向名为2型糖尿病瀑布的河流。 新研究表明，数字化支持的低碳水饮食可能是让你远离边缘的强大桨。

这项研究对糖尿病前期成人进行了五年的跟踪。 它发现，那些坚持个性化低碳水饮食的人体重显著减轻，血糖控制也更好。 即使平均来看，他们的血糖水平与开始时相似，这也是真的。 但这里有个问题：并非所有参与研究的人都看到了这些好处，饮食可能对每个人的作用不同。

实际上，这意味着如果你有糖尿病前期，低碳水饮食可能是你工具箱中的一个工具。 它不是灵丹妙药，但它可以帮助你管理你的健康。 同样重要的是要记住，这只是一项研究，需要更多的研究来完全理解低碳水饮食如何适应糖尿病预防计划。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

## English Plain-Language Version

Navigating prediabetes, a condition that can escalate to type 2 diabetes, is akin to steering clear of a waterfall. According to a study, a digitally delivered, low-carb diet may serve as a robust paddle in this journey.

Over the course of five years, the study observed adults with prediabetes who adhered to a personalized low-carb diet, noting significant weight loss and improved blood sugar control.

Despite average blood sugar levels mirroring initial readings, a considerable portion of participants achieved normoglycemia, suggesting the potential for diabetes prevention.

It's important to note that the diet's effectiveness varied among individuals, indicating that a one-size-fits-all approach may not apply.

This underscores the need for further research to fully grasp how a low-carb diet can be integrated into a diabetes prevention strategy.

The study's findings hint that digital support could be pivotal in diet adherence, hinting at a more significant role for technology in future diabetes prevention efforts.

In summary, a low-carb diet, bolstered by digital support, may be a valuable tool for managing prediabetes, yet it is not a universal solution. Consulting with healthcare providers before embarking on any new diet is essential.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Preventing type 2 diabetes is crucial for individuals with prediabetes, as it can significantly reduce the risk of cardiovascular disease, kidney disease, and other health complications.

This study provides insights into how a digitally supported, low-carb diet can help in this prevention, which is relevant for public health and individual disease management.

Practical Takeaways

- A low-carb diet can be part of a diabetes prevention strategy.
- Digital support may enhance adherence to dietary changes.
- Long-term effects of a low-carb diet need further research.
- This approach may not be suitable for everyone.
- Consult a healthcare provider before starting any new diet regimen.
