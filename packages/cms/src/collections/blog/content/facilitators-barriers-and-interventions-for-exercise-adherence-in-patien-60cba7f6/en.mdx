## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the barriers and facilitators to exercise adherence in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Exercise adherence
- **对照 / Comparison:** Lack of time, lack of appropriate exercise equipment or venues, and health-related physical limitations as barriers; perceived health benefits, social support, and tools or methods to help with exercise adherence as facilitators
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Enhanced physical activity maintenance
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The review identified 'lack of time,' 'lack of appropriate exercise equipment or venues,' and 'health-related physical limitations' as predominant barriers, while 'perceived health benefits,' 'social support,' and 'tools or methods to help with exercise adherence' emerged as key facilitators.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a mixed-methods systematic review that mapped findings to theoretical frameworks, which may not fully capture the individual experiences and contexts of all prediabetes patients.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes can discuss with their healthcare providers the creation of personalized exercise plans that consider the identified barriers and facilitators to improve exercise adherence and potentially reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

## English Plain-Language Version

Managing prediabetes involves lifestyle changes, with exercise being crucial.

However, maintaining an exercise routine can be difficult due to various barriers.

A systematic review has shed light on what hinders and helps prediabetes patients adhere to exercise.

Key barriers include lack of time, inadequate facilities, and physical health issues.

On the positive side, understanding health benefits, social support, and using tools can boost adherence.

Tailored exercise plans with clear goals and social support are recommended to enhance adherence.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the factors influencing exercise adherence in prediabetes is vital for developing effective interventions to prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Exercise is essential for prediabetes management but can be hard to maintain.
- Barriers such as lack of time and facilities can impede exercise routines.
- Facilitators like understanding health benefits and social support are crucial.
- Personalized plans with clear goals and social support can improve exercise adherence.
