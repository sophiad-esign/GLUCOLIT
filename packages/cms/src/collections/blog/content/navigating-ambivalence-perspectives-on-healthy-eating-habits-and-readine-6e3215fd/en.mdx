- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the attitudes of men with prediabetes towards managing their diet and lifestyle after diagnosis?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 21 Swedish men aged 60-72 with prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Qualitative study with individual and group interviews
- **对照 / Comparison:** N/A
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Attitudes towards healthy eating habits and readiness for change
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Men recognized the importance of healthy habits but doubted their ability to maintain lasting change, often due to personal limitations and a focus on short-term goals.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study used convenience sampling and may not be representative of all men with prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While men with prediabetes understand the need for change, practical and psychological support is necessary to help them translate knowledge into lasting lifestyle habits.

  English Plain-Language Version
  Diabetes is a growing health concern, with prediabetes being a critical stage where lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

Despite knowing the importance of diet and exercise, many struggle with making these changes due to various barriers.
Men with prediabetes recognize the need for healthy habits but doubt their ability to maintain them. They often focus on short-term goals and seek social support to aid in their transition towards healthier behaviors.
This study sheds light on the psychological and social aspects that influence men's readiness to change their lifestyle after a prediabetes diagnosis.

It highlights the importance of addressing emotional distress and ambivalence in interventions.

The findings suggest that while men are aware of the need for change, practical and psychological support is crucial for lasting lifestyle modifications.
For those with prediabetes, seeking social support and setting realistic, short-term goals can be a step towards sustainable lifestyle changes. Engaging in activities that are enjoyable and include others can help maintain motivation.
The health tech industry can develop tools that leverage social networks to support lifestyle changes. Creating platforms that allow sharing of successes and challenges can foster a community that encourages long-term behavior change.
Understanding the attitudes and barriers faced by men with prediabetes is crucial for developing effective interventions that can help prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

- Men with prediabetes are aware of the need for lifestyle changes.
- Doubts and personal limitations阻碍男性维持健康习惯。
- Social support and short-term goals are crucial for behavior change.
- Interventions should consider psychological barriers and social networks.
