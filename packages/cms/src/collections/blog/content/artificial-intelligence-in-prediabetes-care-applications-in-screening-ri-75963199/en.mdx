## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How can artificial intelligence assist in prediabetes care, particularly in lifestyle interventions like diet and physical activity?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes, a condition characterized by impaired fasting glucose or impaired glucose tolerance.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** AI-assisted approaches for prediabetes screening, risk prediction, and lifestyle intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Traditional risk scores and lifestyle intervention programs compared to AI-based models integrating various data sources.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improved discrimination beyond traditional risk scores, clinically relevant performance for identifying high-risk individuals, and potential support for lifestyle change through digital programs.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** AI-based models can integrate diverse data sources to enhance risk prediction and suggest personalized lifestyle interventions. Digital programs have shown potential in supporting lifestyle changes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Heterogeneity in prediabetes definitions, limited external validation, uncertain long-term effectiveness, and concerns over privacy and governance.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While AI shows promise in assisting with prediabetes management, it should be viewed as a supportive tool rather than a standalone solution. Its effectiveness and role in routine care are still being established.

AI模型可以通过使用更广泛的数据来改善传统风险评分，包括连续葡萄糖监测和可穿戴设备衍生的行为信号。 这意味着AI可以提供更全面的健康状况视图，识别传统方法可能遗漏的模式。 然而，重要的是要记住，虽然AI可以建议干预措施，但它不能取代专业医疗建议的需求。

这项技术仍在完善中，其有效性在不同人群中可能会有所不同。

在饮食方面，AI可能建议将高血糖指数食物换成低血糖指数的替代品，或增加饮食控制。 对于运动，它可以推荐适合你当前健身水平的频率和强度，确保安全和坚持日常锻炼。 睡眠建议可能包括建立规律的作息时间和提高睡眠质量。 这些都是应该与医疗保健提供者讨论的谨慎步骤。

AI在糖尿病前期护理中的角色是协助，而不是指挥。 它是一个可以帮助识别风险和建议生活方式改变的工具，但最终的决定和行动应该始终在与医疗保健专业人员咨询后进行。 这项技术充满希望，但它不是万能的——它是管理糖尿病前期的更广泛方法的一部分。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, unsure which path leads to better health. Prediabetes is like standing at such a junction, where small steps can lead to significant health improvements.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is emerging as a guide, helping to tailor these steps to your unique needs.

By integrating various data points, from clinical records to personal habits, AI can suggest personalized diet and lifestyle changes that could reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.

AI models can improve on traditional risk scores by using a broader range of data, including continuous glucose monitoring and wearable-derived behavioral signals.

This means AI can provide a more comprehensive view of your health, identifying patterns that traditional methods might miss.

However, it's important to remember that while AI can suggest interventions, it doesn't replace the need for professional medical advice. The technology is still being refined, and its effectiveness can vary across different populations.

In terms of diet, AI might suggest swapping high-glycemic foods for low-glycemic alternatives, or increasing portion control.

For exercise, it could recommend a frequency and intensity that suits your current fitness level, ensuring safety and adherence to a routine. Sleep advice could involve establishing a regular schedule and improving sleep quality.

These are cautious steps that should be discussed with a healthcare provider.

AI's role in prediabetes care is to assist, not dictate. It's a tool that can help identify risks and suggest lifestyle changes, but the final decisions and actions should always be taken in consultation with healthcare professionals.

The technology is promising, but it's not a silver bullet—it's part of a broader approach to managing prediabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical stage where lifestyle modifications can significantly impact the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding how AI can assist in this process is relevant for anyone looking to manage their prediabetes effectively.

Practical Takeaways

- AI can help personalize lifestyle changes for prediabetes, but it's not a replacement for professional medical advice.
- AI models use a wide range of data to provide a more comprehensive health assessment.
- Lifestyle changes suggested by AI should be discussed with healthcare providers before implementation.
- AI's role is to assist in managing prediabetes, not to dictate actions.
- The effectiveness of AI in prediabetes care is still being studied and can vary among populations.
