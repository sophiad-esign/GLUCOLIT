> 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does a media campaign increase the likelihood of at-risk adults enrolling in the National Diabetes Prevention Program?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Hawai'i residents aged 35-64 years who reported at least 1 diabetes risk factor, with an oversample of Native Hawaiian and Other Pacific Islander, and Filipino adults.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Exposure to the 'Beat Diabetes' media campaign.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between those exposed and not exposed to the campaign.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Self-reported likelihood of joining a lifestyle change program, campaign recall, demographic characteristics, diabetes risk factors, and beliefs affecting DPP enrollment likelihood.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exposed individuals had higher mean DPP enrollment likelihood ratings and higher inevitability belief scores than those not exposed. Perceived positive health impact of DPP participation was the strongest contributor to likelihood ratings.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's cross-sectional design limits causal inference, and it relies on self-reported data.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the media campaign showed promise in increasing the intention to enroll in diabetes prevention programs, especially among specific communities, it's important to consider the study's limitations and the need for different strategies for those already diagnosed with prediabetes.
  原文精华摘要
  糖尿病前期是血糖水平高于正常但尚未达到2型糖尿病诊断标准的警告信号。 这是一个改变生活方式以预防或延缓糖尿病发作的行动呼吁。 然而，许多处于风险中的人并没有意识到或没有参与预防计划。
  夏威夷的媒体运动与处于糖尿病风险中的成年人考虑加入预防计划的可能性更高有关，特别是在夏威夷原住民和其他太平洋岛民以及菲律宾社区中。
  该运动在提高对糖尿病预防计划的认识和参与意愿方面的成功是有希望的。 它表明，针对性的媒体运动可以成为公共卫生倡议中的有效工具。 然而，研究还表明，已经诊断出糖尿病前期的人由于运动曝光而没有更有可能加入，这表明需要为这个群体制定不同的策略。
  对于处于糖尿病风险中的人来说，意识是第一步。 考虑寻找当地的预防计划，并了解早期干预的好处。 健康科技公司可以与公共卫生运动合作，提供支持糖尿病预防工作的工具和资源，特别是在风险状况更高的社区中。
  了解媒体运动如何影响健康行为对于制定有效的公共卫生策略至关重要，特别是对于像糖尿病这样的慢性病。
- 媒体运动可以有效提高对糖尿病预防计划的认识和参与意愿。
- 研究强调了针对风险状况更高的特定社区的重要性。
- 对于已经诊断出糖尿病前期的人可能需要不同的策略。
- 公共卫生倡议可以从与健康科技公司的合作中受益，以支持预防工作。
  English Plain-Language Version
  Prediabetes is a warning sign that blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet high enough to be diagnosed as type 2 diabetes.

It's a call to action to make lifestyle changes to prevent or delay the onset of diabetes. However, many people at risk are not aware of or do not enroll in prevention programs.
The Hawai'i media campaign was linked to increased likelihood of at-risk adults considering enrolling in a prevention program, particularly among NHOPI and Filipino communities.
The campaign's success in raising awareness and intention to enroll is promising. It suggests targeted media campaigns can be effective in public health.

However, the study shows no increase in enrollment likelihood among those already diagnosed with prediabetes, indicating different strategies may be needed.
For at-risk individuals, awareness is key. They should seek local prevention programs and understand early intervention benefits.

Health tech companies can partner with public health campaigns to support diabetes prevention efforts, especially in high-risk communities.
Understanding how media campaigns can influence health behaviors is crucial for developing effective public health strategies, especially for chronic conditions like diabetes.

- Media campaigns can effectively increase awareness and intention to enroll in diabetes prevention programs.
- The study highlights the importance of targeting specific communities with higher risk profiles.
- Different strategies may be needed for those already diagnosed with prediabetes.
- Public health initiatives can benefit from partnerships with health tech companies to support prevention efforts.
