## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can liquiritigenin, a compound derived from licorice, improve insulin resistance and glucose metabolism in obesity-induced conditions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Obese mice induced by a high-fat diet, serving as a model for obesity and insulin resistance in humans.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Administration of liquiritigenin to the high-fat diet-induced obese mice.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of glucose tolerance, insulin sensitivity, and lipid metabolism between the mice treated with liquiritigenin and those without.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant improvements in glucose tolerance, insulin sensitivity, and lipid metabolism were observed in the mice treated with liquiritigenin.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Liquiritigenin reduced mitochondrial reactive oxygen species (mtROS), restored oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) function, and maintained mitochondrial homeostasis, thereby suppressing pro-inflammatory macrophage polarization and improving insulin signaling and glucose uptake in adipocytes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in an animal model, and while the results are promising, they may not directly translate to humans. Further research is needed to confirm these effects in clinical settings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the findings suggest that liquiritigenin may have potential benefits for managing insulin resistance, it is important to approach these results with caution. Incorporating foods rich in flavonoids, such as licorice, into a balanced diet could be beneficial, but readers should not overclaim the effects of liquiritigenin without further clinical evidence and should consult with healthcare providers before making significant dietary changes.
