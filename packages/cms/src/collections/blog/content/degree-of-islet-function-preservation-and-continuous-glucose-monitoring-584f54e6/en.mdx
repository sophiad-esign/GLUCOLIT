## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How is islet secretory function preserved after total pancreatectomy with islet autotransplantation, and how does this relate to glycemic control in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals undergoing total pancreatectomy with islet autotransplantation from 2014 to 2024, including those with chronic pancreatitis and a history of prediabetes or diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Total pancreatectomy with islet autotransplantation (TPIAT) and subsequent assessment of islet secretory function through a 2-hour liquid meal stimulation test.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of islet secretory function (C-peptide area under the curve) at baseline, 3 months post TPIAT, and annually post TPIAT.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Preservation of islet secretory function and its correlation with glycemic control measured by continuous glucose monitoring and HbA1c.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Nearly half of islet secretory function was retained at 3 months post TPIAT, and time in range was positively correlated with preserved islet function.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's limitations include a specific population undergoing TPIAT and may not be generalizable to all individuals with prediabetes. The study does not provide long-term data on islet function preservation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes undergoing TPIAT, maintaining a healthy diet and regular exercise may help preserve islet function and improve glycemic control, suggesting the importance of lifestyle adjustments in managing blood sugar levels.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up to a day where your blood sugar levels are stable, and you're not constantly worried about the next insulin shot.

For those with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, understanding how islet function ties into your diet and lifestyle is crucial.

Recent research shows that nearly half of islet secretory function can be retained after total pancreatectomy with islet autotransplantation (TPIAT), which can significantly impact how you manage your blood sugar levels.

This means that with the right dietary and lifestyle adjustments, you can still achieve good glycemic control. The study found that time in range, a measure of blood sugar stability, was positively correlated with preserved islet function.

This suggests that maintaining a healthy diet and regular exercise can help preserve what's left of your islet function and improve your overall health. It's not just about avoiding diabetes;

it's about living a healthier, more balanced life. The evidence shows that even with prediabetes or previous pancreatic surgery, islet autotransplantation can be beneficial.

This opens up a new conversation with your healthcare provider about the potential benefits and how to incorporate dietary and lifestyle changes to support your health.

Remember, every small step towards a healthier diet and regular exercise can make a big difference in preserving your islet function and managing your blood sugar levels.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between islet function and lifestyle is essential for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

This knowledge can guide dietary and exercise decisions that support blood sugar management and overall health.

Practical Takeaways

- Nearly half of islet secretory function can be retained after TPIAT, which can help manage blood sugar levels.
- Maintaining a healthy diet and regular exercise can support preserved islet function and overall health.
- The correlation between time in range and preserved islet function suggests the importance of lifestyle adjustments.
- Individuals with prediabetes or previous pancreatic surgery can still benefit from islet autotransplantation.
