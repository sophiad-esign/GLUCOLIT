## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do anthocyanins have cardiometabolic benefits in healthy participants?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Healthy participants from 18 cohorts and 65 randomized controlled trials.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Anthocyanin intake (≥50 mg/d).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of highest versus lowest habitual anthocyanin intake.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Cardiometabolic biomarkers and clinical endpoints.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Higher anthocyanin intake was associated with a reduced risk of cardiovascular disease, myocardial infarction, hypertension, and type 2 diabetes. Anthocyanins also improved flow-mediated dilation and insulin concentrations in trials.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study shows association but not causation. The benefits were seen with dietary intakes as low as 50 mg per day, which is achievable but may vary by individual.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes or early metabolic risk may consider incorporating anthocyanin-rich foods into their diet to potentially improve cardiometabolic health, but should discuss with a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

晚宴上的浆果沙拉，不仅增添风味，还可能助于抵抗胰岛素抵抗和代谢综合征。 《美国临床营养学杂志》发表的系统综述和元分析显示，多彩果蔬中的抗氧化剂花青素对心血管代谢健康有显著影响。

研究分析了18个队列和65个随机对照试验中的数据，发现较高花青素摄入量与降低心血管疾病等风险相关。 在试验中，花青素还改善了某些动脉粥样硬化和代谢综合征的生物标志物。

研究虽未确定因果关系，但强调了花青素摄入与心血管代谢健康之间的强烈联系。 每天低至50毫克的摄入量即可看到益处，这可以通过均衡饮食中富含水果和蔬菜来实现。

将花青素纳入饮食可能很简单，比如在早餐燕麦粥中加入一把浆果，或者用紫色土豆替换普通土豆。 这些小小的改变可以对你的健康产生巨大影响。

研究还特别指出，特定类型的花青素——氰化物和delphinidin，在改善健康标志物方面显示出特别的希望。 这表明富含这些特定花青素的饮食可能特别有益。

重要的是要记住，尽管花青素可能提供健康益处，但它们并非万能良药。 均衡饮食、规律运动和良好睡眠仍然是健康生活方式的关键组成部分。 然而，了解花青素的潜在益处可以帮助指导我们的食物选择，并支持我们的总体健康。

## English Plain-Language Version

A dinner party might feature a colorful berry salad, which could aid in combating insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome.

A systematic review and meta-analysis in The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition suggests anthocyanins, antioxidants in colorful produce, significantly impact cardiometabolic health.

Anthocyanins, found in fruits and vegetables like berries and red cabbage, were studied across 18 cohorts and 65 trials. The research indicates higher anthocyanin intake reduces the risk of cardiovascular disease and related conditions.

In trials, anthocyanins improved certain biomarkers of atherosclerosis and metabolic syndrome.

The study does not establish causation but highlights a strong link between anthocyanin intake and cardiometabolic health. Benefits were observed with intakes as low as 50 mg per day, achievable through a diet rich in fruits and vegetables.

Incorporating anthocyanins could involve simple dietary changes like adding berries to oatmeal or choosing purple potatoes over regular ones. These adjustments may significantly benefit health.

The study also notes that specific anthocyanins, cyanidin and delphinidin, particularly improved health markers, suggesting their rich diets could be especially beneficial.

While anthocyanins offer potential health benefits, they are not a cure-all. A balanced diet, regular exercise, and good sleep remain essential for a healthy lifestyle.

However, recognizing anthocyanins' benefits can guide food choices and support overall health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Cardiometabolic health is a critical concern for individuals with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic risk.

Understanding the potential benefits of dietary components like anthocyanins can help guide lifestyle changes that support overall health.

Practical Takeaways

- Anthocyanins in colorful fruits and vegetables may aid cardiometabolic health.
- Higher anthocyanin intakes correlate with reduced risks of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes.
- Simple dietary additions, like berries, can boost anthocyanin intake.
- Specific anthocyanins, cyanidin and delphinidin, show promise in improving health markers.
