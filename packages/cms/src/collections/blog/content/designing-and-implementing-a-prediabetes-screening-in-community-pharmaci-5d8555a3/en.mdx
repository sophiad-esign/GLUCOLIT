## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can community pharmacy-based prediabetes screening and online prevention programs effectively identify and manage prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults in community pharmacies in the United Arab Emirates.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Prediabetes screening in community pharmacies and referral to an online prevention program.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Standard care without pharmacy-based screening.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Identification of previously undetected prediabetes and diabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** 24.3% of participants were identified with previously undetected prediabetes, and 12.0% had HbA1c levels consistent with diabetes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's focus on a specific region may limit the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, community pharmacies may offer an accessible point of care for early detection and referral to prevention programs.

## English Plain-Language Version

### Research Background Diabetes is a significant health issue, and early detection of prediabetes can prevent or delay its progression to diabetes. Community pharmacies are underutilized for health promotion and early disease detection.

### Core Findings The study implemented a prediabetes screening service in pharmacies and an online prevention program. 24. 3% of participants were identified with previously undetected prediabetes.

### Your Interpretation and Critique The PREDICT study demonstrates the potential of pharmacies in early diabetes detection and prevention. However, the study's regional focus may limit broader applicability.

Cultural adaptation and pharmacist training are crucial for program success.

### Clinical/Business Insights Pharmacy-based screening can serve as an accessible entry point for early prediabetes detection and intervention.

Integrating digital health tools with community pharmacies offers innovative solutions for diabetes prevention.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant as it provides a replicable model for early detection and prevention of diabetes, a growing health concern worldwide.

### Practical Takeaways

- Community pharmacies can serve as a platform for early prediabetes detection.
- An online prevention program can be effectively integrated with pharmacy services.
- Cultural adaptation is key for the success of such health programs.
- Pharmacy-based screening can increase access to diabetes prevention interventions.
