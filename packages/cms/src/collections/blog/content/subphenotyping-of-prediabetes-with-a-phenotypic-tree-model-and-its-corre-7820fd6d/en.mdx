## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can stratifying prediabetes patients using the Scottish tree model identify their risks of developing diabetes and related outcomes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Subjects with prediabetes from the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study and the Chinese follow-up cohort, comprising 10,165 and 4,090 participants, respectively.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assignment of individuals to the Scottish tree model based on nine phenotypic variables, including HbA1c, BMI, blood lipids, liver enzyme, creatinine, and blood pressure.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Cox proportional hazards regression models to assess relationships between dimensions on the tree and future diabetes, renal and cardiovascular diseases, and death from all causes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Increased risk of progression to subclinical atherosclerosis, cardiovascular disease, microalbuminuria, and all-cause mortality in the upper-right branch of the tree; increased likelihood of progression to type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease in the right part of the tree.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The phenotypic tree model could identify individuals at higher risk of developing diabetes and related complications, potentially linked to elevated blood pressure, dyslipidemia, and body fat percentage.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings are based on two specific Chinese cohorts and may not be generalizable to all populations. The phenotypic tree model is a new approach and requires further validation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes can discuss the implications of this research with their healthcare providers to develop personalized dietary and lifestyle interventions that may reduce their risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes and related complications.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你站在一个十字路口，不确定哪条路能通向更好的健康。 许多糖尿病前期患者就处于这种需要明确饮食指导的境地。 最近来自两个大型中国队列的研究提供了关于特定饮食变化如何影响健康结果的见解。

该研究使用表型树模型，识别出具有某些代谢特征的个体，如高血压和体脂百分比高，发展为糖尿病及相关并发症的风险更大。 这表明，专注于降低这些风险因素的定制饮食可能是有益的。 例如，将高脂食品替换为瘦蛋白和增加纤维摄入量可以帮助管理血压和体脂。

控制份量和正念饮食也至关重要，因为暴饮暴食可能加剧胰岛素抵抗。 重要的是要记住，虽然饮食扮演着重要角色，但它只是拼图的一部分。 定期锻炼和良好的睡眠习惯同样重要。 与你的医疗服务提供者讨论这些发现，以制定适合你的生活方式和健康需求的个性化计划。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

了解糖尿病前期患者的饮食需求对于预防2型糖尿病的发病至关重要。 这项研究为如何针对高风险群体定制特定的饮食干预提供了一个框架，可能改善结果并降低医疗成本。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, unsure which path leads to better health. This is where many with prediabetes find themselves, needing clear dietary guidance.

Recent research from two large Chinese cohorts offers insights into how specific dietary changes can impact health outcomes.

The study, using a phenotypic tree model, identified that individuals with certain metabolic traits, like high blood pressure and body fat percentage, are at greater risk of developing diabetes and related complications.

This suggests that a tailored diet focusing on lowering these risk factors could be beneficial. For instance, swapping out high-fat foods for lean proteins and increasing fiber intake can help manage blood pressure and body fat.

Portion control and mindful eating are also crucial, as overeating can exacerbate insulin resistance. It's important to remember that while diet plays a significant role, it's just one piece of the puzzle.

Regular exercise and good sleep habits are equally important. Discuss these findings with your healthcare provider to develop a personalized plan that fits your lifestyle and health needs.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the dietary needs of individuals with prediabetes is crucial for preventing the onset of type 2 diabetes.

This research provides a framework for how specific dietary interventions can be tailored to high-risk groups, potentially improving outcomes and reducing healthcare costs.

Practical Takeaways

- A personalized diet plan can significantly impact the progression from prediabetes to diabetes.
- Swapping high-fat foods for lean proteins and increasing fiber intake can help manage blood pressure and body fat.
- Portion control and mindful eating are essential to prevent overeating, which can worsen insulin resistance.
- Discuss dietary changes with your healthcare provider to develop a plan that fits your lifestyle and health needs.
- Incorporate regular exercise and good sleep habits as part of a comprehensive approach to managing prediabetes.
