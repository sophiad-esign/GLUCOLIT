## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do pre-pregnancy and gestational high-fat diets affect maternal metabolism, placental function, and gut microbiota?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Female Sprague-Dawley rats
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** High-fat diet before and/or during pregnancy
- **对照 / Comparison:** Control diet (V) versus high-fat diet (H) in different combinations
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Maternal metabolic parameters, placental gene expression, oxidative stress, and gut microbiota composition
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Pre-pregnancy high-fat diet increased maternal body and liver weights and elevated placental oxidative stress. Gestational high-fat diet increased adiposity and impaired glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity. Placental nutrient transporters were mainly regulated by pre-pregnancy diet, while insulin-like growth factor 2 was influenced by gestational diet and its interaction with pre-pregnancy diet.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in rats, and findings may not directly translate to humans. The study did not report on long-term outcomes for the offspring.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, the timing and composition of diet before and during pregnancy may have distinct impacts on maternal metabolism and placental function, suggesting the importance of dietary management in these stages.

## 原文精华摘要

计划要孩子时，要考虑饮食，因为它可能影响未来孩子的健康。 研究发现，孕前高脂饮食可能导致体重和肝脏重量增加，以及胎盘氧化应激提高。 孕期高脂饮食可能增加体脂，损害葡萄糖耐受性和胰岛素敏感性。 这些发现强调了饮食时机对代谢健康的重要性。

该研究根据大鼠的饮食将它们分为孕前和孕期的组别，观察到孕前高脂饮食主要影响胎盘营养物质的运输，而孕期高脂饮食影响胰岛素敏感性和体脂。 与生长因子和代谢调节因子相关的基因表达也有所变化。

对于患有糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗的人来说，了解饮食时机的影响至关重要。 孕前减少高脂食物可能有助于管理体重和肝脏健康，而孕期均衡饮食可以改善胰岛素敏感性和葡萄糖耐受性。 研究还强调了对肠道健康的潜在影响，这会影响代谢健康。

这项研究对于计划怀孕的糖尿病前期或胰岛素抵抗个体很重要，因为它提供了关于饮食时机如何影响母婴健康的看法。

## English Plain-Language Version

Planning for a family involves considering diet, as it can affect future children's health. A study reveals that a high-fat diet before pregnancy can lead to increased body and liver weights and higher placental oxidative stress.

During pregnancy, such a diet can increase body fat and impair glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity. These findings underscore the importance of diet timing for metabolic health.

The study divided rats into groups based on diet before and during pregnancy, observing that pre-pregnancy high-fat diet mainly affected placental nutrient transport, while gestational high-fat diet impacted insulin sensitivity and body

fat. Changes in gene expression related to growth factors and metabolic regulators were also noted.

For those with prediabetes or insulin resistance, understanding the impact of diet timing is crucial.

Reducing high-fat foods before conception may help manage body weight and liver health, while a balanced diet during pregnancy could improve insulin sensitivity and glucose tolerance.

The study also highlights the potential impact on gut health, influencing metabolic health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is significant for individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance who are planning for pregnancy, as it provides insights into how diet timing can affect maternal and fetal health.

Practical Takeaways

- Maternal body and liver weights, along with placental oxidative stress, can be affected by a high-fat diet before pregnancy.
- Gestational high-fat diet can increase adiposity and impair glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity.
- The timing of a high-fat diet has distinct impacts on maternal metabolism and placental function.
- Discuss dietary changes with a healthcare provider to ensure they are safe and appropriate for individual health needs.
