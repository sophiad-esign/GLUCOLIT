## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Are body composition phenotypes defined by the Chinese Visceral Adiposity Index (CVAI) associated with prediabetes outcomes in Chinese adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 3,905 adults attending a health check-up center in 2024.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Classification into four body composition phenotypes based on CVAI and relative muscle mass.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of associations between phenotypes and prediabetes outcomes (impaired fasting glucose, elevated HbA1c levels, and composite prediabetes).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Odds ratios for prediabetes outcomes across different body composition phenotypes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The phenotype combining low muscle mass with high CVAI-defined visceral fat showed the strongest associations with prediabetes outcomes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Cross-sectional study design limits the ability to establish causation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests a link between certain body composition phenotypes and prediabetes, it does not prove causation, and further research is needed.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between body composition and prediabetes can help in developing targeted interventions to prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

### Practical Takeaways

- Prediabetes is a critical period for preventing type 2 diabetes.
- Body composition, including muscle mass and visceral fat, affects prediabetes risk.
- The CVAI provides a nuanced measure of obesity's metabolic impact.
- Further research is needed to understand the development of prediabetes over time.
