## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do glucose metabolism disorders contribute to intracranial and extracranial atherosclerotic stenosis?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with diabetes mellitus, insulin resistance, and impaired glucose tolerance.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Hyperglycemia and insulin resistance.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Correlation with atherosclerotic stenosis and stroke risk.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Positive correlation between HbA1c ≥ 6.5% and anterior circulation ICAS; positive correlation between TyG index and asymptomatic ECAS and ICAS.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Hyperglycemia and insulin resistance accelerate atherosclerosis through AGEs accumulation, oxidative stress, NLRP3 inflammasome activation, and VSMC phenotypic switching.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The review is narrative and does not include a systematic meta-analysis; key questions remain about differential effects and the impact of early intervention.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the review suggests a strong association between glucose metabolism disorders and atherosclerotic stenosis, it highlights the need for further research and emphasizes the potential benefits of early intervention in prediabetes for stroke prevention.
