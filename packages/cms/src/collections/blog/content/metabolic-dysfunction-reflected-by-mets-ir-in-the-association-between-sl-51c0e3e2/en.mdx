## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is there an association between sleep disorders and metabolic dysfunction, particularly in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** The study analyzed data from 4112 participants in the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2011-2020.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Self-reported physician-diagnosed sleep disorder status was the exposure.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Continuous metabolic score for insulin resistance (METS-IR) was used as the primary intermediate metabolic variable.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Tooth looseness without injury was the outcome.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Sleep disorder status was associated with higher odds of tooth looseness and higher continuous METS-IR levels. Continuous METS-IR was associated with tooth looseness.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a secondary analysis of cross-sectional survey data, limiting the ability to establish causality. The associations should be interpreted as statistical decompositions rather than evidence of causal mediation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests a link between sleep disorders and metabolic dysfunction, it does not prove causation. For individuals with prediabetes, discussing sleep issues with a healthcare provider could be beneficial for exploring potential interventions to improve sleep quality and metabolic health.
