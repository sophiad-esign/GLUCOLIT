## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the combined national impact of population-level sugar-sweetened beverage reduction and individual lifestyle interventions on preventing type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A nationally representative cohort of U.S. adults without diagnosed diabetes, constructed using data from the 2013-2018 National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Population-level sugar-sweetened beverage (SSB) reduction interventions and individual lifestyle intervention programs for adults with elevated glycemic risk (HbA1c 5.7%-6.5%).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Two scenarios: (a) a moderate strategy involving a 13% reduction in SSB consumption and an online lifestyle intervention; (b) an intensive strategy involving a 55% reduction in SSB consumption and an in-person lifestyle intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incident T2D cases and diabetes-related complications prevented over 10 years.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The moderate strategy was projected to prevent 3.7% (562,546) of new T2D cases over 10 years, while the intensive strategy would prevent 12.8% (1,958,164). Of the total cases prevented, 64%∼98% were attributable to the SSB reduction.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a simulation model and actual results may vary. It does not account for all possible variables that could influence the incidence of type 2 diabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals at risk of type 2 diabetes, reducing SSBs and engaging in lifestyle interventions could significantly reduce their risk. However, it's important to discuss these changes with a healthcare provider to set realistic and personalized goals.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你在家庭聚餐时，需要在含糖饮料和水之间做出选择。 最近的一项研究表明，选择水可能是预防2型糖尿病的一小步。 该研究模拟了减少含糖饮料（SSB）和个体生活方式改变对10年内2型糖尿病发病率的影响。 研究发现，包括减少13%的SSB和在线生活方式干预的温和策略可以预防3. 7%的新病例。

而包括减少55%的SSB和面对面生活方式干预的强化策略，可以预防高达12. 8%的新病例。 这突出了在糖尿病预防中群体层面变化和个体努力的重要性。

该研究使用国家健康和营养调查的数据，通过模拟模型评估了两种情况：一种是温和干预，另一种是更强化的措施。 结果显示，预防的相当一部分可以归因于减少SSB。 然而，需要注意的是，这是一个模型，实际结果可能会有所不同。

同样重要的是要记住，虽然减少SSB有益，但它只是预防2型糖尿病所需的全面生活方式改变的一部分。

在实际应用中，这意味着对于有2型糖尿病风险的人来说，做出饮食改变，比如选择水而不是苏打水，以及参与生活方式干预，可以显著降低他们的风险。 这不仅仅是关于剧烈的措施，而是关于做出小的、可持续的改变，这些改变可以累积成巨大的健康益处。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a family dinner, and you're trying to decide between a sugary drink or water. A recent study suggests that choosing water could be a small step towards preventing type 2 diabetes.

The study modeled the impact of reducing sugar-sweetened beverages (SSBs) and individual lifestyle changes on the incidence of type 2 diabetes over a 10-year period.

It found that a moderate strategy, including a 13% reduction in SSBs and an online lifestyle intervention, could prevent 3. 7% of new cases.

An intensive strategy, with a 55% reduction in SSBs and in-person lifestyle intervention, could prevent up to 12. 8% of new cases.

This highlights the importance of both population-level changes and individual efforts in diabetes prevention.

The study used a simulation model with data from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey. It evaluated two scenarios: one with moderate interventions and one with more intensive measures.

The results show that a significant portion of the prevention could be attributed to reducing SSBs. However, it's important to note that this is a model and actual results may vary.

It's also crucial to remember that while reducing SSBs is beneficial, it's just one part of a comprehensive lifestyle change needed to prevent type 2 diabetes.

In practical terms, this means that for someone at risk of type 2 diabetes, making dietary changes, such as choosing water over soda, and engaging in lifestyle interventions could significantly reduce their risk.

It's not about drastic measures but about making small, sustainable changes that can add up to big health benefits.

GLUCOLIT insight: Integrating these findings into daily life could mean setting realistic goals, like reducing SSBs gradually and finding enjoyable physical activities to incorporate into a routine.

It's about creating a sustainable plan that works for the individual, which can be discussed and adjusted with a healthcare provider.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Diabetes is a growing health concern, and understanding how lifestyle changes can help in prevention is crucial.

This study provides insights into the potential impact of diet and lifestyle modifications on a population level, which can guide individuals and communities in making healthier choices.

Practical Takeaways

- Reducing sugar-sweetened beverages can significantly contribute to preventing type 2 diabetes.
- Lifestyle interventions, when combined with population-level strategies, can have a substantial impact on diabetes prevention.
- It's important to make sustainable dietary and lifestyle changes to reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes.
- Discussing and planning with a healthcare provider can help in setting realistic goals for diabetes prevention.
