## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do serum advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) increase across the metabolic disease spectrum and can they serve as a biomarker for diabetes complications?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 226 adults across five groups: healthy controls, isolated hyperlipidaemia, impaired glucose tolerance, uncomplicated type 2 diabetes, and complicated type 2 diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Measurement of serum AGEs by ELISA.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of serum AGEs across different metabolic states.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Progressive increase in serum AGEs from prediabetes to complicated diabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Serum AGEs increased progressively across groups, with significant increases in uncomplicated and complicated diabetes compared to healthy controls.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is cross-sectional and does not establish causation. Demographic imbalances across groups are acknowledged as a limitation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Serum AGEs may serve as a complementary biomarker for complication risk stratification in type 2 diabetes, but further validation is needed.
