## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the specific dietary recommendations for South Asians with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** South Asians with obesity and prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Modification of dietary patterns
- **对照 / Comparison:** International dietary recommendations
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Weight loss, management of prediabetes
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A reduction of total energy intake by approximately 15% or 500 kcal/day, carbohydrate intake ranging from 49% to 65% of total energy, fat intake of 19-32%, protein intake of 15-19%, incorporation of pre-meal nut consumption, and achieving a target weight loss of 0.5-7%.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on a synthesis of interventional studies and guidelines specific to South Asians, which may not be generalizable to other populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, especially those of South Asian descent, understanding these specific dietary recommendations can help in making informed decisions about their diet to manage their condition effectively.

## 原文精华摘要

在家庭聚会上，面对传统南亚菜肴，管理前糖尿病似乎是个挑战。

然而，调整饮食习惯可以帮助保持健康，同时不牺牲文化美食。

由于独特的饮食习惯，南亚人需要量身定制的营养建议。

本文根据干预性研究和指南概述了具体饮食建议。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

前糖尿病是一个重要的健康问题，特别是在像南亚人这样更容易肥胖的人群中。

理解和实施特定的饮食变化可以帮助管理这一状况，并防止发展为2型糖尿病。

你可以带走的重点

- 对于南亚裔前糖尿病患者来说，调整饮食模式至关重要。
- 建议减少总能量摄入约15%或每天500千卡。
- 宏观营养素比例应根据南亚人的饮食习惯进行调整。
- 饭前摄入坚果可以帮助实现减重目标。
- 与医疗保健提供者讨论这些饮食调整，以个性化你的计划。

## English Plain-Language Version

At a family gathering, surrounded by traditional South Asian dishes, managing prediabetes might seem challenging.

However, adjusting dietary habits can help maintain health without sacrificing cultural cuisine.

South Asians require tailored nutritional advice due to unique dietary practices.

Drawing from interventional studies and guidelines, this article outlines specific dietary recommendations.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a significant health concern, especially in populations prone to obesity like South Asians.

Understanding and implementing specific dietary changes can help manage prediabetes and prevent progression to type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Modifying dietary patterns is key for South Asians with prediabetes.
- Reducing total energy intake by about 15% or 500 kcal/day is advised.
- Macronutrient ratios should align with South Asian dietary habits.
- Including nuts before meals can support weight loss goals.
- Consult a healthcare provider to personalize dietary plans.
