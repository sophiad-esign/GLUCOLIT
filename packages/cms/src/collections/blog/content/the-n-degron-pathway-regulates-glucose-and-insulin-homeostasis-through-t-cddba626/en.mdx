## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does the SQSTM1/p62 receptor affect glucose metabolism and insulin secretion in prediabetes and type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Prediabetic and type 2 diabetes models
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Activation of the SQSTM1/p62 receptor through the chemical compound ATB1002
- **对照 / Comparison:** RXRA degradation and its effects on insulin secretion and glucose metabolism
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Enhanced glucose-stimulated insulin secretion and insulin responsiveness
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** SQSTM1/p62 sequesters RXRA for lysosomal degradation, modulating glucose metabolism and insulin secretion. ATB1002, an SQSTM1 agonist, induced RXRA degradation, enhancing insulin secretion and responsiveness in prediabetic and type 2 diabetes models.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on prediabetic and type 2 diabetes models, and the findings may not directly translate to all individuals with prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the biological mechanisms are complex, the practical implication for individuals with prediabetes is to focus on lifestyle changes that can support insulin sensitivity, such as a balanced diet and regular exercise.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你在家庭聚餐中，试图控制你的血糖。 这不仅仅是避免甜点的问题； 而是在整个餐食中做出明智的选择。 最近的一项研究揭示了某些饮食干预如何帮助调节葡萄糖代谢和胰岛素分泌，这对于糖尿病前期的人来说至关重要。

研究发现SQSTM1/p62受体通过靶向RXRA进行降解，在调节葡萄糖代谢中发挥关键作用。 这一过程可以通过化学化合物ATB1002激活，它在糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病模型中增强了胰岛素分泌和反应性。

虽然这是一个复杂的生物学机制，但对你来说，要点很简单：专注于均衡饮食和定期运动，以支持你的胰岛素敏感性。 这不仅仅是关于剧烈变化，而是对你的日常生活习惯进行小的、可持续的调整。 例如，将白米饭换成糙米或在饮食中加入更多的全谷物可以有所作为。

定期的体育活动，即使只是一周几次，也可以帮助提高胰岛素敏感性。 记住，这些不是医疗处方，而是你可以与你的医疗服务提供者讨论的实用步骤，以有效管理你的糖尿病前期。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a family dinner, and you're trying to manage your blood sugar. It's not just about avoiding the dessert; it's about making smart choices throughout the meal.

A recent study sheds light on how certain dietary interventions can help regulate glucose metabolism and insulin secretion, which is crucial for those with prediabetes.

The study found that the SQSTM1/p62 receptor plays a key role in modulating glucose metabolism by targeting RXRA for degradation.

This process can be activated by a chemical compound, ATB1002, which enhances insulin secretion and responsiveness in prediabetic and type 2 diabetes models.

While this is a complex biological mechanism, the takeaway for you is simple: focus on a balanced diet and regular exercise to support your insulin sensitivity.

It's not about drastic changes but making small, sustainable adjustments to your daily routine. For example, swapping white rice for brown rice or incorporating more whole grains into your diet can make a difference.

Regular physical activity, even just a few times a week, can also help improve insulin sensitivity.

Remember, these are not medical prescriptions but practical steps you can discuss with your healthcare provider to manage your prediabetes effectively.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Managing prediabetes is crucial for preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding the role of specific biological pathways in glucose metabolism can guide practical lifestyle changes that support insulin sensitivity and overall health.

Practical Takeaways

- Balanced diet and regular exercise are key to managing prediabetes.
- Small, sustainable dietary changes can have a significant impact on insulin sensitivity.
- Physical activity a few times a week can improve glucose metabolism.
- Discuss practical lifestyle changes with your healthcare provider to manage prediabetes effectively.
- Stay informed about the latest research to make informed decisions about your health.
