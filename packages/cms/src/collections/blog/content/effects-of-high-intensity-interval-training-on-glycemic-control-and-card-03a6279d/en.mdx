## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the effects of high-intensity interval training (HIIT) on glycemic control and cardiometabolic risk factors in adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Structured high-intensity interval training (HIIT)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT) and non-exercise controls
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glycemic measures, cardiometabolic risk-related indicators
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** HIIT showed significant advantages for 2-hour plasma glucose and cardiorespiratory fitness compared to MICT, but the certainty of evidence was low to very low. No significant differences were found for body mass index.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Limited number of eligible studies, low certainty of evidence, short-term studies suggesting potential short-term physiological responses rather than sustained clinical adaptations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While HIIT may offer specific benefits for post-challenge glycemic control and cardiorespiratory fitness in prediabetes, the evidence is not definitive and requires further high-quality trials with longer intervention periods.

## English Plain-Language Version

先说结论 High-intensity interval training (HIIT) may offer specific benefits for prediabetes management, particularly in post-challenge glycemic control and cardiorespiratory fitness.

为什么值得关注 Prediabetes is a warning sign for type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease. Effective lifestyle changes, including exercise, are crucial for managing this condition.

证据告诉我们什么 The review found that HIIT could be more effective than moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT) for certain outcomes, such as 2-hour plasma glucose and cardiorespiratory fitness.

However, the certainty of this evidence is low.

应该怎样理解 While HIIT shows promise, the evidence is not definitive. The effects may be short-term, and more long-term studies are needed. It's important not to overclaim the benefits based on current evidence.

可以怎么做 For readers with prediabetes, consider incorporating HIIT into your exercise routine, but balance it with other types of exercise and lifestyle changes.

For the health tech industry, there's an opportunity to develop tools that help track and personalize HIIT programs.

给糖前读者 For readers with prediabetes, it's important to remember that HIIT is just one part of a broader lifestyle intervention.

给健康科技行业 For the health tech industry, developing personalized exercise tracking and guidance could be beneficial.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical phase that can escalate into type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease if not managed properly.

HIIT is an exercise modality that could offer a time-efficient and potentially effective approach to managing this condition.

Understanding its specific benefits and limitations is crucial for individuals with prediabetes and healthcare providers.

Practical Takeaways

- HIIT may be more favorable for post-challenge glycemic control and cardiorespiratory fitness in prediabetes.
- The certainty of evidence for glycemic outcomes is low, suggesting caution in interpreting benefits.
- HIIT effects may be short-term, indicating a need for longer-term studies.
- Incorporating HIIT into a broader lifestyle intervention is recommended for prediabetes management.
