## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do social determinants of health influence adherence to lifestyle recommendations among individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes participating in the PREDIPHONE trial, a nurse-led telephone intervention for lifestyle changes in glycaemic control.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Social determinants of health, including employment status, social class, and gender.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of adherence to lifestyle modifications across different social determinants.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Adherence measured using a composite index, analyzed as both a continuous and categorical variable.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Employment status was a significant factor, with unemployed or retired participants showing better adherence. Lower social class participants reported financial barriers to healthy eating and time constraints limiting physical activity. Women faced greater challenges due to caregiving responsibilities.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings are limited to the specific population and settings of the PREDIPHONE trial and may not be generalizable to all individuals with prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes should be aware of how their social circumstances might affect their ability to make lifestyle changes and consider discussing these factors with their healthcare providers to develop personalized strategies.

## English Plain-Language Version

Adopting a healthier lifestyle is challenging. Work can leave little time for cooking nutritious meals, and financial constraints might limit exercise options. These are common obstacles for those with prediabetes.

A study reveals how factors like employment status, social class, and gender affect lifestyle adherence. Unemployed or retired individuals often have more time for self-care.

Lower social class individuals face financial barriers to healthy eating and physical activity. Women, especially those with caregiving duties, encounter additional challenges.

These insights are crucial for tailoring interventions that consider individual circumstances.

For diet, consider replacing high-glycemic foods with whole grains and vegetables. For exercise, aim for moderate-intensity activities that fit your schedule and budget.

And for sleep, establish a regular routine that allows for 7-9 hours of quality rest each night. Remember, these are general suggestions; it's important to discuss your personal situation with a healthcare provider.

Managing prediabetes is not just about willpower—it's about overcoming life's obstacles.

Understanding the social factors that affect your ability to make changes can help you work with your healthcare team to find solutions that fit your unique needs.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the social determinants of health is crucial for individuals with prediabetes because it helps identify the specific barriers they face in adhering to lifestyle modifications, which are key to preventing the progression to

type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Social determinants like employment status and social class significantly impact lifestyle adherence in prediabetes.
- Tailored interventions that consider individual circumstances are essential for effective lifestyle changes.
- Practical steps include food swaps for better diet, moderate-intensity exercises, and establishing a regular sleep routine.
- Discuss personal challenges and needs with healthcare providers to find the most suitable lifestyle modifications.
