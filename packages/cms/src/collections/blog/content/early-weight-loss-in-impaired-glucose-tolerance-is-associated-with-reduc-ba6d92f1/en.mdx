## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is early weight loss in individuals with impaired glucose tolerance (IGT) associated with reduced long-term mortality and cardiovascular risk?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 540 adults with IGT from the Da Qing Diabetes Prevention Study
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Weight loss during the initial 6 years following IGT diagnosis
- **对照 / Comparison:** Non-weight loss, low weight loss (<5%), and high weight loss (≥5%)
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** All-cause mortality, cardiovascular disease (CVD) mortality, and CVD events
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Weight loss ≥5% was associated with significantly lower risks of CVD death, CVD events, hospitalized heart failure, and all-cause death.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study shows association but does not prove causation. Individual responses to weight loss can vary, and other factors may influence outcomes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, early weight management could be crucial for reducing long-term health risks, suggesting the importance of discussing weight loss strategies with healthcare providers.

这不仅仅是关于体重秤上的数字，而是关乎生活质量和寿命。

这项研究对540名糖耐量受损的成年人进行了长达34年的跟踪。 他们根据体重变化被分为三组：无减重、低减重（少于5%）和高减重（5%或以上）。 那些减重至少5%的人在心血管死亡、事件和全因死亡的风险上显著降低。 这表明，对于糖尿病前期患者来说，早期有效的体重管理至关重要。

实际步骤可以包括在饮食中加入更多全食物，找到你喜欢的体育活动，并设定现实的减重目标。 记住，目标不仅仅是减重，而是改善整体健康状况，降低发展更严重健康问题的风险。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you've just been diagnosed with prediabetes. The news can be daunting, but what if taking action early could significantly reduce your risk of serious health complications later in life? A recent study provides hope and guidance.

It shows that losing weight in the first few years after an impaired glucose tolerance diagnosis can lead to substantial health benefits, including lower risks of cardiovascular events and death.

This is not just about numbers on a scale, but about the quality and length of life.

The study followed 540 adults with IGT for 34 years. They were divided into three groups based on their weight change: non-weight loss, low weight loss (less than 5%), and high weight loss (5% or more).

Those who lost at least 5% of their body weight had significantly lower risks of cardiovascular death, events, and all-cause mortality. This suggests that early and effective weight management is crucial for individuals with prediabetes.

It's important to understand that while this study shows a strong association, it does not prove causation. There may be other factors at play, and individual responses to weight loss can vary.

However, the findings do suggest that discussing weight management strategies with a healthcare provider could be a wise step for those with prediabetes.

Practical steps can include incorporating more whole foods into your diet, finding physical activities you enjoy, and setting realistic weight loss goals.

Remember, the goal is not just to lose weight but to improve overall health and reduce the risk of developing更严重的 health issues.

In conclusion, the study's findings are a reminder that taking action in the early stages of prediabetes can have a profound impact on your future health.

It's never too early to start making changes that could lead to a longer, healthier life.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical stage where lifestyle changes can have a significant impact on long-term health outcomes.

Understanding the benefits of early weight loss can motivate individuals to take action and improve their health before conditions worsen.

Practical Takeaways

- Early weight loss after a prediabetes diagnosis can significantly reduce long-term health risks.
- Individuals with prediabetes should consider discussing weight management strategies with healthcare providers.
- Incorporating whole foods and enjoyable physical activities can aid in weight loss and health improvement.
- It's never too early to start making changes that could lead to a longer, healthier life.
