> 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is the CHG index associated with the risk of incident impaired fasting glucose in Chinese adults with normal fasting glucose?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 100,802 Chinese adults with normal baseline fasting plasma glucose
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Baseline cholesterol, high-density lipoprotein, and glucose (CHG) index
- **对照 / Comparison:** Incident impaired fasting glucose (IFG) cases
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Risk of developing IFG
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Each one-unit increase in the CHG index was associated with a significantly increased risk of incident IFG, with a nonlinear threshold relationship.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is observational and does not establish causation. The findings are specific to Chinese adults with normal fasting glucose.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with normal fasting glucose, a higher CHG index may indicate a higher risk of developing prediabetes, suggesting the need for monitoring and potential lifestyle interventions.

  English Plain-Language Version
  Diabetes is a growing health concern worldwide, and early identification of prediabetes is crucial for timely intervention.

Prediabetes, indicated by impaired fasting glucose (IFG), is a significant risk factor for developing diabetes.
The study indicates that a higher CHG index is significantly associated with an increased risk of developing IFG, showing a nonlinear threshold relationship.
The CHG index, integrating cholesterol, high-density lipoprotein, and glucose levels, offers a practical tool for early identification of prediabetes.

However, this study is observational and does not establish causation. The findings suggest a strong association, but individual health outcomes can vary.
Understanding the CHG index can help in managing prediabetes risk. Monitor your levels and discuss with healthcare providers about lifestyle changes.

The CHG index could be integrated into health apps and devices for early warning systems, aiding in the prevention of diabetes.
Understanding the CHG index is crucial for individuals at risk of prediabetes, as it can help in early detection and management, potentially preventing the progression to diabetes.

- The CHG index is a significant predictor of prediabetes risk.
- A higher CHG index is linked to an increased risk of IFG.
- The relationship between CHG index and IFG is nonlinear.
- Monitoring CHG index can aid in early identification of prediabetes.
