## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between the C-reactive protein-triglyceride glucose index (CTI) and unfavorable outcomes in acute ischemic stroke (AIS) patients, especially those with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1485 patients with AIS admitted to Seoul National University Hospital between 2010 and 2016.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** CTI levels calculated using the formula: 0.412 × ln (hs-CRP [mg/L]) + ln (TG [mg/dL] × FBG [mg/dL])/2.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of CTI levels with poor prognosis defined as a modified Rankin scale score ≥3 at 3 months.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Poor prognosis at 3 months post-AIS onset.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A significant positive linear association between CTI levels and the risk of unfavorable outcomes in AIS patients, with a stronger association in males and among individuals with prediabetes and diabetes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study did not include all types of stroke patients and relied on a specific hospital's patient population.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While CTI can signal risk, it's important to manage blood sugar and inflammation, and discuss these findings with a healthcare provider for personalized health advice.

高CTI水平与中风患者的高风险不良结果相关，特别是在男性和有糖尿病前期或糖尿病的人中。 这很重要，因为它可能帮助医生发现谁需要更多的照顾。 但请记住，CTI只是一个标记，不是命令。 它是健康拼图的一块。

该研究包括了1400多名中风患者。 他们发现，对于CTI的每一个单位增加，不良结果的风险都会上升。 这在男性和有血糖问题的人中更为明显。 这就像如果你的船的引擎和舵都工作不正常，你撞上岩石的机会就会增加。 但对于血糖正常的人来说，CTI似乎没那么重要。

这表明管理血糖和炎症可能是避免健康岩石的关键。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're in a boat on a river. The boat's direction and speed are like your health choices. A new study shows that a marker called CTI can help predict if your boat might hit rocks—bad outcomes after a stroke.

CTI combines measures of inflammation and blood sugar, which are like the rudder and engine of your health boat.

High CTI levels were linked to a higher risk of poor outcomes in stroke patients, especially men and those with prediabetes or diabetes. This is important because it might help doctors spot who needs more care.

But remember, CTI is just a marker, not a command. It's one piece of the health puzzle.

The study included over 1400 stroke patients. They found that for every unit increase in CTI, the risk of poor outcomes went up. This was more pronounced in men and in those with血糖 problems.

It's like if your boat's engine and rudder were both not working well, your chances of hitting rocks increase. But for those with normal blood sugar, CTI didn't seem to matter as much.

This suggests that managing blood sugar and inflammation might be key to avoiding those health rocks.

It's important to note that while CTI can signal risk, it doesn't cause the outcomes. It's an association, like seeing more rain when there are more clouds, but clouds don't make the rain.

The study also had limits, like not including all types of stroke patients. So, while CTI might be a useful sign, it's not the only factor.

For those with prediabetes, this means working on a healthy diet and regular exercise could be more important than ever. It's about keeping your health boat steady and avoiding the rocks of poor health outcomes.

Discuss these findings with your doctor to understand how they apply to you.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding CTI's role is crucial for prediabetes and diabetes patients because it can signal potential health risks related to stroke outcomes.

This knowledge can guide lifestyle changes and medical interventions to improve health outcomes.

Practical Takeaways

- CTI can help predict poor outcomes in stroke patients, especially those with prediabetes or diabetes.
- High CTI levels were linked to a higher risk of poor outcomes, particularly in men.
- Managing blood sugar and inflammation might be key to better stroke recovery.
- CTI is an association, not a cause, so it's important not to overclaim its impact.
- Discuss these findings with your healthcare provider to understand your personal health risks.
