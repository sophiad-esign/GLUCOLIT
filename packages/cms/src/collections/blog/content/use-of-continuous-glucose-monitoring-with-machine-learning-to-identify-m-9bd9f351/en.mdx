- **研究问题 / Research question:** How can continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) and machine learning be used to identify metabolic subphenotypes and inform precision lifestyle changes in prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Use of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) and machine learning models
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison to traditional static glucose threshold methods
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prediction of muscle insulin resistance and beta-cell function, identification of metabolic subphenotypes based on postprandial glycemic responses
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** CGM combined with machine learning can predict muscle insulin resistance and beta-cell function. Individual postprandial glycemic responses to meals can serve as biomarkers for metabolic subtypes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a review of existing evidence, and practical application in real-world settings may vary. The technology's accessibility and cost could also be limiting factors.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** This approach offers a more personalized view of metabolic health by leveraging CGM data, potentially leading to more effective lifestyle interventions tailored to an individual's core metabolic defects.

  利用CGM数据提供代谢健康的个性化视角。 它超越了一般的血糖控制，针对特定的代谢缺陷，这可能导致更有效的生活方式干预。 然而，技术的可及性和成本也可能是限制因素。
