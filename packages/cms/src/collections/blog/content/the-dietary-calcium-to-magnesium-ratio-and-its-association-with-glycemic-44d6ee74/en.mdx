## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between the dietary calcium-to-magnesium ratio and glycemic control in individuals with type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), aged 19 to 59 years, from Primary Health Care services in Sergipe, Brazil.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of dietary calcium and magnesium intake and calculation of the calcium-to-magnesium ratio.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Participants were stratified according to dietary calcium-to-magnesium ratio (≤ 2 and > 2).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), insulin sensitivity (HOMA-%S), and β-cell function (HOMA-%B).
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A higher dietary calcium-to-magnesium ratio was associated with lower insulin sensitivity and higher insulin resistance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is cross-sectional, limiting the ability to establish causation. The sample size and population may not be representative of all individuals with T2DM.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study suggests a link between the dietary calcium-to-magnesium ratio and insulin resistance in individuals with T2DM, it does not provide definitive dietary advice. Readers should consider this information as part of a broader discussion with their healthcare provider about managing diabetes through diet.
