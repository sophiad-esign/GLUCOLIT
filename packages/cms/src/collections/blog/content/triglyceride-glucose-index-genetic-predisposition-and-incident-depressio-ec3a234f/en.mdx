## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is the triglyceride-glucose (TyG) index associated with the risk of developing depression, independent of genetic predisposition?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A total of 335,586 UK Biobank participants without baseline depression.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** The TyG index, calculated from fasting blood levels of triglycerides and glucose, as a marker for insulin resistance.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of incident depression rates across different quartiles of the TyG index.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incident depression cases identified through linked electronic health records.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A higher TyG index was associated with an increased risk of incident depression, independent of genetic predisposition. The fully adjusted hazard ratios for the second, third, and fourth quartiles compared to the lowest were 1.051, 1.078, and 1.144, respectively.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study did not have a preregistered protocol, which could introduce potential biases. Additionally, while the study adjusted for many factors, there may be residual confounding from unmeasured variables.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, managing insulin resistance through lifestyle factors, including ensuring quality sleep, may reduce the risk of developing depression. However, the relationship is complex, and genetic factors also play a role, indicating the need for a personalized approach to health management.

## 原文精华摘要

睡眠问题可能不仅仅是辗转反侧——它们可能预示着更高的抑郁风险，特别是对于糖尿病前期的人来说。 TyG指数反映了胰岛素抵抗，与睡眠恶化和抑郁风险增加有关。 这项英国生物银行的研究强调了这种联系。

TyG指数是根据空腹血液中的甘油三酯和葡萄糖水平计算的，表明胰岛素抵抗。 这项研究包括了超过300,000名参与者，发现TyG指数越高，发展抑郁的风险也越高，与遗传倾向无关。

生活方式的改变，如定期锻炼、均衡饮食和确保充足的睡眠，可以降低TyG指数，可能减少抑郁风险。 优质睡眠对精神健康至关重要，管理胰岛素抵抗是身体健康和精神福祉的关键。 虽然基因无法改变，但可以通过影响生活方式来控制健康。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

这项研究之所以重要，是因为它强调了睡眠质量在糖尿病前期管理中的重要性以及对精神健康的广泛影响，表明需要采取更全面的健康管理策略。

## English Plain-Language Version

Sleep troubles may signal more than just restlessness—they could indicate a higher risk of depression, especially for those with prediabetes.

The TyG index, which reflects insulin resistance, has been linked to worsening sleep and an increased risk of depression. This UK Biobank study underscores this association.

The TyG index is calculated from fasting blood levels of triglycerides and glucose, indicating insulin resistance.

Over 300,000 participants were included in the study, which found a correlation between a higher TyG index and the risk of developing depression, irrespective of genetic predisposition.

Lifestyle changes, such as regular exercise, balanced diet, and ensuring adequate sleep, can lower the TyG index and potentially reduce the risk of depression.

Quality sleep is essential for mental health, and managing insulin resistance is key to both physical and mental well-being. While genes cannot be changed, lifestyle can be influenced to take control of health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is significant as it highlights the importance of sleep quality in prediabetes management and the broader implications for mental health, suggesting a more comprehensive approach to health strategies.

Practical Takeaways

- The TyG index, indicating insulin resistance, is associated with an increased risk of depression.
- Lifestyle factors, including sleep, diet, and exercise, can influence the TyG index.
- Good sleep quality is important for mental health.
- Managing insulin resistance is crucial for both physical and mental well-being.
- Individuals with prediabetes should consider their lifestyle habits as part of a health management plan.
