## 原文精华摘要

工作了一整天后感到疲惫，你决定再次跳过健身房。 但如果运动能帮助你对抗的不仅仅是疲劳呢？ 最新研究表明，体育活动，加上均衡饮食，可以显著影响你发展为糖尿病前期和某些类型癌症的风险。

糖尿病前期是一个警告信号，表明血糖水平高于正常但尚未达到糖尿病范围。 这是一个采取行动改变生活方式的呼吁。 特别是，运动可以提高胰岛素敏感性，减少发展为2型糖尿病的风险。

证据显示，将连续葡萄糖监测（CGM）整合到护理中可以增强代谢控制。 这很关键，因为血糖控制不良与更糟糕的肿瘤学结果和降低治疗依从性有关。

虽然确切的机制很复杂，但很明显，慢性高血糖和胰岛素抵抗会促进肿瘤发展。 运动可以通过改善葡萄糖代谢和减少胰岛素抵抗来提供帮助。

需要注意的是，虽然运动有益，但它不是单独的解决方案。 均衡饮食、控制食量和定期检查也是必不可少的。 目标是与你的医疗保健提供者合作，制定一个适合你的生活方式和健康需求的个性化计划。

总之，对你的日常例程进行小而持续的改变可以对你的长期健康产生重大影响。 无论是晚餐后的快走还是每周的健身房课程，每一次运动都很重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Feeling tired after a long day at work, you decide to skip the gym again. But what if exercise could help you fight more than just fatigue?

Recent research suggests that physical activity, along with a balanced diet, can significantly impact your risk of developing prediabetes and certain types of cancer.

Prediabetes is a warning sign that blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet in the diabetes range. It's a call to action to make lifestyle changes.

Exercise, in particular, can improve insulin sensitivity and reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.

The evidence shows that integrating continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) into care can enhance metabolic control. This is crucial because poor glycemic control is associated with worse oncologic outcomes and reduced treatment adherence.

While the exact mechanisms are complex, it's clear that chronic hyperglycemia and insulin resistance promote tumor development. Exercise can help by improving glucose metabolism and reducing insulin resistance.

It's important to note that while exercise is beneficial, it's not a standalone solution. A balanced diet, portion control, and regular check-ups are also essential.

The goal is to work with your healthcare provider to develop a personalized plan that fits your lifestyle and health needs.

In summary, making small, consistent changes to your daily routine can have a big impact on your long-term health. Whether it's a brisk walk after dinner or a weekly gym session, every bit of movement counts.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise and diet are crucial for managing prediabetes and cancer risk because they directly impact insulin sensitivity and glucose control, which are key factors in disease progression.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular physical activity can help manage prediabetes and reduce cancer risk.
- Exercise improves insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism.
- Poor glycemic control is linked to worse cancer outcomes.
- A balanced diet and regular check-ups are essential components of a healthy lifestyle.
- Small, consistent changes to your daily routine can have a significant impact on your health.
