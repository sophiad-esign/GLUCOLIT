## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do weight loss and aerobic exercise affect insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism in older adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Sixty-seven adults aged 60.75 ± 7.58 with a BMI between 25 and 50 kg/m2, nonsmokers, not on insulin or hypoglycemic agents, and sedentary.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A subset of participants completed a six-month weight loss program or a supervised aerobic exercise intervention three times per week.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Baseline measurements and post-intervention measurements of insulin sensitivity, glucose metabolism, and skeletal muscle CNTF expression.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Weight loss reduced body weight, fat mass, glucose AUC, insulin AUC, and basal muscle CNTF expression. Aerobic exercise increased VO2max and reduced fat mass without altering body weight, glucose AUC, insulin AUC, or basal CNTF.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Both weight loss and aerobic exercise improved insulin sensitivity, with changes in muscle CNTF associated with improvements in glucose disposal.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on a specific population of older adults and may not be generalizable to all individuals with prediabetes. The study duration was six months, which may not capture long-term effects.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, engaging in regular weight loss efforts and aerobic exercise can lead to improvements in insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism, which are key in managing prediabetes and preventing the onset of type 2 diabetes.

糖尿病前期可能导致疲劳，影响能量水平。 规律运动和均衡饮食可以增强胰岛素敏感性。 一项研究观察了糖尿病前期的老年人进行减重和有氧运动，发现这两种方法都减少了胰岛素抵抗，这是管理糖尿病前期的一个关键因素。

有氧运动增加胰岛素敏感性，帮助身体控制血糖水平。 减重也减少了胰岛素抵抗，改善了身体对胰岛素的反应。 小步骤，如在饮食中增加全谷物或每日散步，可以产生显著差异。 持续性是长期成功的关键。

研究还发现，胰岛素敏感性的改善与肌肉CNTF的变化有关，这为我们提供了关于身体如何响应运动和饮食变化的见解。

A study observed older adults with prediabetes undergoing weight loss and aerobic exercise, finding both methods reduced insulin resistance, a key factor in managing prediabetes.

Small steps, like adding whole grains to the diet or daily walking, can make a significant difference. Consistency is key for long-term success.

The study also found that improvements in insulin sensitivity were associated with changes in muscle CNTF, offering insights into how the body responds to exercise and diet changes.
