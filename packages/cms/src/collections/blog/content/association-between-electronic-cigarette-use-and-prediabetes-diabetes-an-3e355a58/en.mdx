## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between e-cigarette use and metabolic health outcomes such as prediabetes, diabetes, and insulin resistance?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adult populations from studies included in the systematic review and meta-analysis.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** E-cigarette use, including current exclusive e-cigarette users, dual users, and former e-cigarette users.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Never users of e-cigarettes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prediabetes, diabetes, and insulin resistance.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Current exclusive e-cigarette users had a 34% higher odds of prediabetes, dual users had a 26% higher odds, and former users had a 13% increase. For diabetes, only dual users showed a significant elevation. Current exclusive e-cigarette users had a 37% higher odds of insulin resistance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study found associations but could not establish causation. The evidence is based on observational studies, which are subject to various biases and cannot prove that e-cigarette use causes these conditions.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While there is an observed association, readers should not overclaim causation. It is important to consider these findings in the context of personal health decisions and to seek professional medical advice for personalized risk assessment and management.

## 原文精华摘要

家庭聚会上，有人提到为了戒烟而改抽电子烟。

你可能会关心这是否对健康有益，尤其是在糖尿病风险方面。

最近一项系统综述和元分析研究了电子烟使用与糖尿病前期、糖尿病和胰岛素抵抗之间的关系。

研究汇总了超过280万参与者的数据，涵盖了十项研究。

对于糖尿病前期，仅使用电子烟的用户和双重使用者（即同时使用电子烟和传统香烟的人）比从未使用过的用户有更高的风险。

前电子烟使用者也显示出风险增加。

然而，研究并未证明因果关系，表明需要更多的研究。

如果你对糖尿病风险有担忧，最好与医疗保健提供者讨论，以获得个性化的建议。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

随着电子烟的普及，了解其对健康的影响至关重要，尤其是对于那些关心代谢健康和糖尿病风险的人来说。

你可以带走的重点

- 电子烟的使用可能与糖尿病前期和胰岛素抵抗风险增加有关。
- 电子烟和传统香烟的双重使用可能会增加糖尿病的风险。
- 需要更多的研究来理解电子烟使用和代谢健康之间的因果关系。
- 与医疗保健提供者讨论任何吸烟习惯的变化对于获得个性化建议非常重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

At a family gathering, someone mentions switching to e-cigarettes to quit smoking. You might wonder about the health implications, especially regarding diabetes risk.

A systematic review and meta-analysis investigated this, focusing on the link between e-cigarette use and conditions like prediabetes and insulin resistance. The study included data from over 2. 8 million participants across ten studies.

For prediabetes, exclusive e-cigarette users had higher odds compared to never users, as did dual users. Former users also showed an increase. However, causation was not proven, indicating more research is needed.

It's important to discuss any concerns about diabetes risk with a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the health implications of e-cigarette use is crucial as their popularity grows, especially among those concerned about metabolic health and diabetes risk.

Practical Takeaways

- E-cigarette use may be associated with an increased risk of prediabetes and insulin resistance.
- Dual use of e-cigarettes and traditional cigarettes may increase the risk of diabetes.
- More research is needed to understand the causal relationship between e-cigarette use and metabolic health.
- It's important to discuss any changes in smoking habits with a healthcare provider for personalized advice.
