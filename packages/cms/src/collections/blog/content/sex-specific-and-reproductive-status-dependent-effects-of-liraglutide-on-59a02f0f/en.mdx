## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does liraglutide affect metabolic disorders in prediabetes differently based on sex and reproductive status?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Male, female, and ovariectomized female hereditary hypertriglyceridemic (HHTg) rats as a prediabetic model.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Liraglutide administration at 0.2 mg/kg/day subcutaneously for 8 weeks.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of metabolic effects between male, female, and ovariectomized female rats.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glucose tolerance, lipid metabolism, visceral adiposity, and hepatic triacylglycerol (TAG) accumulation.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Liraglutide improved glucose tolerance in all HHTg rats. Female and ovariectomized female rats showed a stronger effect on lipid metabolism and visceral adiposity than males. No changes in hepatic TAG accumulation were observed in males.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in rats, which may limit direct extrapolation to humans. The study duration was 8 weeks, which may not capture long-term effects.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While liraglutide improved glucose tolerance in all rats with prediabetes, its effects on lipid metabolism and fat accumulation were more pronounced in female and ovariectomized rats. This suggests that the medication's impact may vary by sex and reproductive status, which could influence treatment strategies in humans.
