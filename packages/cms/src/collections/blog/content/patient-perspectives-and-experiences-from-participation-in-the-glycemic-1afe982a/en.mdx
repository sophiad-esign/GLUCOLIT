## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does the Glycemic Excursion Minimization/Glucose Everyday Matters (GEM) intervention impact the perspectives and experiences of individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Participants from a larger clinical trial who completed the GEM intervention for prediabetes (n = 16).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Self-guided intervention with a guide paired with a continuous glucose monitor and activity monitor.
- **对照 / Comparison:** N/A
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Increased knowledge on the relationship between lifestyle choices and glucose excursions, positive insight on the GEM intervention and its components.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Analysis of 16 interviews revealed five major themes highlighting positive outcomes in health status, disease insight, and support experienced during the GEM trial.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is qualitative and based on a small sample size, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the GEM intervention shows promise in empowering individuals with prediabetes to understand and manage their blood glucose levels, further research with larger and diverse populations is needed to confirm these findings and refine the intervention for broader use.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，醒来就知道你的早餐将如何影响你的血糖水平。 这就是GEM干预的力量，它将指南与持续葡萄糖监测配对，帮助糖尿病前期患者做出明智的生活方式选择。 这项研究与16名参与者一起进行，揭示了通过GEM，个体获得了更深入的理解，了解他们的日常选择如何影响他们的健康。

他们学会了哪些食物会导致血糖飙升，哪些活动有助于稳定他们的血糖水平。 这些知识不仅改善了他们的健康状况，还为他们提供了控制糖尿病前期的工具。 GEM干预不仅仅是屏幕上的数字； 它是关于理解生活方式与健康之间的联系。 它是关于做出小的、可持续的改变，这些改变可以导致生活质量的显著提高。

这项研究的反馈将在完善GEM干预中发挥宝贵作用，确保它满足糖尿病前期人群的需求，并帮助他们踏上更健康的道路。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病前期是一个影响数百万美国人的关键健康问题，许多人每年都会发展成2型糖尿病。 GEM干预提供了一种积极的生活方式管理方法，赋予个体控制自己健康的能力，可能延迟或预防2型糖尿病的发作。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up and knowing exactly how your breakfast will affect your blood sugar levels.

This is the power of the GEM intervention, which pairs a guide with continuous glucose monitoring to help prediabetes patients make informed lifestyle choices.

The study, conducted with 16 participants, revealed that through GEM, individuals gained a deeper understanding of how their daily choices impact their health.

They learned which foods cause血糖 spikes and which activities help stabilize their glucose levels. This knowledge not only improved their health status but also provided them with the tools to take control of their prediabetes.

The GEM intervention is not just about numbers on a screen; it's about understanding the connection between lifestyle and health.

It's about making small, sustainable changes that can lead to significant improvements in one's quality of life.

The feedback from this study will be invaluable in refining the GEM intervention, ensuring it meets the needs of the prediabetes population and helps them on their journey to better health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical health issue affecting millions of Americans, with many progressing to type 2 diabetes annually.

The GEM intervention offers a proactive approach to lifestyle management, empowering individuals to take control of their health and potentially delay or prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Understanding the impact of daily choices on blood glucose levels is crucial for managing prediabetes.
- The GEM intervention provides individuals with the tools to make informed decisions about their diet and activity.
- Small, sustainable lifestyle changes can lead to significant health improvements.
- Feedback from participants will help refine the GEM intervention for broader adoption.
- Empowerment through knowledge is key to managing prediabetes and preventing the onset of type 2 diabetes.
