- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle modifications: Mediterranean and plant-based dietary patterns, regular exercise, and maintaining a regular sleep schedule.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads in life where your body is going through significant changes.

This is the reality for many women during the climacteric transition, a stage marked by hormonal shifts that can affect metabolism and cardiovascular health. Diet, exercise, and sleep are the cornerstones of managing these changes.

A balanced diet rich in Mediterranean and plant-based foods can help improve metabolic health. Opt for meals with plenty of vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins, while limiting processed foods and added sugars.

Regular exercise, even just 30 minutes a day, can make a significant difference in body composition and cardiometabolic health. Choose activities you enjoy to ensure consistency. Good sleep is also crucial;

aim for 7-9 hours per night and establish a regular sleep schedule.

The evidence shows that these lifestyle changes can help mitigate the increased risk of cardiometabolic issues during the climacteric transition.

However, it's important to remember that every woman's experience is unique, and what works for one may not work for another. Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new health regimen.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

The climacteric transition is a critical period for women's health, where lifestyle modifications can significantly impact cardiometabolic health.

Understanding the importance of diet, exercise, and sleep during this time is crucial for managing the increased risk of metabolic syndrome and cardiovascular diseases.

Practical Takeaways

- A balanced diet with Mediterranean and plant-based foods can improve metabolic health.
- Regular exercise, even just 30 minutes a day, can significantly impact body composition and cardiometabolic health.
- Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night and maintain a regular sleep schedule.
- Consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new health regimen.
- Integrating technology, such as wearables, can provide personalized feedback and support adherence to lifestyle changes.
