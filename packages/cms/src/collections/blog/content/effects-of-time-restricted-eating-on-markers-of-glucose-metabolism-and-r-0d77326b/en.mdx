## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the effects of time-restricted eating on glucose metabolism and regulation in individuals with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with prediabetes (fasting blood glucose of 5.6-6.9 mmol/l or HbA1c of 39-47 mmol/mol [5.7-6.4%]) or type 2 diabetes (fasting blood glucose ≥7 mmol/l or HbA1c ≥48 mmol/mol [6.5%]).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Time-restricted eating (TRE) with a ≤12-hour eating window.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Non-time-restricted control diet.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Markers of glucose metabolism and regulation, including HbA1c and fasting blood glucose.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** TRE showed no overall effect on HbA1c but reduced fasting blood glucose. In subgroups, TRE improved HbA1c and fasting glucose in individuals with prediabetes and improved fasting glucose in individuals with type 2 diabetes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Evidence was limited by imprecision due to wide confidence intervals in some studies, possibly due to small sample sizes. The effects of TRE on insulin sensitivity, beta cell function, and continuous glucose monitoring measurements were inconclusive.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes, TRE may be a useful dietary strategy to discuss with a healthcare provider, particularly for managing fasting blood glucose. However, more research is needed to confirm these findings and explore long-term effects.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你正在管理一个繁忙的时间表，需要在不打乱日常的情况下安排餐食。 限时饮食（TRE）可能是解决方案。 TRE涉及将食物摄入限制在特定的每日窗口内，可能改善血糖控制。 对于糖尿病前期患者来说，TRE可以降低HbA1c水平和空腹血糖，这是糖尿病管理的关键指标。

虽然对HbA1c的整体影响并不显著，但在特定亚组中，特别是在糖尿病前期患者中，TRE显示出了希望。 这不仅仅是关于你吃什么，而是关于你什么时候吃。 这种方法可以成为你的糖尿病管理计划的实用补充，与药物和其他生活方式改变相辅相成。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

对于糖尿病前期或2型糖尿病患者来说，管理血糖至关重要，以防止疾病进展。 TRE提供了一种非药物方法，可以融入日常生活，可能在不需要药物的情况下改善结果。

你可以带走的重点

- 限时饮食（TRE）可能有助于管理糖尿病前期和2型糖尿病患者的血糖水平。
- TRE涉及将食物摄入限制在特定的每日窗口内，这对于繁忙的时间表来说可能是实用的。
- 在糖尿病前期，TRE可以降低HbA1c和空腹血糖，这是糖尿病管理的重要指标。
- 需要更多的研究来了解TRE对糖尿病预防和治疗的长期影响。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're managing a busy schedule and need to fit in meals without disrupting your day. Time-restricted eating (TRE) might be the solution.

TRE involves limiting food intake to a specific daily window, potentially improving glucose control. For those with prediabetes, TRE could lower HbA1c levels and fasting blood glucose, which are key markers for diabetes management.

While the overall effect on HbA1c wasn't significant, TRE showed promise in specific subgroups, particularly for those with prediabetes. It's not just about what you eat, but when you eat it.

This approach could be a practical addition to your diabetes management plan, complementing medication and other lifestyle changes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Managing blood sugar is crucial for individuals with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes to prevent disease progression.

TRE offers a non-pharmacological approach that could be integrated into daily life, potentially improving outcomes without the need for medication.

Practical Takeaways

- Time-restricted eating (TRE) may help manage glucose levels in prediabetes and type 2 diabetes.
- TRE involves limiting food intake to a specific daily window, which could be practical for busy schedules.
- In prediabetes, TRE can reduce HbA1c and fasting blood glucose, important markers for diabetes management.
- More research is needed to understand the long-term effects of TRE on diabetes prevention and treatment.
