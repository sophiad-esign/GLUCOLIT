## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can a digital-enhanced multiple-behavior self-monitoring intervention improve weight and glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 45 adults with type 2 diabetes enrolled from certified diabetes care and support programs.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Participants logged diet, activity, blood glucose, and weight using smartphone apps or paper diaries and received a brief lifestyle intervention adapted from Look AHEAD.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Exploratory descriptive comparisons between smartphone and paper diary methods.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Weight loss and A1C levels at 3 months, feasibility assessed through retention and qualitative interviews.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Thirty-one participants completed the study, showing modest within-participant weight loss and maintained glycemic control with stable A1C levels.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was a 3-month mixed-method implementation study with a small sample size, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, implementing a self-monitoring intervention, either digitally or on paper, can be a feasible and acceptable approach to managing weight and glycemic control.

## 原文精华摘要

管理糖尿病需要跟踪饮食、活动和血糖水平。 辅助此过程的工具可以帮助适度减轻体重并稳定血糖水平。

在一项研究中，45名2型糖尿病患者记录了他们的饮食、活动、血糖和体重。 他们接受了简短的生活方式干预，三个月后显示出小幅但显著的体重减轻和稳定的A1C水平。

无论是通过智能手机应用程序还是纸质日记，持续的健康数据跟踪是有效管理糖尿病的关键。

饮食监测可以带来更好的食物选择和控制食量。 规律的锻炼和充足的睡眠对整体健康也至关重要。

研究发现，这种干预措施是可以接受和有用的，这对于长期坚持任何健康方案至关重要。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病管理需要持续监测和生活方式调整。 这项研究提供了数字工具如何支持这一过程的见解，对患有糖尿病前期或早期代谢风险的人有益。

## English Plain-Language Version

Managing diabetes involves tracking diet, activity, and blood glucose levels. A tool that aids in this can lead to modest weight loss and stable blood sugar levels.

In a study, 45 adults with type 2 diabetes logged their diet, activity, blood glucose, and weight. They received a brief lifestyle intervention, showing small but significant weight loss and stable A1C levels after three months.

Consistency in health data tracking, whether through a smartphone app or paper diary, is key to managing diabetes effectively.

Diet monitoring can lead to better food choices and portion control. Regular exercise and adequate sleep are also crucial for overall health.

The study found the intervention acceptable and useful, which is vital for long-term adherence to any health regimen.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Diabetes management requires ongoing monitoring and lifestyle adjustments. This study offers insights into how digital tools can support this process, benefiting those with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Practical Takeaways

- A self-monitoring tool can assist in managing diabetes by tracking diet, activity, and blood glucose levels.
- Consistency in monitoring is essential for effective diabetes management.
- Small, consistent changes in lifestyle can lead to significant health improvements over time.
- The intervention's feasibility and acceptability are key for long-term adherence.
