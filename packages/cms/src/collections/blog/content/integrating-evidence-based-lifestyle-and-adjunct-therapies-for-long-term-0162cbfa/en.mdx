## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the role of exercise in managing polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and its associated metabolic complications?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Reproductive-aged women with PCOS, a multisystem disorder characterized by hormonal and metabolic dysregulation.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle-based interventions, specifically structured physical activity including aerobic exercise, resistance training, high-intensity interval training, and mind-body practices.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons made against standard care or no intervention, focusing on the effects of physical activity on metabolic and hormonal outcomes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvements in metabolic parameters and insulin sensitivity, beneficial effects on hormonal outcomes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Emerging evidence suggests that structured physical activity can significantly improve clinical outcomes in PCOS, including metabolic and hormonal health.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Heterogeneity in study design and quality, necessitating cautious interpretation and further high-quality clinical validation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, incorporating regular, structured physical activity into their lifestyle may help improve insulin sensitivity and metabolic health, although the specific type and intensity of exercise should be tailored to individual needs and capabilities.

## English Plain-Language Version

After a long day, skipping the gym might seem tempting. However, regular exercise is crucial for managing prediabetes and metabolic health. It improves insulin sensitivity, a key factor in preventing type 2 diabetes.

A mix of aerobic activities, resistance training, and yoga can have significant benefits. Even moderate physical activity can make a difference. Consistency is key to incorporating exercise into your routine.

While beneficial, exercise is part of a larger picture. It works with a healthy diet and other lifestyle changes. Low-glycemic index diets also play a crucial role.

Combining these with regular physical activity can lead to improved metabolic parameters.

Frequency and intensity of exercise are important. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week, along with strength training exercises at least twice a week.

Listen to your body to avoid overexertion.

Lifestyle and metabolic health changes are gradual and require ongoing commitment. It's about creating sustainable habits that you can maintain over the long term.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Exercise is a cornerstone in managing prediabetes because it directly impacts insulin sensitivity, a key factor in preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Understanding how different types of exercise can influence metabolic health is essential for creating effective lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Regular exercise is crucial for managing prediabetes and metabolic health.
- A mix of aerobic, resistance, and mind-body exercises can improve insulin sensitivity.
- Combining a healthy diet with regular physical activity leads to better metabolic outcomes.
- Start with moderate activity and gradually increase intensity and duration for sustainable benefits.
- Consult with a healthcare provider to personalize your exercise and diet plan.
