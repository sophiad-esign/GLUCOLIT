## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the prevalence of undiagnosed impaired glucose metabolism and metabolic syndrome in men with erectile dysfunction, and is there an association with ED severity?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 605 consecutive men aged ≥ 18 years presenting with erectile dysfunction.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of ED severity using the 5-item International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF-5) and evaluation for metabolic syndrome and impaired glucose metabolism.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of IGM prevalence across different ED severity categories.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Identification of metabolic syndrome in 30.3% and IGM in 26.4% of patients, including newly diagnosed diabetes mellitus in 9.0% and prediabetes in 17.4%.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Nearly one-third of patients with ED had either prediabetes or metabolic syndrome, with a higher prevalence in those with mild-to-moderate ED.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted at a single center and included only men with ED, limiting the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Men with ED, especially those with mild-to-moderate symptoms, may have a higher likelihood of having undiagnosed metabolic issues. This suggests the importance of evaluating and managing metabolic risks in this population.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，因为难以启齿的问题，比如勃起功能障碍（ED），去看医生，结果发现自己还有未被诊断的代谢问题，比如糖尿病前期。 这并不罕见。 许多有ED的男性也有未被诊断的代谢问题，这会增加他们患心脏病和糖尿病的风险。

最近的一项研究发现，近三分之一的ED男性患有糖尿病前期或代谢综合征，这是一组增加心脏病、中风和糖尿病风险的条件。 研究还表明，ED越严重，拥有这些代谢问题的可能性就越高。 这是一个警钟，提醒我们要注意饮食和生活方式的改变，以管理这些风险。

首先，理解饮食在管理血糖水平中扮演着关键角色是很重要的。 一个均衡的饮食，富含来自全谷物、水果和蔬菜的纤维，可以帮助调节血糖水平。 将精制谷物换成全谷物，选择精益蛋白而不是加工肉类，以及在你的盘子上增加更多的非淀粉类蔬菜，是你实际可以采取的步骤。

锻炼是另一个关键组成部分。 定期的体育活动可以帮助提高胰岛素敏感性，降低发展糖尿病的风险。 目标是每周至少进行150分钟的中等强度运动，比如快步走或骑自行车。 倾听你的身体，根据需要调整强度和持续时间，以保持安全和一致性。

睡眠经常被忽视，但它对健康至关重要。 每晚目标是7-9小时的高质量睡眠。 如果你在睡眠上挣扎，考虑建立一个规律的睡眠计划，创造一个宁静的环境，并避免在睡前摄入兴奋剂。

虽然这些生活方式的改变可以带来差异，但重要的是要记住，它们不是医疗建议的替代品。 始终与医疗保健提供者咨询，讨论你的具体情况，并制定一个个性化的计划。

总之，如果你正在处理ED，它可能是潜在代谢风险的一个信号。 采取措施改善你的饮食，增加体育活动，并确保良好的睡眠可以帮助管理这些风险。 但记住，始终与你的医疗保健提供者合作，制定适合你的计划。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine going to the doctor for a problem that's hard to talk about, like erectile dysfunction (ED), and finding out you also have a hidden health issue like prediabetes. This is not uncommon.

Many men with ED also have undiagnosed metabolic problems, which can increase their risk of heart disease and diabetes.

A recent study found that nearly one-third of men with ED had either prediabetes or metabolic syndrome, a group of conditions that raise the risk of heart disease, stroke, and diabetes.

The study also showed that the more severe the ED, the higher the likelihood of having these metabolic issues. This is a wake-up call to pay attention to diet and lifestyle changes to manage these risks.

To start, it's important to understand that diet plays a crucial role in managing blood sugar levels. A balanced diet with plenty of fiber from whole grains, fruits, and vegetables can help regulate glucose levels.

Swapping out refined grains for whole grains, choosing lean proteins over processed meats, and adding more non-starchy vegetables to your plate are practical steps you can take.

Exercise is another key component. Regular physical activity can help improve insulin sensitivity and reduce the risk of developing diabetes.

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week, like brisk walking or cycling. Listen to your body and adjust the intensity and duration as needed to stay safe and consistent.

Sleep is often overlooked but is vital for good health. Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night.

If you're struggling with sleep, consider establishing a regular sleep schedule, creating a restful environment, and avoiding stimulants close to bedtime.

While these lifestyle changes can make a difference, it's important to remember that they are not a substitute for medical advice. Always consult with a healthcare provider to discuss your specific situation and develop a personalized plan.

In summary, if you're dealing with ED, it might be a sign of underlying metabolic risks. Taking steps to improve your diet, increase physical activity, and ensure good sleep can help manage these risks.

But remember, always work with your healthcare provider to develop a plan that's right for you.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Erectile dysfunction is not just a personal issue; it can be a sign of broader health problems, particularly those related to metabolism.

Understanding this connection can help men take proactive steps to improve their overall health and reduce the risk of more serious conditions like diabetes and heart disease.

Practical Takeaways

- Erectile dysfunction can be a sign of underlying metabolic risks like prediabetes and metabolic syndrome.
- A balanced diet with whole grains, fruits, and vegetables can help regulate blood sugar levels.
- Regular physical activity can improve insulin sensitivity and reduce the risk of diabetes.
- Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night to support overall health.
- Consult with a healthcare provider to develop a personalized plan for managing metabolic risks.
