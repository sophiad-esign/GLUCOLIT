## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do different subgroups of type 2 diabetes (T2D) exhibit distinct movement behaviors, and how does this relate to their risk of complications?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 706 study participants diagnosed with T2D, classified into subgroups based on age at T2D onset, body mass index, hbA1c, HOMA2-B, and HOMA2-IR.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Classification into T2D subgroups and measurement of 24-hour movement behavior including light PA, moderate-vigorous PA, day inactivity, and night sleep.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of movement behavior profiles across T2D subgroups.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Relative risk ratios (RRR) for having the least and most active profiles within each T2D subgroup.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Compared to MOD, MARD had a RRR of 0.69 for the least active profile and 1.53 for the most active profile. SIRD had RRRs of 1.32 for the least active and 1.44 for the most active profile.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's cross-sectional design limits causal inference. The sample may not be representative of all T2D patients, and the findings are specific to the Asian population.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes should be aware that their movement behavior might differ based on their T2D subgroup, which could influence the effectiveness of lifestyle interventions. It suggests the need for personalized approaches to physical activity and sleep management.

## 原文精华摘要

正如每种植物都需要独特的照料，2型糖尿病的不同亚型患者也是如此。 最近的研究揭示了这些亚型中体力活动和睡眠模式的差异，这可能有助于制定个性化的健康策略。

糖尿病前期是糖尿病的前兆，提示需要改变生活方式。 这项研究显示了糖尿病前期亚组中不同的运动行为，表明一些亚组可能需要更多的鼓励去积极活动，而其他亚组可能需要关于如何平衡活动与休息的建议。

研究表明，SIRD亚型的个体活动较少，可能需要针对性的干预措施来增加体力活动。 相比之下，MARD亚型的个体显示出更高的活动水平，可能需要指导如何保持充足的睡眠。

充足的睡眠对健康至关重要，研究表明六到九小时的睡眠是管理血糖水平的理想选择。 睡眠的质量和时间与数量一样重要，强调了睡眠管理需要全面的方法。

了解这些模式可以帮助为糖尿病前期个体制定个性化计划，无论是调整睡眠时间表、持续时间还是体力活动水平。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

了解2型糖尿病亚型中运动行为的细微差别对于制定有效的、个性化的生活方式干预措施至关重要，这些干预措施可以预防或延缓糖尿病相关并发症的发生。

你可以带走的重点

- 2型糖尿病不同亚型患者的体力活动和睡眠模式存在差异。
- 可能需要根据个体亚型特征制定针对性的干预措施。
- 对于活动较少的亚型，增加体力活动可能是关键。
- 对于活动较多的亚型，平衡活动与休息至关重要。
- 最佳睡眠时长和质量对血糖管理至关重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Just as each plant requires unique care, so too do individuals with different subtypes of type 2 diabetes.

Recent research sheds light on how physical activity and sleep patterns differ among these subtypes, which could inform personalized health strategies.

Prediabetes is a precursor to diabetes, signaling the need for lifestyle changes.

This study reveals distinct movement behaviors among prediabetes subgroups, suggesting that some may need more encouragement to be active, while others might benefit from advice on balancing activity with rest.

The study indicates that individuals with SIRD, a subtype of prediabetes, are less active and might require targeted interventions to increase physical activity.

In contrast, those with MARD show higher activity levels and may need guidance on maintaining restful sleep.

Adequate sleep is essential for health, and the study suggests that six to nine hours of sleep is ideal for managing blood sugar levels.

The quality and timing of sleep are just as important as the quantity, emphasizing the need for a holistic approach to sleep management.

Understanding these patterns can aid in crafting personalized plans for individuals with prediabetes, whether it involves adjusting sleep schedules, durations, or physical activity levels.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the nuances in movement behavior across type 2 diabetes subtypes is vital for developing effective, personalized lifestyle interventions that can prevent or delay the onset of diabetes-related complications.

Practical Takeaways

- Physical activity and sleep patterns vary among type 2 diabetes subgroups.
- Tailored interventions may be necessary based on individual subtype characteristics.
- Encouraging physical activity could be key for less active subtypes.
- Balancing activity with rest is crucial for more active subtypes.
- Optimal sleep duration and quality are vital for血糖 management.
