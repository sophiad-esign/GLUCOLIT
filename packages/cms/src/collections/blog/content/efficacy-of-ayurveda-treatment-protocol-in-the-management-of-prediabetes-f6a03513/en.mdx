## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does an Ayurveda-based treatment protocol improve glycaemic outcomes in prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 40 patients with HbA1C 5.7-6.4% and Indian diabetic risk Score of ≥60, aged 18-70 years.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Integrated Ayurveda Treatment Protocol (IATP) containing Triphala lauha churna 3 g twice a day along with Ayurveda dietary and lifestyle intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Placebo powder 3 g twice a day for 90 days along with standard prediabetic care (SPC).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Changes in glycated hemoglobin (HbA1C), fasting blood glucose (FBG), postprandial blood glucose (PPBG), lipid profile, diabetes quality of life (DQOL), and anthropometric measures.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** IATP showed significant improvement in HbA1C, FBG, PPBG, total cholesterol, high-density lipoprotein, low-density lipoprotein, serum triglycerides, serum creatinine, and DQOL compared to SPC.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted over a limited period of 90 days and with a relatively small sample size of 40 patients.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that an Ayurveda-based treatment protocol can significantly improve glycaemic outcomes in prediabetes, readers should consider these findings as part of a broader lifestyle modification plan and consult with healthcare providers for personalized advice.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up to the realization that your blood sugar levels are on the brink of diabetes, but there's a natural way to turn things around.

A recent study explored the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda to manage prediabetes through diet and lifestyle changes. In this randomized controlled trial, 40 patients with HbA1C levels between 5. 7-6.

4% received either an Ayurvedic treatment protocol or a placebo along with standard care. The Ayurvedic intervention included Triphala lauha churna and specific dietary advice.

After 90 days, the Ayurvedic group showed significant improvements in HbA1C, fasting blood glucose, and lipid profiles compared to the placebo group.

This suggests that incorporating Ayurvedic practices could be a valuable addition to the management of prediabetes.

It's important to note that while these results are promising, they are not a guarantee of individual outcomes and should be considered alongside other lifestyle modifications.

Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new treatment regimen.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a silent risk factor for various metabolic and cardiovascular diseases.

Understanding and implementing effective lifestyle modifications, such as those derived from Ayurvedic practices, can be crucial in managing and potentially reversing this condition.
