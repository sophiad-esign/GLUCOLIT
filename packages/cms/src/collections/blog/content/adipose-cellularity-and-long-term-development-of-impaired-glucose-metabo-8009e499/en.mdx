## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does adipose tissue cellularity relate to the future long-term development of impaired glucose metabolism?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1014 participants living in Stockholm, Sweden, re-examined between 2018-2022.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Measurement of abdominal subcutaneous adipocyte volume and number, and the presence of impaired glucose metabolism (IGM).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of adipocyte volume and number in participants who developed IGM, those who remained without IGM, and those who always had IGM.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Subcutaneous adipocytes were 20% larger in those with IGM, and the odds ratio for developing IGM was 2.8 for large adipocytes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Adipocyte volume, but not number, associates with the risk of long-term development of IGM. Large adipocytes confer an increased risk, possibly through insulin resistance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study does not establish causation and does not provide specific exercise recommendations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, managing adipose tissue through exercise may help reduce the risk of developing impaired glucose metabolism.

## English Plain-Language Version

Adipose tissue cellularity, consisting of adipocyte volume and number, is pivotal in glucose metabolism.

A Swedish study observed that larger adipocytes are linked to an increased risk of impaired glucose metabolism.

Physical activity may play a role in managing adipose tissue and glucose metabolism.

Incorporating exercise into daily routines could be beneficial for maintaining healthy glucose levels.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between adipose tissue cellularity and glucose metabolism is essential for individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, as it can guide lifestyle changes to prevent type 2 diabetes.

Practical Takeaways

- Adipocyte volume is associated with the risk of developing impaired glucose metabolism.
- Physical activity may help in managing adipose tissue and improving insulin sensitivity.
- Regular exercise is essential for healthy glucose metabolism.
