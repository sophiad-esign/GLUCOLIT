- **研究问题 / Research question:** How can hyperglycemia be staged to prevent type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Middle- and older-aged Brazilian adults from the Rio Grande do Sul center of the ELSA-Brasil cohort
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Combining fasting plasma glucose with 1-hour plasma glucose or HbA1c levels
- **对照 / Comparison:** Staging schemas with and without an initial clinical score cutoff of ≥10% probability of developing diabetes in 10 years
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Relative risk of developing diabetes, sensitivity and specificity in predicting incident diabetes
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The FPG/1 h PG schema stratified participants into three stages with increasing risk of complications and relative risk of incident diabetes. Using a clinical score improved specificity and reduced the need for laboratory testing.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings are based on a specific population and may not be generalizable to all. The study's duration and follow-up may also limit the long-term applicability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, understanding the staging of hyperglycemia can help in personalized risk assessment and targeted prevention efforts. For the health tech industry, these findings suggest opportunities for developing tools that assist in glucose level monitoring and personalized diabetes prevention strategies.

  English Plain-Language Version
  Prediabetes is a warning sign that blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet high enough to be diagnosed as type 2 diabetes.

It's a call to action to make lifestyle changes to prevent or delay the onset of diabetes.
The study identified stages in the development of type 2 diabetes by combining fasting plasma glucose with 1-hour plasma glucose or HbA1c levels, which can help in early identification and prevention.
The study's approach to staging hyperglycemia provides a nuanced view of the progression to type 2 diabetes. It shows that as glucose levels increase, so does the risk of developing diabetes.

The use of a clinical score in staging can reduce the need for laboratory testing and improve the specificity of diabetes prediction. However, the study's findings are based on a specific population and may not be generalizable to all.
For those with prediabetes, understanding your glucose levels and the associated risks is crucial.

Regular monitoring and lifestyle modifications, such as diet and exercise, can significantly reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes. The health tech industry can leverage these findings to develop tools that help individuals track their glucose levels and receive personalized advice on managing their prediabetes.

This can lead to more effective prevention strategies and better health outcomes.
This study is relevant as it provides a systematic approach to understanding the progression from prediabetes to type 2 diabetes, which is crucial for early intervention and prevention strategies.

- Understanding the stages of hyperglycemia can help in early identification and prevention of type 2 diabetes.
- Combining fasting plasma glucose with 1-hour plasma glucose or HbA1c levels provides a nuanced view of diabetes risk.
- A clinical score can improve the specificity of diabetes prediction and reduce the need for laboratory testing.
- Lifestyle modifications, such as diet and exercise, are crucial for individuals with prediabetes to reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
