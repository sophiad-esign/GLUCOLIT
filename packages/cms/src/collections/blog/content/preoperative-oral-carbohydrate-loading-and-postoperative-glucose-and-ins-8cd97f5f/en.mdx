## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does preoperative oral carbohydrate loading affect postoperative glucose and insulin metabolism in patients undergoing open abdominal aortic aneurysm repair?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Patients undergoing elective open aortic repair due to abdominal aortic aneurysm.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Preoperative oral carbohydrate loading (800 mL the evening before, and 400 mL 2 hours before anesthesia induction).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Standard overnight fasting.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Plasma glucose and insulin concentrations, insulin resistance and sensitivity indices, and patient-reported well-being.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** On postoperative Day 1, insulin levels were significantly lower in the OCHL group compared with controls. Homeostatic model assessment for insulin resistance was significantly lower, and insulin sensitivity index significantly higher in OCHL patients. Patients receiving OCHL reported lower levels of hunger, pain, and fatigue compared with controls.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study had a small sample size of forty patients, which limits the generalizability of the results. The study only provides association and not causation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests potential benefits of preoperative carbohydrate loading in improving glycemic control and patient well-being, readers should be cautious in overclaiming its effects due to the study's limitations. Further research is needed to confirm these findings and explore the role of preoperative nutrition in metabolic outcomes.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你即将进行一次大手术，医生建议你在手术前夜和手术前几小时吃大量碳水化合物。 这不仅仅是为了安慰食物； 这是一种可能影响手术后你的身体如何处理葡萄糖和胰岛素的策略。 最近的一项研究调查了这一点，专注于接受开放性腹主动脉瘤修复的患者。

他们发现，遵循碳水化合物负荷方案的人在术后第一天胰岛素水平较低，胰岛素敏感性更好。 他们还报告说感到不那么饿、不那么痛、不那么疲劳。 这表明，手术前的一个简单的饮食调整可能会导致更好的代谢控制和更高的幸福感。

然而，重要的是要注意这只是一项研究，需要更多的研究来完全理解术前碳水化合物负荷的好处。 目前，它为与医疗保健提供者讨论提供了一个潜在的饮食策略，以进行主要血管手术。

研究结果很有希望，但它们也有局限性。 样本量只有四十名患者，样本量小，结果可能不适用于每个人。 此外，尽管研究表明碳水化合物负荷与改善的代谢结果之间存在关联，但它并不证明因果关系。 这是理解营养在手术中作用的一步，但需要更多的工作。

如果你或你爱的人面临大手术，与你的医疗团队讨论所有术前护理方面至关重要。 这包括像碳水化合物负荷这样的饮食策略，这可能会影响你的术后恢复。 在改变你的饮食或医疗计划之前，总是寻求专业建议。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine preparing for a major surgery, and you're told to load up on carbohydrates the night before and a few hours before the operation. This isn't just about comfort food;

it's a strategy that could influence how your body handles glucose and insulin after surgery. A recent study looked into this, focusing on patients undergoing open abdominal aortic aneurysm repair.

They found that those who followed the carbohydrate loading protocol had lower insulin levels and better insulin sensitivity on the first postoperative day. They also reported feeling less hungry, less painful, and less fatigued.

This suggests that a simple dietary adjustment before surgery might lead to better metabolic control and a higher sense of well-being after the operation.

However, it's important to note that this is just one study, and more research is needed to fully understand the benefits of preoperative carbohydrate loading.

For now, it offers a potential dietary strategy for discussion with healthcare providers before major vascular surgery.

Carbohydrate loading before surgery might sound like a sports nutrition term, but in this context, it's about consuming a specific amount of carbohydrates in a liquid form before going under the knife.

The study showed that this approach could help manage blood sugar levels and insulin responses in the immediate postoperative period. It's a reminder that what we eat before a procedure can have a significant impact on our recovery.

The study's findings are promising, but they also come with limitations. With only forty patients, the sample size is small, and the results may not apply to everyone.

Additionally, while the study suggests an association between carbohydrate loading and improved metabolic outcomes, it doesn't prove causation. It's a step towards understanding the role of nutrition in surgery, but more work is needed.

If you or a loved one is facing major surgery, it's crucial to discuss all aspects of preoperative care with your healthcare team.

This includes dietary strategies like carbohydrate loading, which could potentially influence your postoperative recovery. Always seek professional advice before making any changes to your diet or healthcare plan.

In summary, the study hints at the potential benefits of preoperative carbohydrate loading for patients undergoing vascular surgery. It's a concept worth considering, but remember, more research is needed, and individual responses may vary.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how dietary interventions like preoperative carbohydrate loading can influence postoperative metabolic outcomes is crucial for patients with prediabetes or insulin resistance who are facing major surgery.

It can provide insights into how to optimize their care and recovery.

Practical Takeaways

- Patients who followed this dietary strategy reported less hunger, pain, and fatigue postoperatively.
- The study's findings are promising but come with limitations, such as a small sample size.
