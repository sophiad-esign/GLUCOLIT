## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does insulin resistance, as assessed by triglyceride-glucose indices, stratify incident cardiovascular disease risk after glycaemic transition in adults with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1468 participants with prediabetes from the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of glycaemic transition and TyG-derived indices from Wave 1 to Wave 3.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of incident CVD risk between persistent prediabetes, reversion to normoglycaemia, and progression to diabetes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incident CVD assessed from Waves 3 to 5.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Reversion to normoglycaemia was not associated with lower CVD risk, whereas progression to diabetes was associated with higher risk. High cumulative TyG-derived indices were significantly associated with higher CVD risk among those who reverted to normoglycaemia.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings are specific to the Chinese population and may not be generalizable to other ethnicities. The study design does not allow for causation to be inferred from the observed associations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, focusing on insulin resistance measures like the TyG index, in addition to blood sugar levels, may provide a more comprehensive assessment of cardiovascular risk and guide targeted lifestyle interventions.

中国最近的一项研究提供了关于如何通过甘油三酯-葡萄糖（TyG）指数等胰岛素抵抗的测量来预测谁可能仍然处于风险之中的见解。 该研究从第一波到第五波跟踪了1468名糖尿病前期患者的健康，作为中国健康和退休纵向研究的一部分。

他们发现，即使在血糖水平恢复正常后，那些累积TyG指数高的人患CVD的风险显著更高。 这表明，虽然血糖控制至关重要，但管理胰岛素抵抗对于长期心脏健康同样重要。 这不仅仅是血糖计上的数字问题； 而是胰岛素抵抗所反映的潜在代谢健康问题。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, unsure whether the path you're on leads to better health or potential heart trouble.

This is the situation for many with prediabetes, where the risk of cardiovascular disease (CVD) doesn't simply disappear after blood sugar levels normalize.

A recent study from China provides insight into how measures of insulin resistance, like the triglyceride-glucose (TyG) index, can help predict who might still be at risk.

The study followed 1468 prediabetes patients, tracking their health from Wave 1 to Wave 5 of the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study.

They found that even after reverting to normal blood sugar levels, those with high cumulative TyG indices had a significantly higher risk of CVD.

This suggests that while blood sugar control is crucial, managing insulin resistance is equally important for long-term heart health. It's not just about the numbers on the glucose meter;

it's about the underlying metabolic health that insulin resistance reflects.

For those with prediabetes, this means that even if they manage to get their blood sugar under control, they should still focus on lifestyle changes to improve insulin sensitivity, such as regular exercise and a healthy diet.

The study's findings underscore the importance of a comprehensive approach to managing prediabetes, one that looks beyond just glucose levels to the broader metabolic picture.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the relationship between insulin resistance and cardiovascular risk is crucial for individuals with prediabetes, as it can guide lifestyle and treatment decisions that extend beyond just managing blood sugar levels.

Practical Takeaways

- Insulin resistance, as measured by the TyG index, can predict cardiovascular risk even after blood sugar levels return to normal.
- Managing insulin resistance is crucial for long-term heart health, not just controlling blood sugar.
- Lifestyle changes, including regular exercise and a healthy diet, are important for improving insulin sensitivity.
- Prediabetes management should consider the broader metabolic health, not just glucose levels.
- Even with normal blood sugar, high TyG indices can signal a higher risk for cardiovascular disease.
