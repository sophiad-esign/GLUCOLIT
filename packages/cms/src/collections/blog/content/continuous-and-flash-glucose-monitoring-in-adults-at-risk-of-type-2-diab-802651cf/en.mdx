## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do continuous and flash glucose monitoring technologies support interventions aimed at preventing type 2 diabetes in adults at risk?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults at risk of developing type 2 diabetes, identified through blood test results, risk scores, or known risk factors such as obesity.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Use of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) or flash glucose monitoring (FGM) in interventional settings.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Health-related behaviors, including diet and physical activity, before and after the intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvements in glucose control, reductions in glycated hemoglobin, fasting glucose, and insulin resistance.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** 75% of the studies reported significant improvements in glucose control, and 50% reported improvements in anthropometrics.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The studies varied in quality, and the long-term effectiveness of these devices is still uncertain.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Glucose monitoring technologies may support behavior changes that can help prevent type 2 diabetes, but more research is needed to confirm their long-term efficacy.

## 原文精华摘要

血糖监测就像长途旅行中观察油表。 它有助于健康管理和预防2型糖尿病。 研究表明，血糖监测可以激励行为改变，比如增加体力活动，这对于有风险的人来说至关重要。

一项涉及1144名参与者的12项研究综述发现，血糖控制和身体测量有所改善。 定性数据揭示了感知到的益处和行为改变的动机，以及设备使用的可接受性和可行性。

虽然前景看好，但这些研究的质量参差不齐，需要进一步的研究来确认血糖监测设备的长期有效性。 然而，它们表明这些技术在预防2型糖尿病方面可能是宝贵的，尤其是与生活方式的改变（如定期运动）结合起来时。

将血糖监测融入日常生活可以提供实时反馈，了解生活方式选择如何影响健康，激励行为改变，可能导致更好的健康结果。

## English Plain-Language Version

Monitoring blood sugar is like watching a fuel gauge on a long trip. It helps manage health and prevent type 2 diabetes.

Recent studies show glucose monitoring can motivate behavior changes, like increasing physical activity, which is crucial for those at risk.

A review of 12 studies with 1144 participants found improvements in glucose control and body measurements. Qualitative data revealed perceived benefits and motivators for behavior change, and the acceptability and feasibility of device use.

While promising, the studies varied in quality, and further research is needed to confirm the long-term effectiveness of glucose monitoring devices.

However, they suggest these technologies could be valuable in preventing type 2 diabetes, especially with lifestyle modifications like regular exercise.

Integrating glucose monitoring into daily routines can provide real-time feedback on how lifestyle choices impact health, motivating behavior change and potentially leading to better health outcomes.

Keeping an active lifestyle and monitoring blood sugar can be a winning combination in the fight against type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Preventing type 2 diabetes is crucial, especially for those at risk. Understanding how daily activities affect blood sugar levels can lead to better self-management and healthier lifestyle choices.

Practical Takeaways

- Glucose monitoring devices can help motivate behavior changes like increasing physical activity.
- Studies show improvements in glucose control and body measurements with the use of these devices.
- The long-term effectiveness of glucose monitoring devices still needs further research.
- Combining glucose monitoring with lifestyle modifications like regular exercise may be beneficial.
- Real-time feedback from glucose monitoring can be a powerful motivator for behavior change.
