## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the relationship between insulin resistance, sarcopenia, obesity, and dietary protein intake?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 6,670 adults aged 20-70 years from the Monitoring of Metabolic Diseases Risk Factors in Tehran (MMRT) cohort.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Insulin resistance quantified using the Homeostasis Model Assessment (HOMA-IR) and sarcopenia defined as the lowest two population quintiles of skeletal muscle index (SMI) measured via dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Subgroup and combined-phenotype analyses by sex, age, diabetes, obesity, and protein intake levels.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Higher HOMA-IR associated with 2.64 times greater odds of sarcopenia. Associations were stronger in females and individuals with obesity. Low protein intake amplified the effect of insulin resistance.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Insulin resistance is independently and strongly associated with sarcopenia, particularly among females and individuals with obesity. Coexistence of low protein intake and insulin resistance markedly worsens muscle loss.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Cross-sectional study design limits causal inference. Variability in muscle mass measurement tools and definitions of sarcopenia across studies.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, managing insulin resistance and sarcopenia may benefit from a diet rich in high-quality protein and regular moderate-intensity physical activity, but further personalized guidance from healthcare providers is essential.

## 原文精华摘要

家庭聚会时，你可能会注意到，尽管定期锻炼，但叔叔的肌肉似乎不那么结实了。 这可能是肌肉减少症，一种常与胰岛素抵抗相关的肌肉质量损失。 《内分泌学调查杂志》最近的一项研究表明，饮食，尤其是蛋白质摄入，在管理这些状况中扮演着关键角色。

胰岛素抵抗可能导致肌肉减少症的风险增加2. 64倍。 在女性和肥胖人群中，这一风险甚至更高。 有趣的是，低蛋白质摄入可能放大这一风险，而较高的摄入可以帮助减轻风险。 这意味着我们吃什么，尤其是我们的蛋白质消费，可以显著影响肌肉健康。

将这些付诸实践，考虑在你的饮食中加入更多高质量的蛋白质，如瘦肉、鱼类以及豆类和豆腐等植物性选择。 目标是构建一个包含各种营养素的平衡饮食结构，以支持肌肉健康和血糖管理。

同样重要的是记住，虽然饮食至关重要，但定期的体育活动同样重要。 参与中等强度的锻炼，可以帮助提高胰岛素敏感性和肌肉力量。 然而，始终要倾听你的身体，并咨询医疗保健提供者，以确保活动对你的情况安全且合适。

该研究的发现强调了平衡饮食和定期锻炼在管理胰岛素抵抗和肌肉减少症中的重要性。 虽然需要更多的研究，但这些实用的步骤可以成为与你的医疗保健提供者讨论个性化护理计划的起点。

## English Plain-Language Version

Family dinners can reveal subtle health changes. Notice your uncle's less firm muscles despite regular exercise? It might be sarcopenia, often linked with insulin resistance.

A study from the Journal of Endocrinological Investigation shows diet, especially protein intake, plays a key role.

Insulin resistance raises the risk of sarcopenia by 2. 64 times, more so in females and those with obesity. Low protein intake can amplify this risk, while higher intake can mitigate it.

This suggests our diet, particularly protein, significantly impacts muscle health.

Incorporate high-quality proteins like lean meats, fish, beans, and tofu into your diet. Aim for balanced meals with diverse nutrients to support muscle health and blood sugar management.

Remember, diet is crucial, but regular physical activity is equally important. Engage in moderate-intensity exercises to improve insulin sensitivity and muscle strength.

Always consult with a healthcare provider to ensure activities are safe and suitable.

The study underscores the importance of a balanced diet and regular exercise in managing insulin resistance and sarcopenia.

While more research is needed, these steps can guide discussions with healthcare providers about personalized care plans.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Insulin resistance and sarcopenia are significant health concerns, especially for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Understanding how diet and exercise can influence these conditions is crucial for developing effective lifestyle interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- Adequate protein intake in a balanced diet can help manage insulin resistance and sarcopenia.
- Moderate-intensity physical activities benefit insulin sensitivity and muscle strength.
- Personalize diet and exercise plans by consulting healthcare providers.
- Monitor dietary protein intake as it significantly impacts muscle health.
- Regular check-ups and discussions with healthcare providers are essential for early diagnosis and management.
