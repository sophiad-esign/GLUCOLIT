## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Do formula diets lead to alterations in trace element status, and are these changes associated with glycaemic traits like HbA1c?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Participants with type 2 diabetes enrolled in the FAIR study.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assignment to one of two different formula diets (HEPAFAST® or OPTIFAST®) for 12 weeks.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of trace element levels and HbA1c before and after the intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Weight, BMI, and HbA1c were assessed; trace element biomarkers were quantified.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Both diets led to weight loss and decreased HbA1c. Changes in trace elements were mostly unrelated to HbA1c, except for selenoprotein P in the OPTIFAST® group.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was a subset of a larger trial with a limited sample size of 44 participants.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** A balanced formula diet can contribute to diabetes management without causing significant trace element imbalances, but individual responses may vary.

这项研究涉及两种不同的配方饮食，HEPAFAST®和OPTIFAST®，并发现在12周后，两种饮食都导致了体重减轻和HbA1c水平的改善。 有趣的是，微量元素的变化大多与HbA1c的改善无关，除了硒蛋白P在OPTIFAST®组中与HbA1c呈正相关。

这表明，通过平衡的饮食可以实现体重减轻和糖尿病缓解，而不会导致微量元素的显著缺乏或过量。 这就像确保你的花园有适当的营养平衡，而不会过度施肥或忽视它。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're managing a small garden and you realize that the plants are not doing well. You might start by checking the soil, the water, and the sunlight – all the essentials for growth.

Similarly, when it comes to managing type 2 diabetes, diet is a fundamental part of the equation.

A recent study looked into how specific formula diets can affect not just weight, but also the levels of trace elements like copper, zinc, and selenium, which are crucial for our body's metabolic health.

The study involved two different formula diets, HEPAFAST® and OPTIFAST®, and found that after 12 weeks, both diets led to weight loss and improvements in HbA1c levels.

Interestingly, the changes in trace elements were mostly unrelated to the improvements in HbA1c, except for a positive association with selenoprotein P in the OPTIFAST® group.

This suggests that a balanced diet can help achieve weight loss and diabetes remission without causing significant deficiencies or excesses in trace elements.

It's like ensuring your garden has the right balance of nutrients without over-fertilizing or neglecting it.

When considering a diet for diabetes management, it's important to look at the whole picture.

A balanced formula diet can be a part of that, but it's also crucial to discuss with a healthcare provider about how these findings might apply to your personal health journey.

Remember, what works for one person might not work for another, and a tailored approach is often the best way to achieve sustainable health improvements.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the impact of diet on trace element levels is crucial for individuals with type 2 diabetes because these elements play a significant role in metabolic health.

A balanced diet can help manage diabetes without causing nutritional imbalances.

Practical Takeaways

- Formula diets can lead to weight loss and improvements in HbA1c levels.
- Trace element levels can change with diet, but these changes are mostly unrelated to HbA1c improvements.
- A balanced diet can help achieve diabetes remission without causing significant deficiencies or excesses in trace elements.
- Discuss with a healthcare provider how these findings might apply to your personal health journey.
