## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the prevalence of isolated systolic hypertension among middle-aged and older Filipinos, and what factors are associated with it?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Middle-aged (40-59 years) and older (60 and above) Filipinos
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Isolated systolic hypertension (ISH) defined as blood pressure ≥140/<90 mmHg
- **对照 / Comparison:** No specific comparison group mentioned, as the study is observational and analyzes prevalence and risk factors.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prevalence of ISH and associated risk factors
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The prevalence of ISH was 9.10%, with significant associations found with age, little to no formal education, presence of diabetes or prediabetes, family history of hypertension, current alcohol drinking.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on secondary data analysis and may have limitations inherent to the data source. Causality cannot be inferred from this observational study.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** The findings suggest that ISH is a significant health concern among Filipinos, particularly in older age groups and those with certain health conditions or behaviors. This information can guide targeted health interventions and lifestyle modifications to reduce the risk of cardiovascular complications.
