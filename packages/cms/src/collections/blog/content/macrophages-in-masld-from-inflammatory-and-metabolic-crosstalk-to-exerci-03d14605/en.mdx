## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does exercise intervention impact the pathogenesis of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) through macrophage function?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic risks, particularly those with MASLD.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Exercise intervention as a lifestyle modification strategy.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between the effects of exercise on hepatic macrophage function and the inflammatory state in MASLD.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Reduction in pro-inflammatory inputs from adipose tissue, amplification of protective signals from muscles, and reprogramming of hepatic macrophage function.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Exercise can re-polarize macrophages, reshape the intrahepatic immune landscape, and mediate muscle-liver communication through exercise-induced factors, potentially ameliorating MASLD.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a review and does not provide direct experimental evidence but integrates existing evidence on the role of exercise in MASLD management. The applicability of these findings may vary among different populations and stages of MASLD.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with MASLD, regular exercise may offer a non-pharmacological approach to improve liver health by modulating macrophage function and inter-organ communication. However, the effectiveness of exercise as an intervention should be considered within the context of a comprehensive treatment plan and discussed with a healthcare provider.

## English Plain-Language Version

Fatigue upon waking can signal more than a late night—it might indicate silent inflammation, a common issue for those with prediabetes or early metabolic risks. The liver's health is a key indicator in such cases.

Exercise communicates with the liver, reducing inflammation and improving sugar metabolism, offering hope for managing MASLD.

The liver, central to metabolism, struggles with excess fat in MASLD, leading to health complications. Exercise can reprogram liver macrophages, reducing fat tissue inflammation and boosting muscle signals.

Finding a sustainable exercise routine that fits your lifestyle is key. Whether it's a walk after dinner or cycling to work, the goal is consistency and intensity that raises your heart rate.

Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new exercise regimen.

Exercise can help re-polarize macrophages and improve liver health, but it's not a standalone solution. It works best as part of a comprehensive approach that includes diet and possibly medication.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is a growing concern for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risks.

Exercise has been identified as a non-pharmacological strategy that can influence the disease's progression by targeting macrophage function and inter-organ communication, which is particularly relevant for those seeking lifestyle

interventions to manage their health.

Practical Takeaways

- Exercise can help manage MASLD by communicating with the liver and reducing inflammation.
- Consistent exercise is more beneficial than sporadic intense workouts.
- Choose an exercise intensity that supports your system without overexertion.
- Exercise should be part of a comprehensive approach to managing MASLD, including diet and medical advice.
- Regular exercise can lead to better metabolic health and enhanced natural defenses over time.
