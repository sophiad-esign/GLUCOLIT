## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does maternal insulin resistance during late gestation affect brown adipose tissue development and thermogenic function in neonatal calves?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Forty-four dry Holstein cows and their calves
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Maternal insulin resistance evaluated using the homeostasis model assessment (HOMA-IR)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Cows divided into maternal low IR and maternal high IR groups
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Brown adipose tissue mass, thermogenic gene expression, glucose tolerance, and calf health
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Maternal IR increased serum insulin and glucose levels in cows, reduced BAT mass and thermogenic gene expression in calves, and impaired glucose tolerance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on cows and calves, and results may not be directly applicable to humans. The sample size was relatively small.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study provides insights into the effects of maternal insulin resistance on offspring, it is important to consult with healthcare providers for personalized advice on managing insulin resistance and its implications for pregnancy and offspring health.

These calves also exhibit poor glucose tolerance and increased health issues, such as diarrhea. This underscores the importance of managing blood sugar levels during pregnancy for the metabolic health of future generations.

The study monitored the health and growth of calves from cows with varying insulin resistance levels. It revealed that calves from mothers with high insulin resistance had less brown fat and showed signs of impaired glucose metabolism.

This suggests that a mother's metabolic health can influence her offspring's metabolic system development.
