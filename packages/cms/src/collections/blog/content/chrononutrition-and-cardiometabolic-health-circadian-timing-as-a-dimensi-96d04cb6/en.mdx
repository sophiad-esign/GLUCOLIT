## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does meal timing affect cardiometabolic health, particularly in individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic risk.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Eating later in the biological day or night and eating patterns aligned with endogenous circadian rhythms.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of early versus late energy intake and circadian-aligned versus misaligned eating patterns.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Postprandial glucose regulation, insulin sensitivity, lipid handling, and cardiometabolic profiles.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Eating later in the biological day or night is associated with impaired postprandial glucose regulation, reduced insulin sensitivity, altered lipid handling, and adverse cardiometabolic profiles, independent of dietary composition and total energy intake. In contrast, eating patterns aligned with endogenous circadian rhythms are metabolically favorable.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a narrative review and does not provide causal evidence. The findings are based on observational studies, randomized clinical trials, and mechanistic investigations, which may have varying levels of evidence strength.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes or early metabolic risk may benefit from aligning their meal times with their body's internal clock to improve cardiometabolic health. However, they should not overclaim the association as causation and should consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to their diet.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，你正在尝试调音一件乐器。 就像音乐家需要找到正确的时机敲击正确的音符一样，我们的身体也有一个“生物钟”，告诉我们何时进食以获得最佳健康。 最近的研究显示，晚上或深夜进食可能会打乱我们身体的自然节律，导致胰岛素抵抗和更高的心血管风险等问题。

另一方面，使我们的饮食模式与身体的内部时钟保持一致，对代谢健康有益。 这意味着要提前摄入能量，避免深夜进食。 虽然还需要更多的研究，但这些发现可以帮助我们个性化饮食，更好地管理糖尿病前期并降低代谢风险。

根据我们身体的自然节律进食听起来简单，但在不规律的日程中并不总是那么容易。 然而，小小的改变可以带来很大的不同。 例如，尝试在一天中较早的时候吃主餐，并考虑在晚上吃较轻的餐食或小吃。 同样重要的是倾听你的身体，不要在不饿的时候强迫自己进食。

记住，这不是一个放之四海而皆准的解决方案，最适合你的方法可能有所不同。 在对饮食做出重大改变之前，一定要咨询医疗保健提供者。

与此同时，在我们等待更多研究的同时，我们可以开始注意自己的饮食习惯。 你是一个倾向于深夜进食的人吗？ 如果是这样，你可能想逐渐调整你的进食时间表。 如果你发现因为深夜进食而难以入睡，那么与医生讨论可能值得。

毕竟，我们的健康就像一首交响乐，我们敲击的每一个音符，或者在这个例子中，我们安排的每一顿饭，都为最终的旋律做出贡献。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

心脏代谢疾病是全球发病率和死亡率的首要原因。 了解饮食时间如何影响我们的健康可以帮助我们对饮食做出更明智的决策，特别是对于糖尿病前期或早期代谢风险的人。

你可以带走的重点

- 将用餐时间与身体的内部时钟保持一致对心脏代谢健康很重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're trying to tune a musical instrument. Just like a musician needs to find the right timing to hit the right notes, our bodies also have a 'biological clock' that tells us when to eat for optimal health.

Recent research suggests that eating later in the day or at night might disrupt our body's natural rhythms, leading to issues like insulin resistance and higher cardiovascular risk.

On the other hand, aligning our eating patterns with our body's internal clock can be beneficial for metabolic health. This means having earlier energy intake and avoiding late-night meals.

While more research is needed, these findings could help us personalize our diets to better manage prediabetes and reduce metabolic risks.

Eating according to our body's natural rhythms might sound simple, but it's not always easy with irregular schedules. However, small changes can make a big difference.

For instance, try to have your main meal earlier in the day and consider lighter meals or snacks in the evening. It's also important to listen to your body and not force yourself to eat when you're not hungry.

Remember, this is not a one-size-fits-all solution, and what works best for you might be different. Always consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet.

In the meantime, while we wait for more studies, we can start by being mindful of our eating habits. Are you someone who tends to eat late? If so, you might want to gradually shift your eating schedule.

And if you're finding it hard to sleep well because of late-night eating, it might be worth discussing with your doctor.

After all, our health is like a symphony, and every note we hit, or in this case, every meal we time, contributes to the final melody.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Cardiometabolic diseases are a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide.

Understanding how meal timing affects our health can help us make more informed decisions about our diets, especially for those with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Practical Takeaways

- Eating earlier in the day and avoiding late-night meals can help manage prediabetes and metabolic health.
- Aligning meal times with your body's internal clock is important for cardiometabolic health.
- Small changes in meal timing can make a big difference in how your body processes food.
- Consult with a healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet.
- Being mindful of your eating habits is a step towards better health.
