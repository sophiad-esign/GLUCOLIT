> 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does early time-restricted eating (eTRE) have varying effects on cardiometabolic health across different subgroups?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults aged 25-75 years with obesity (BMI: 30-60 kg/m2) undergoing weight-loss treatment.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Early time-restricted eating (eTRE) where participants ate within an 8-hour period from 07:00 to 15:00.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Control group ate over a ≥12-hour period.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Diastolic blood pressure, hemoglobin A1c, HOMA-IR, and cholesterol levels.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** eTRE lowered diastolic blood pressure and hemoglobin A1c in relevant subgroups, but increased cholesterol levels in severely obese adults.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Small sample size, particularly for racial subgroups, and the need for further investigation into eTRE's effects on cholesterol in severely obese individuals.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While eTRE shows promise in managing certain cardiometabolic health markers, its effects vary, and consultation with healthcare providers is advised before implementing dietary changes.
  原文精华摘要
  限时进食（TRE）作为一种流行的间歇性禁食形式，已显示出在减重和心脏代谢健康改善方面的潜力。 但其在不同人群和健康状况中的有效性尚不清楚。
  研究发现，早期限时进食（eTRE）可以降低高血压成人的舒张压和糖尿病前期成人的糖化血红蛋白水平。
  这些发现很有希望，但需要注意的是研究的局限性。 样本量，尤其是种族亚组的样本量很小，这限制了结果的普遍适用性。 此外，由于观察到胆固醇水平的增加，需要进一步调查eTRE对严重肥胖成人的影响。
  对于糖尿病前期的人来说，eTRE可能是管理血糖水平的潜在饮食策略。 然而，在做出重大饮食改变之前，与医疗保健提供者咨询至关重要。 健康科技行业可以探索开发工具和应用程序，帮助个人跟踪和管理他们的TRE方案，特别是对于那些心脏代谢风险更高的人。
  这项研究与糖尿病前期或早期代谢风险的个体相关，因为它探索了可能有助于管理他们健康状况的潜在饮食干预措施。
- 早期限时进食（eTRE）可能有助于管理血压和血糖水平。

  English Plain-Language Version
  Time-restricted eating (TRE), a popular form of intermittent fasting, has shown potential in weight loss and cardiometabolic health improvements. Its effectiveness across different demographics and health conditions remains unclear.
  eTRE can lower diastolic blood pressure and hemoglobin A1c levels in adults with hypertension and prediabetes, respectively.
  The study's findings are promising, but its limitations, such as the small sample size, especially for racial subgroups, should be noted.

Further investigation is needed into eTRE's impact on cholesterol levels in severely obese individuals.
For prediabetes management, eTRE could be a potential dietary strategy. However, consultation with healthcare providers is crucial before making significant dietary changes.

The health tech industry can explore tools to help track and manage TRE regimens for those at higher cardiometabolic risk.
This study is relevant to individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risks as it explores a potential dietary intervention that could help manage their health conditions.

- Early time-restricted eating (eTRE) may help manage blood pressure and blood sugar levels.
- The study's findings should be considered preliminary due to the small sample size, especially for racial subgroups.
- eTRE's impact on cholesterol in severely obese individuals needs further investigation.
- Consult healthcare providers before making significant dietary changes based on eTRE findings.
