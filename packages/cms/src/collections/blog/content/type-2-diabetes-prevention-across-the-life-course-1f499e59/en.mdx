## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，某天早上醒来发现你的车启动不了。 检查电池，发现没电了。 这与糖尿病前期类似——一个警告信号，表明你的身体代谢系统正在失去活力。 好消息是，就像你可以给汽车搭电启动一样，你也可以采取措施恢复身体的代谢健康，预防2型糖尿病的发生。

糖尿病前期是一个血糖水平高于正常但不足以被诊断为糖尿病的阶段。 这是一个关键的行动窗口，如果不加以解决，可能会发展成2型糖尿病。 关键是要将我们对糖尿病预防的方法重新定位在实现和维持正常血糖水平上。

研究表明，从正常血糖水平到糖尿病的转变是由一生的生物学、行为和环境因素塑造的。 这意味着预防策略应该针对不同的生活阶段和环境进行定制。 例如，儿童的饮食和活动水平在发育中至关重要，而成年人可能更关注压力管理和定期锻炼。

在预测谁会从糖尿病前期发展到糖尿病方面仍存在不确定性，并非所有干预措施都适用于每个人。 然而，通过了解血糖调节的机制和生活方式选择的影响，我们可以做出关于我们健康的明智决定。

实际行动包括将更多的全谷物、精益蛋白和蔬菜纳入你的饮食中，每周至少进行150分钟的中等强度锻炼，并确保你每晚获得足够的高质量睡眠。 这些步骤，与医疗保健提供者讨论后，可以成为预防2型糖尿病的个性化计划的一部分。

在GLUCOLIT，我们相信精准预防的力量，将干预措施与个人需求和社会经济环境相一致。 这种方法不仅有助于疾病预防，还有助于在生命早期恢复代谢健康。

总之，糖尿病前期是一个行动的号召。 通过专注于生活方式的改变，我们可以启动通往更健康的道路，预防发展成2型糖尿病。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

2型糖尿病是一个日益增长的全球健康负担，糖尿病前期是一个关键阶段，干预可以预防或延缓其发生。 了解全生命周期的糖尿病预防方法对于有糖尿病前期或早期代谢风险的个体做出明智的生活方式选择至关重要。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up one day to find your car won't start. You check the battery, and it's drained. This is similar to prediabetes—a warning sign that your body's metabolic system is losing its charge. The good news?

Just as you can jump-start a car, you can also take steps to restore your body's metabolic health and prevent the onset of type 2 diabetes.

Prediabetes is a stage where blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not high enough to be diagnosed as diabetes. It's a critical window to act, as it can progress to type 2 diabetes if left unaddressed.

The key is to reframe our approach to diabetes prevention around achieving and maintaining normoglycemia—normal blood sugar levels.

Research suggests that the journey from normoglycemia to diabetes is shaped by a lifetime of biological, behavioral, and environmental factors. This means that prevention strategies should be tailored to different life stages and contexts.

For instance, a child's diet and activity levels are crucial in development, while an adult might focus on stress management and regular exercise.

Uncertainty remains in predicting who will progress from prediabetes to diabetes, and not all interventions will work for everyone.

However, by understanding the mechanisms behind blood sugar regulation and the impact of lifestyle choices, we can make informed decisions about our health.

Practical actions include incorporating more whole grains, lean proteins, and vegetables into your diet, aiming for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week, and ensuring you get enough quality sleep each night.

These steps, when discussed with a healthcare provider, can form part of a personalized plan to prevent type 2 diabetes.

At GLUCOLIT, we believe in the power of precision prevention, aligning interventions with individual needs and socioeconomic settings. This approach not only helps in disease prevention but also in restoring metabolic health early in life.

In summary, prediabetes is a call to action. By focusing on lifestyle changes, we can jump-start our way to better health and prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Type 2 diabetes is a growing global health burden, and prediabetes is a critical stage where intervention can prevent or delay its onset.

Understanding the life-course approach to diabetes prevention is essential for individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk to make informed lifestyle choices.

Practical Takeaways

- Prediabetes is a warning sign that your body's metabolic system needs attention.
- Prevention strategies should be tailored to different life stages and contexts.
- Understanding the mechanisms behind blood sugar regulation can guide lifestyle choices.
- Practical actions like dietary changes, regular exercise, and quality sleep are part of a prevention plan.
- Precision prevention, aligned with individual needs, can help prevent type 2 diabetes and restore metabolic health.
