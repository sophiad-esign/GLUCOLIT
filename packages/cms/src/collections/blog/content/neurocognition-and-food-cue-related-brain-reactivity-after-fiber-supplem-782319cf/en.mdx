## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does fiber supplementation within a high-protein, plant-based diet affect neurocognitive functioning and food-reward related brain activity in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with overweight and prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A 12-week randomized-controlled trial with a potato fiber and sugar beet pectin supplement compared to an isocaloric placebo within a high-protein (approximately 45% plant-based) diet.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Fiber supplement vs. isocaloric placebo (maltodextrin).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Neurocognitive performance, food-reward-related brain activity (fMRI), satiety hormones, stress, general well-being, and associations with cardiometabolic markers.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** A trend towards increased amygdala BOLD response over time in the fiber group, improvements in psychomotor speed, visual memory, and learning abilities, and less subjective hunger after 12 weeks on a high-protein, partially plant-based diet.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study did not find significant changes in brain activity induced by visual food cues in reward-related brain regions due to fiber supplementation. The sample size and duration of the study may also limit the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that a high-protein, plant-based diet with fiber supplementation may improve certain cognitive functions and reduce hunger, it does not provide evidence that fiber supplementation alone alters brain reactivity to food cues. Readers with prediabetes should consider discussing dietary changes with healthcare providers to manage their condition.

## 原文精华摘要

面对自助餐中高热量食物，富含纤维和植物性蛋白的饮食可能有助于控制渴望并改善认知功能，尤其是对于糖尿病前期人群。 这种饮食方法可能是对抗肥胖及其相关疾病的一种可持续策略。

一项为期12周的试验比较了在高蛋白饮食中添加纤维补充剂和安慰剂的效果，使用功能性磁共振成像（fMRI）来观察大脑对食物提示的反应。 高蛋白饮食在提高精神运动速度、视觉记忆和学习能力，以及减少主观饥饿感方面显示出了有希望的结果。 这些发现暗示了饮食和认知益处之间的关联，而不是因果关系。

在餐食中加入更多的植物性蛋白质和纤维，可能导致更好的饱腹感和认知表现。 在进行重大饮食改变之前，始终咨询医疗保健提供者。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

这项研究之所以相关，是因为它提供了饮食变化的见解，特别是增加高蛋白植物性饮食中的纤维摄入量，可能对糖尿病前期个体的神经认知功能和饱腹感产生影响，提供了一种非药物管理代谢风险的方法。

## English Plain-Language Version

At a buffet with high-calorie foods, a diet rich in fiber and plant-based protein might help manage cravings and improve cognitive function, especially for those with prediabetes.

This approach could be a sustainable strategy against obesity and related diseases. A 12-week trial compared fiber supplements to a placebo within a high-protein diet, using fMRI to observe brain activity in response to food cues.

The high-protein diet showed promising results in enhancing psychomotor speed, visual memory, and learning abilities, and reducing subjective hunger. These findings suggest an association between diet and cognitive benefits, not causation.

Incorporating more plant-based proteins and fibers into meals could lead to better satiety and cognitive performance. Always consult with a healthcare provider before making significant dietary changes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant because it provides insights into how dietary changes, specifically increasing fiber intake within a high-protein, plant-based diet, can potentially influence neurocognitive function and satiety in individuals with

prediabetes, offering a non-pharmacological approach to managing metabolic risks.

Practical Takeaways

- A high-protein, plant-based diet with fiber may help manage hunger and improve cognitive function.
- Fiber supplementation did not significantly change brain activity in reward-related regions.
- The study suggests an association between diet and cognitive benefits, not causation.
- Incorporating more plant-based proteins and fibers into meals could lead to better satiety and cognitive performance.
