## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does a primary care-embedded dietitian health coaching intervention improve cardiometabolic health outcomes in patients with prediabetes or diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 364 adults receiving primary care health coaching from a registered dietitian.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Health coaching by registered dietitians focusing on nutrition, physical activity, sleep, and other health behaviors.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Pre-post changes in weight, BMI, HbA1c, and blood pressure at 3 and 6 months post the initial health coaching visit.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant weight reductions, BMI decreases, and HbA1c value decreases among patients with diabetes or prediabetes.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Participants had mean decreases of -0.96 and -1.07 kg after 3 and 6 months, and a -0.35 kg/m2 decrease in BMI after 6 months. Among patients with diabetes or prediabetes, HbA1c values decreased by -0.55% after 6 months.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was observational and conducted within a specific health care system, which may limit generalizability. The number of coaching visits and frequency were not standardized, leaving room for variability in intervention exposure.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes or early metabolic risk, engaging in health coaching with a dietitian may lead to improvements in weight, BMI, and blood sugar control. However, the specific impact may vary based on individual factors and the consistency of coaching sessions.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at the doctor's office, and instead of just getting a prescription, you're offered a session with a dietitian who can help you make healthier food choices and lifestyle changes.

This is what happened in a recent study, and the results are encouraging for those of us with prediabetes or early signs of metabolic issues.

Patients who worked with dietitian health coaches in primary care settings lost weight, reduced their BMI, and improved their HbA1c levels, a key indicator of blood sugar control.

The more sessions they had with the coach, the more they benefited.

This suggests that having dietitians as part of primary care teams could be a powerful tool in managing our health, especially when it comes to preventing or controlling diabetes.

Losing weight and improving metabolic health isn't just about willpower; it's about having the right support and guidance.

A dietitian can help you understand what a healthy diet looks like for you and how to make it a part of your everyday life. They can also provide tips on physical activity and sleep, which are just as important for metabolic health.

The study shows that even small changes, with the help of a professional, can lead to big improvements in our health.

It's important to remember that while this study is promising, it's not a one-size-fits-all solution. Everyone's body and lifestyle are different, and what works for one person might not work for another.

But it does show that with the right support, we can make progress towards better health. If you have prediabetes or are concerned about your metabolic health, it might be worth discussing health coaching with your doctor.

In summary, this study highlights the potential of dietitian health coaching in primary care to help manage metabolic health. It's not just about losing weight;

it's about understanding how the foods we eat and the activities we do can impact our health in the long term. And with the right guidance, we can make changes that can last a lifetime.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Managing prediabetes and metabolic risk is crucial for preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes and other cardiovascular diseases.

This study provides evidence that dietitian health coaching in primary care can be an effective approach to support patients in making lifestyle changes that improve their metabolic health.

Practical Takeaways

- Working with a dietitian health coach in primary care can lead to significant weight loss and improvements in BMI and HbA1c levels.
- The more sessions patients had with the health coach, the greater their weight loss.
- Dietitian health coaches can provide valuable support and guidance for making healthier food choices and lifestyle changes.
- Small changes with professional support can lead to big improvements in metabolic health.
- Discussing health coaching with your doctor could be beneficial if you have prediabetes or are concerned about your metabolic health.
