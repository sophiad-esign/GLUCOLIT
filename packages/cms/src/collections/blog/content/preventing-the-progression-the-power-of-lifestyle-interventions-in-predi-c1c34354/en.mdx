## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of lifestyle interventions on preventing the progression from prediabetes to type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults with prediabetes, aged 18 years and above.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle interventions including diet, exercise, yoga, and behavioral support.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons were made between intervention groups and control groups within the studies.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Improvements in glycemic parameters, cardiometabolic profiles, and reductions in diabetes incidence.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Six high-quality RCTs showed significant improvements in glycemic parameters and reductions in diabetes incidence among prediabetic individuals.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The studies were conducted within a specific population and may not be generalizable to all individuals with prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, culturally tailored lifestyle interventions may help prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes, but more research is needed to understand their applicability across different populations.

### 核心发现 文化适应的生活方式干预显著改善了糖尿病前期成人的血糖参数，并降低了糖尿病发病率。

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background Prediabetes is a reversible metabolic condition that can lead to type 2 diabetes. This review evaluates the impact of lifestyle interventions in preventing this progression.

### Core Findings Culturally tailored lifestyle interventions significantly improved glycemic parameters and reduced diabetes incidence in prediabetic adults.

### Your Interpretation and Critique The review underscores the importance of culturally adapted interventions in diabetes prevention. While promising, the applicability of these findings to diverse populations needs further exploration.

### Clinical/Business Insights Incorporating lifestyle interventions into healthcare could enhance metabolic health, particularly for high-risk groups.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding how lifestyle changes can prevent the progression of prediabetes is crucial for those at risk of developing type 2 diabetes. It can also guide healthcare providers in developing more effective prevention strategies.

### Practical Takeaways

- Lifestyle modifications are key in managing prediabetes.
- Cultural adaptation enhances intervention efficacy.
- Further research is needed for broader population applicability.
- Healthcare integration of interventions may improve outcomes.
