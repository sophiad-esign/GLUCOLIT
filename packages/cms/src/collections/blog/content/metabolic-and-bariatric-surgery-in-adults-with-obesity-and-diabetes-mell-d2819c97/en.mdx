## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the medium- and long-term benefits and harms of different metabolic and bariatric surgery procedures compared with non-surgical treatment on outcomes relevant to adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Adults (≥ 18 years) with obesity (body mass index ≥ 30 kg/m² or ethnic-specific cut-off values) and type 2 diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Metabolic and bariatric surgery procedures (i.e., laparoscopic adjustable gastric banding, sleeve gastrectomy, Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, one-anastomosis gastric bypass, gastric plication, biliopancreatic diversion with duodenal switch, and their variations) or non-surgical treatment (i.e., lifestyle intervention, medical treatment).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Non-surgical treatment (lifestyle intervention and medical treatment).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Medium- (≥ 3 to < 5 years) and long-term (≥ 5 years) weight loss, waist circumference, type 2 diabetes remission, serious adverse events, all-cause mortality, type 2 diabetes complications, glycaemic control (glycated haemoglobin), and health-related quality of life.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** RYGB showed greater long-term weight loss compared to non-surgical treatment. OAGB and SG may also result in greater weight loss, but the evidence is very uncertain. RYGB may reduce HbA1c and increase health-related quality of life compared to non-surgical treatment, but the evidence is very uncertain for other outcomes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The certainty of evidence was downgraded due to risk of bias and imprecision. The evidence for complete type 2 diabetes remission, serious adverse events, all-cause mortality, and type 2 diabetes complications is very uncertain.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While MBS, particularly RYGB, may offer significant long-term benefits for weight loss and diabetes management, the evidence for other outcomes and procedures is less certain. It's important to discuss these findings with healthcare providers to understand the potential benefits and risks for individual health profiles.

## 原文精华摘要

面对2型糖尿病和肥胖，可以考虑代谢和减重手术（MBS）。 一项研究比较了MBS方法与生活方式和医疗干预。 Roux-en-Y胃旁路手术（RYGB）显示出更大的长期减重效果。 单吻合口胃旁路手术（OAGB）和袖状胃切除术（SG）也显示出潜力，但确定性较低。

在减少腰围和改善糖化血红蛋白水平方面，RYGB似乎最为有效。 然而，由于包括不精确性和偏倚风险在内的各种限制，这些发现的确定性并不高。 每个人的糖尿病之旅都是独特的，决定接受手术应该在与医疗保健提供者咨询后做出。

实际上，这意味着对于一些肥胖和2型糖尿病患者来说，与非手术方法相比，MBS可能提供了一个更有效的长期减重和糖尿病管理解决方案。 然而，由于证据中的不确定性，与医疗保健提供者进行详细讨论，以了解针对个人健康档案的潜在好处和风险至关重要。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

随着肥胖和2型糖尿病的患病率增加，了解不同治疗选项的有效性对个人和医疗保健提供者至关重要。 这项研究提供了有关代谢和减重手术与非手术治疗潜在好处的见解，可以为决策过程提供信息。

你可以带走的重点

- 代谢和减重手术可能为肥胖和2型糖尿病患者提供显著的长期减重和糖尿病管理好处。
- Roux-en-Y胃旁路手术似乎是长期减重和糖化血红蛋白降低最有效的MBS程序。
- 其他MBS程序如OAGB和SG也显示出潜在好处，但证据较为不确定。
- 由于证据中的不确定性，与医疗保健提供者讨论MBS的潜在好处和风险非常重要，需要根据个人情况而定。

## English Plain-Language Version

Facing type 2 diabetes and obesity? Consider metabolic and bariatric surgery (MBS). A study compared MBS methods to lifestyle and medical interventions. Roux-en-Y gastric bypass (RYGB) showed greater long-term weight loss.

OAGB and SG also showed potential but with less certainty. RYGB seemed most effective for reducing waist circumference and improving HbA1c levels. However, due to limitations, these findings are not highly certain.

Each person's diabetes journey is unique, and the decision to undergo surgery should be made in consultation with healthcare providers.

For some individuals with obesity and type 2 diabetes, MBS might offer a more effective long-term solution for weight loss and diabetes management than non-surgical approaches.

Due to the uncertainties in the evidence, it's crucial to have a detailed discussion with healthcare providers to understand the potential benefits and risks tailored to individual health profiles.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

With the increasing prevalence of obesity and type 2 diabetes, understanding the effectiveness of different treatment options is crucial for individuals and healthcare providers.

This study provides insights into the potential benefits of metabolic and bariatric surgery compared to non-surgical treatments, which can inform decision-making processes.

Practical Takeaways

- Metabolic and bariatric surgery may offer significant long-term benefits for weight loss and diabetes management in individuals with obesity and type 2 diabetes.
- Roux-en-Y gastric bypass appears to be the most effective MBS procedure for long-term weight loss and HbA1c reduction.
- Other MBS procedures like OAGB and SG also show potential benefits, but the evidence is less certain.
- Due to the uncertainties in the evidence, it's important to discuss the potential benefits and risks of MBS with healthcare providers on an individual basis.
