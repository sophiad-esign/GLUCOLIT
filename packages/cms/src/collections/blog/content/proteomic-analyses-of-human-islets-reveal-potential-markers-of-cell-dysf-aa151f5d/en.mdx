## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What proteomic changes in pancreatic islets can predict the onset of diabetes in individuals with prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals without diabetes undergoing partial pancreatectomy, previously characterized for glucose tolerance, insulin sensitivity, and insulin secretion.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Proteomic analysis of pancreatic islets.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between individuals with normal glucose tolerance (NGT) and impaired glucose tolerance (IGT).
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Reduction in proteins regulating glycolysis, lipid metabolism, glucose transport, and insulin secretion in IGT individuals.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** IGT individuals had reductions in proteins regulating key metabolic processes compared with NGT individuals.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is based on a small sample size and further research is needed to confirm these findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** These findings provide insights into the early metabolic changes that occur in prediabetes and could serve as predictive markers for diabetes progression.
