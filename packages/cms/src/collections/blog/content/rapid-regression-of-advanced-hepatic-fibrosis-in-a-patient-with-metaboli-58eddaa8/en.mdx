## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can intensive lifestyle intervention lead to regression of advanced fibrosis in patients with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD)?
- **研究对象 / Population:** A man in his 30s with severe obesity (BMI 35.2 kg/m^2) and advanced fibrosis (liver stiffness measurement [LSM] 18 kPa).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A 12-month program of caloric restriction and resistance training.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparative serum proteomic analysis before and after the intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** 33.8% weight loss, normalized liver enzymes, and an 80.6% reduction in LSM.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Intensive lifestyle intervention can induce rapid regression of advanced fibrosis in MASLD.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a single case report, limiting the generalizability of the findings. Further research with larger sample sizes is needed to confirm these results.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic risk, this case suggests that significant lifestyle changes, including diet and exercise, may lead to substantial improvements in liver health and potentially reverse advanced liver disease.

## English Plain-Language Version

Diet and exercise can significantly improve liver health. A case report details a patient with severe obesity and advanced fibrosis due to MASLD who underwent a 12-month program.

The program included caloric restriction and resistance training, leading to a 33. 8% weight loss and normalized liver enzymes. Mediterranean diet, low-carb diets, and intermittent fasting are beneficial dietary strategies.

Regular physical activity and resistance training help reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity. Quality sleep is crucial for metabolic health, aiming for 7-9 hours per night.

Small, consistent changes in diet and lifestyle can lead to significant health improvements for those with prediabetes or early metabolic risk.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

MASLD is a progressive condition that can lead to severe liver complications if left untreated.

This case report highlights the potential for significant regression of advanced fibrosis through intensive lifestyle intervention, which is particularly relevant for individuals with prediabetes, insulin resistance, or early metabolic

risk. Understanding the impact of diet and exercise on MASLD can empower patients to take control of their health and prevent disease progression.

Practical Takeaways

- Lifestyle changes can significantly improve liver health in MASLD patients.
- The Mediterranean diet, low-carb diets, and intermittent fasting are beneficial for MASLD.
- Regular physical activity and resistance training help reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity.
- Quality sleep is essential for metabolic health; aim for 7-9 hours per night.
- Small, consistent changes in diet and lifestyle can lead to significant health improvements.
