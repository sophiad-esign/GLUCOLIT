## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of a team-based multisite care model on improving cardiometabolic health in patients with prediabetes and related conditions?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Patients with type 2 diabetes/prediabetes and cardiovascular and/or chronic kidney disease.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Enrollment in a clinical program with team care, structured visits, and standardized assessments.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of cardiometabolic risk factor levels and adherence to guideline-directed medical therapy before and after the intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant reductions in weight, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol, triglycerides, fasting glucose, HbA1c, and insulin dose.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Participants experienced improvements in all measured cardiometabolic risk factors and an increase in adherence to guideline-directed medical therapy.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted across specific sites within the Cardiometabolic Center Alliance and may not be generalizable to all populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, engaging in a structured care program with team support can lead to significant improvements in health metrics and adherence to medical therapy, suggesting the importance of a comprehensive approach to managing prediabetes.

## 原文精华摘要

想象你在家庭聚餐时，有人告诉你面前的美味佳肴可以更健康。 这就是通过饮食和生活方式改变来管理糖尿病前期的本质。 最近的一项研究表明，通过结构化的护理，包括团队支持和标准化评估，患有糖尿病前期和相关疾病的个体在体重、血压、胆固醇和血糖水平上都有了显著改善。

这意味着通过在你的饮食中做一些特定的替换，以及加入规律的锻炼，你可以采取具体的步骤走向更健康的生活。 例如，用全谷物替换精制谷物，选择瘦蛋白，增加纤维摄入可以导致更健康的饮食。 规律的体育活动，即使只是快步走，也可以发挥作用。

重要的是要记住，这些改变并不剧烈，而是逐渐转向更健康生活方式的一部分。 该研究的结果表明，有了正确的支持和方法，就有可能做出可持续的改变，这些改变可以显著影响你的健康。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

管理糖尿病前期对于预防发展为2型糖尿病和降低心血管疾病的风险至关重要。 这项研究提供了实用的见解，说明基于团队的护理模式如何支持个人做出可以显著影响他们健康的生活方式改变。

你可以带走的重点

- 结构化的护理和团队支持可以帮助个人做出可持续的生活方式改变。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a family dinner, and you're told that the delicious meal in front of you could be healthier. That's the essence of managing prediabetes through diet and lifestyle changes.

A recent study showed that with structured care, including team-based support and standardized assessments, individuals with prediabetes and related conditions saw significant improvements in weight, blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood

sugar levels. This means that by making certain swaps in your diet and incorporating regular exercise, you can take concrete steps towards better health.

For instance, replacing refined grains with whole grains, opting for lean proteins, and increasing fiber intake can lead to a healthier diet. Regular physical activity, even if it's just a brisk walk, can also make a difference.

It's important to remember that these changes are not drastic but are part of a gradual shift towards a healthier lifestyle.

The study's findings suggest that with the right support and approach, it's possible to make sustainable changes that can significantly impact your health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Managing prediabetes is crucial for preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes and reducing the risk of cardiovascular diseases.

This study provides practical insights into how a team-based care model can support individuals in making lifestyle changes that can significantly impact their health.

Practical Takeaways

- Making small dietary changes can lead to significant health improvements.
- Regular physical activity is an essential part of managing prediabetes.
- Structured care and team support can help individuals make sustainable lifestyle changes.
- Improvements in weight, blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar levels are achievable with the right approach.
- Discussing these changes with a healthcare provider can help tailor a plan that suits individual needs.
