## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Is the uric acid to high-density lipoprotein-cholesterol ratio (UHR) associated with obesity-related comorbidities in youth?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 1215 youths with overweight or obesity from the CARITALY study.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of UHR and its association with various health outcomes.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of health outcomes across different quartiles of UHR.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Impaired fasting glucose, impaired glucose tolerance, high cardiometabolic risk, metabolic-associated steatotic liver disease, reduced glomerular filtration, and low-grade inflammation.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Youth in the top UHR quartile showed higher odds ratios for mild reduced-estimated glomerular filtration rate and continuous cardiometabolic risk score. Youths in the third quartile showed higher odds ratios for impaired fasting glucose, impaired glucose tolerance, homeostasis model assessment of insulin resistance, and high white blood cell count.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings are associative and do not establish causation. The sample is specific to the CARITALY study population and may not be generalizable to all youth with obesity.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While UHR shows promise as a biomarker for detecting obesity-related comorbidities in youth, it should be considered alongside other health assessments and not as a standalone diagnostic tool. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle remains essential for managing obesity and its complications.

## 原文精华摘要

担心肥胖青少年的健康吗？ 一项血液测试，测量尿酸与高密度脂蛋白胆固醇比值（UHR），可能有助于预测他们发展成2型糖尿病等并发症的风险。 这项研究观察了1215名青少年，发现UHR较高的青少年有更高的空腹血糖受损和其他健康问题的风险。 这意味着UHR可能是医生早期识别高危青少年的有用工具。

然而，重要的是要记住这只是谜题的一部分，健康的生活方式对于管理肥胖及其相关健康风险至关重要。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

## English Plain-Language Version

Concerned about a teenager's health due to obesity? A blood test measuring the UHR could predict the risk of complications like type 2 diabetes.

The study involved 1215 youths, revealing higher UHR linked to greater risks of impaired fasting glucose and other issues. This suggests UHR's utility in early identification of at-risk youth.

Yet, it's part of a broader health management strategy that includes a healthy lifestyle.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding UHR's role is crucial for parents and healthcare providers as it offers a potential early warning system for obesity-related health risks in youth, allowing for timely interventions.

Practical Takeaways

- UHR may help identify youth at risk of obesity-related comorbidities.
- Higher UHR is linked to a greater risk of impaired glucose metabolism in youth.
- A healthy lifestyle is crucial for managing obesity and reducing health risks.
- Further research is needed to understand UHR's predictive role fully.
