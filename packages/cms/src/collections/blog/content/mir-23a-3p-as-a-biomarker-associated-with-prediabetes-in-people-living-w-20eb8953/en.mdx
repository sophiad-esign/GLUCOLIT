## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can miR-23a-3p serve as an early biomarker for prediabetes in people living with HIV?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 80 adults divided into five groups: normoglycemic PLWHIV, PLWHIV with prediabetes, PLWHIV with type 2 diabetes, HIV-negative individuals with type 2 diabetes, and controls.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of clinical, anthropometric, biochemical, inflammatory, and insulin resistance markers, with plasma miR-23a-3p quantified by digital PCR.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of metabolic and inflammatory profiles between groups.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** PLWHIV with type 2 diabetes showed the most unfavorable profiles, while those with prediabetes exhibited significant overexpression of miR-23a-3p.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** miR-23a-3p correlated positively with IL-6 and GDF-15, and showed good discriminative performance for prediabetes in PLWHIV.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is cross-sectional and the suggested targets of miR-23a-3p require experimental validation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While miR-23a-3p shows promise as a biomarker, it should be considered alongside other clinical markers. Lifestyle modifications, particularly diet and exercise, remain crucial for managing prediabetes in PLWHIV.

## English Plain-Language Version

Choosing a salad over a burger might help manage prediabetes, especially for those with HIV. A study indicates miR-23a-3p could be an early biomarker for prediabetes in this population.

Molecular changes signal the need for lifestyle adjustments. Prioritize a balanced diet, regular exercise, and quality sleep. These are essential for managing prediabetes in the context of HIV.

HIV increases the risk of type 2 diabetes, and early biomarkers like miR-23a-3p can aid in prevention. This is significant for those with prediabetes or insulin resistance, offering a tailored approach to health management.
