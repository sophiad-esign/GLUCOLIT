## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can semaglutide help prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes in women with postpartum prediabetes following gestational diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 252 women with a recent history of gestational diabetes and postpartum prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Once-weekly subcutaneous injections of semaglutide 1 mg or placebo in addition to standardized lifestyle intervention.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Semaglutide versus placebo.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Development of type 2 diabetes, need for diabetes rescue therapy, regression to normoglycaemia, weight loss, insulin sensitivity, beta-cell function, cardiometabolic risk profile, patient-reported outcomes, and cost-effectiveness.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** The study is ongoing, and results will provide insights into the efficacy, safety, and cost-effectiveness of semaglutide in preventing progression to type 2 diabetes.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study has not yet been completed, and findings are not yet available. The study's results will be subject to the sample size and may not be generalizable to all women with postpartum prediabetes.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While waiting for the study results, women with postpartum prediabetes can focus on making healthy lifestyle choices, such as improving their diet and increasing physical activity, as these are known to be beneficial in managing prediabetes.
