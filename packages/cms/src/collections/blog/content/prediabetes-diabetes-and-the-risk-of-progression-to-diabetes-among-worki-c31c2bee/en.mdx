## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the progression and regression patterns of prediabetes and diabetes among working adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Working adults aged 18-65 in Beijing.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Observational study of the progression from normoglycemia to prediabetes and diabetes.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of progression rates between different age groups and genders.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Incidence of prediabetes and diabetes, rates of glycemic normalization.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** 23.9% of normoglycemic individuals progressed to prediabetes, and 2.3% developed diabetes. Among prediabetics, 28.0% progressed to diabetes, and 18.7% normalized their glycemic levels. Younger adults had lower progression rates.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is observational and cannot establish causation. It also does not provide data on the effectiveness of specific interventions.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Understanding these patterns can help individuals and healthcare providers make informed decisions about lifestyle changes and interventions to prevent diabetes.

This study is relevant as it provides insights into the progression rates of prediabetes to diabetes in a working population, which is crucial for developing targeted prevention strategies and public health policies.

Practical Takeaways
