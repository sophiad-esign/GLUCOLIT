## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do sex differences affect the epidemiology, pathophysiology, and management of Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD)?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Global population across Asia, Europe, North America, the Middle East, and other regions.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle interventions, including diet and exercise, and their impact on MASLD.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Sex-based differences in MASLD prevalence, pathophysiology, and outcomes.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Higher MASLD prevalence in men, sex-specific pathophysiological drivers, and differential treatment responses.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Men have a higher prevalence of MASLD before menopause, while postmenopausal women face increased risks due to hormonal changes. Sex-specific pathophysiological drivers and treatment responses are evident.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The review is narrative and synthesizes data from various studies, which may have different methodologies and populations.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes or early metabolic risk should be aware of sex-specific differences in MASLD and consider these when discussing lifestyle interventions with their healthcare providers.
