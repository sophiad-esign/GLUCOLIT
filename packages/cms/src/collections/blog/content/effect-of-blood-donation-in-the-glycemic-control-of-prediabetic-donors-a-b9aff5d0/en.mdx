## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does blood donation have an impact on glycemic control in prediabetic individuals?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 38 habitual blood donors with prediabetes, characterized by borderline glycemia.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** A single blood donation of 450 mL versus a simulated donation of 6 mL.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Measurements before and two weeks after the intervention.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Ferritin levels, insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), liver enzymes, lipid profile, and glucose tolerance during an OGTT.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Ferritin decreased significantly in the blood donation group, associated with improvements in liver function and early-phase glucose tolerance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study did not find changes in lipid profiles or overall insulin resistance, and the sample size was relatively small.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While blood donation may offer some benefits for glycemic control in prediabetes, it should be considered as part of a broader lifestyle intervention and not a standalone treatment.

## English Plain-Language Version

Visiting a blood donation center might offer more than just altruism; it could aid in managing prediabetes.

A study suggests that a single blood donation can improve short-term blood sugar control, particularly for overweight prediabetic individuals.

The research involved 38 regular blood donors with prediabetes, split into two groups: one donated 450 mL, and the other simulated a donation of 6 mL.

Real donors saw a significant drop in ferritin levels, indicating reduced iron stores, which correlated with better liver function and early-phase glucose tolerance.

However, the study did not observe changes in lipid profiles or overall insulin resistance.

Blood donation is not a replacement for a healthy diet and regular exercise, which are key in managing prediabetes.

It's important to consult with healthcare providers before considering blood donation as part of a prediabetes management plan.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical phase where lifestyle interventions can prevent or delay the onset of type 2 diabetes.

Understanding the potential benefits of blood donation in this context can offer an additional tool for individuals looking to manage their blood sugar levels.

Practical Takeaways

- A single blood donation may help improve short-term glycemic control in prediabetic individuals.
- Ferritin levels, a marker of body iron stores, significantly decreased only in the group that donated blood.
- Improvements in liver function and early-phase glucose tolerance were observed after blood donation.
- Discuss with your healthcare provider before considering blood donation as part of your prediabetes management plan.
