## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the level of knowledge, attitude, and practices regarding metabolic syndrome among ambulance support personnel, and how can lifestyle modifications help?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 119 ambulance support personnel in Karad City, Maharashtra, India
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Occupational stressors including prolonged shift durations, erratic meal schedules, chronic sleep deficit, and work-related stress
- **对照 / Comparison:** Assessment of knowledge, attitude, and practices (KAP) regarding metabolic syndrome through a structured questionnaire
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Moderate knowledge, positive attitude, and fair preventive practices regarding metabolic syndrome
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** 58.0% demonstrated moderate knowledge, 67.2% exhibited a positive attitude, and 60.5% demonstrated fair preventive practices
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in a specific region and among a specific occupational group, limiting generalizability
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While ambulance support personnel have moderate knowledge and positive attitudes towards metabolic syndrome, there is room for improvement in preventive practices, suggesting a need for targeted health promotion programs focusing on lifestyle modifications.

救护车支持人员经常长时间工作、不规律的用餐时间和持续的压力，这可能导致代谢问题。

印度的一项研究发现，尽管大多数人员对代谢综合征有适度的了解，但他们的预防实践不足。

改善饮食、运动和睡眠习惯可以帮助管理这些风险。

饮食至关重要； 考虑将高糖零食换成水果和蔬菜，目标是均衡饮食。

对于运动，即使在休息时间进行短暂的散步也有帮助，目标是每周至少进行150分钟的中等强度活动。

睡眠同样重要； 尝试保持规律的睡眠时间表，目标是每晚7-9小时。

这些变化可以与医疗保健提供者讨论，以制定个性化计划。

## English Plain-Language Version

Ambulance support personnel often work long shifts with irregular meal times and constant stress, which can lead to metabolic issues.

A study from India found that while most personnel had moderate knowledge about metabolic syndrome, their preventive practices were lacking.

Improving diet, exercise, and sleep habits can help manage these risks.

Diet is crucial; consider swapping high-sugar snacks for fruits and vegetables, aiming for balanced meals.

For exercise, even short walks during breaks can help, aiming for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.

Sleep is just as important; try to maintain a regular sleep schedule and aim for 7-9 hours per night.

These changes can be discussed with a healthcare provider to develop a personalized plan.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Ambulance support personnel often face occupational stressors that can lead to metabolic syndrome, increasing the risk of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes.

Understanding and implementing lifestyle changes is essential for this high-risk group.

Practical Takeaways

- Balanced meals with fruits and vegetables can help manage blood sugar levels.
- Short walks during breaks can contribute to the recommended weekly physical activity.
- Maintaining a regular sleep schedule is crucial for metabolic health.
- Discuss lifestyle modifications with a healthcare provider to develop a personalized plan.
- Small, consistent changes in diet, exercise, and sleep can lead to significant health improvements.
