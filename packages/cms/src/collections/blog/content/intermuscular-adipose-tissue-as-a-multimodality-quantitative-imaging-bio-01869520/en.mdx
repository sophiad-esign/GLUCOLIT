饮食和运动是管理肌间脂肪组织（IMAT）的关键。 IMAT位于肌肉群之间，与肌肉质量降低和胰岛素抵抗有关。 了解IMAT的作用有助于就餐食和活动做出明智的决策。

IMAT的积累与不良的代谢健康有关，包括胰岛素抵抗和2型糖尿病。 像CT和MRI这样的医学影像技术有助于量化IMAT，它与代谢功能障碍强烈相关。 这突出了饮食和运动在影响肌肉健康和整体代谢健康中的重要性。

富含全食物的饮食和定期运动可以帮助管理IMAT水平。 例如，选择糙米而不是白米或在餐食中加入更多蔬菜可能有益。 中等强度的运动，如快步走或骑自行车，也起着关键作用。 找到适合你的平衡点是必要的，不要过度劳累或放弃。

## English Plain-Language Version

Diet and exercise are key to managing intermuscular adipose tissue (IMAT). IMAT, located between muscle groups, is linked to reduced muscle quality and insulin resistance.

Understanding IMAT's role helps in making informed decisions about meals and activities.

IMAT accumulation is associated with poor metabolic health, including insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes. Medical imaging techniques like CT and MRI help quantify IMAT, which is strongly associated with metabolic dysfunction.

This highlights the importance of diet and exercise in influencing muscle health and overall metabolic health.

A diet rich in whole foods and regular exercise can help manage IMAT levels. For instance, choosing brown rice over white or adding more vegetables to meals can be beneficial.

Moderate-intensity exercise, such as brisk walking or cycling, also plays a crucial role. Finding a balance that works for you is essential, without overexerting or giving up.

The goal is to improve overall health, not just muscle composition. Sustainable changes should be discussed with healthcare providers. While IMAT is a significant marker, it is just one part of the broader picture of health.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding IMAT's impact on muscle health and metabolic profiles is essential for individuals with prediabetes or insulin resistance, guiding lifestyle interventions for better health.

Practical Takeaways

- Intermuscular adipose tissue (IMAT) is linked to muscle quality and insulin resistance.
- Diet and exercise can influence IMAT levels, impacting metabolic health.
- Choose whole foods and incorporate regular exercise to manage IMAT.
- Discuss sustainable health changes with healthcare providers.
- IMAT is one part of a broader approach to health and wellness.
