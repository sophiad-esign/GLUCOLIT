## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the effects of Physalis philadelphica husk infusion on obesity and associated metabolic disturbances in an experimental model?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Male Wistar rats allocated into three groups: healthy control, obese control group, and intervention group (husk infusion).
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Obesity induced by a high-fructose and high-saturated fat diet, followed by 16 weeks of husk infusion administration.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparisons made between the obese control group and the intervention group receiving the husk infusion.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Analysis of weight gain, serum glucose levels, free fatty acids, insulin sensitivity, hepatic steatosis, and oxidative stress markers.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Rats receiving the husk infusion showed significant reduction in weight gain, serum glucose levels, and free fatty acids. Oxidative stress markers decreased, and antioxidant enzyme activity in the liver increased.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on rats, and results may not directly translate to humans. Detailed composition of the infusion and long-term effects were not provided.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While suggesting benefits, readers should approach the results with caution. Further research, especially in human subjects, is needed to understand the full implications and safety of Physalis philadelphica husk infusion.
