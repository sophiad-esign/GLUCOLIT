## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How do various insulin resistance-related indices perform in distinguishing between prediabetes and normoglycemia?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 100 adults aged 25-55 years, equally divided between individuals with prediabetes and normoglycemia.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Cross-sectional analysis of 25 candidate indices, including blood-based, lipid-anthropometry combinations, and non-invasive anthropometric and body-composition measures.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of these indices with the Homeostasis Model Assessment for Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR) as a biological anchor.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant correlations found between 17 candidate indices and HOMA-IR; non-invasive indices showed good comparative performance.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Non-invasive indices, particularly the bioimpedance-based somatotype adiposity index, demonstrated high performance in assessing metabolic alterations associated with insulin resistance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study requires further validation, and the sample size is relatively small.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, non-invasive measures could offer a practical approach to monitor insulin resistance and inform lifestyle adjustments.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, deciding between a cheeseburger and a salad. For those with prediabetes, such choices can significantly impact their health trajectory.

A recent study evaluated various indices to assess insulin resistance, a key factor in prediabetes management.

The findings suggest that non-invasive measures, like body composition indices, could be as effective as traditional blood tests in monitoring insulin resistance.

This means that with simple, non-invasive tests, we can better understand our metabolic health and make informed lifestyle choices. Let's explore how this translates into actionable steps for your daily routine.

Diet plays a crucial role in managing insulin resistance. Swapping a high-sugar breakfast for a fiber-rich one, like whole grains, can help regulate blood sugar levels. Portion control is also key;

consider smaller, more frequent meals to maintain stable glucose levels. Regular physical activity can improve insulin sensitivity.

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week, gradually increasing intensity to enhance benefits while ensuring safety. Good sleep is vital for metabolic health.

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night, establishing a consistent sleep schedule to support your body's natural rhythms. Remember, these are general guidelines. Always consult with a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

The study's findings underscore the importance of early identification and intervention in prediabetes.

By integrating non-invasive indices into routine check-ups, we can facilitate timely lifestyle adjustments, potentially preventing the progression to type 2 diabetes.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding and managing insulin resistance is crucial for individuals with prediabetes to prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes.

This study provides insights into practical, non-invasive methods that can aid in early identification and intervention.
