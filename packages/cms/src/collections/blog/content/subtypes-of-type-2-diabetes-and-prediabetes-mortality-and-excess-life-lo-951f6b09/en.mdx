## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the subtypes of type 2 diabetes and prediabetes, and how are they associated with mortality?
- **研究对象 / Population:** 14,036 South Asian participants from the CArdiometabolic Risk Reduction cohort
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Unsupervised k-means clustering based on age, BMI, HbA1c, insulin resistance, and β-cell dysfunction
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of mortality and excess years of life lost between subtypes and normal glucose tolerance
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Cox hazard ratios for all-cause and cardiovascular disease mortality
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Three subtypes of T2D (SIDD, MIDD, SIRD) and two subtypes of prediabetes (IDPD, IRPD) were identified. Insulin-deficient subtypes had higher mortality hazards and lost more years of life.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted on a specific South Asian population, which may limit the generalizability of the findings.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** Readers with prediabetes should be aware of the potential differences in health risks associated with subtypes and consider discussing these findings with their healthcare provider to develop a personalized management plan.

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine you're at a crossroads, unsure which path leads to better health. New research helps us understand the different subtypes of prediabetes, guiding us towards more personalized care.

Prediabetes is a warning sign, but not all types carry the same risks. Some are more closely linked to higher mortality rates. Knowing your subtype can help tailor lifestyle changes to reduce these risks.

The study analyzed South Asian participants, identifying subtypes based on factors like age, BMI, HbA1c, insulin resistance, and β-cell dysfunction.

It found that insulin-deficient subtypes had higher mortality hazards and lost more years of life.

This doesn't mean everyone with prediabetes will face the same outcome, but it does highlight the importance of early and targeted intervention. It's not just about managing blood sugar;

it's about understanding your unique health profile to make the best decisions for your future. Discuss these findings with your healthcare provider to develop a plan that fits your specific needs.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Prediabetes is a critical health milestone that can lead to type 2 diabetes if not managed properly.

Understanding the subtypes of prediabetes can help individuals and healthcare providers make more informed decisions about treatment and lifestyle changes, potentially preventing the progression to diabetes and reducing long-term health

risks.

Practical Takeaways

- Understanding your prediabetes subtype can help personalize your health management plan.
- Insulin-deficient subtypes of prediabetes are associated with higher mortality risks.
- Early intervention is crucial for individuals with prediabetes, especially those with insulin-deficient subtypes.
- Discuss your prediabetes subtype with your healthcare provider to develop a targeted intervention plan.
- Lifestyle changes, including diet and exercise, can significantly impact health outcomes for those with prediabetes.
