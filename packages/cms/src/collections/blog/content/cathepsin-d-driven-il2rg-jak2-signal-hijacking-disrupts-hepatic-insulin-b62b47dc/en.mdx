## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the role of cathepsin D in hepatic insulin resistance and its potential as a preventative strategy for prediabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals with obesity/MASLD and HIR cellular models
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Assessment of mRNA expression/protein expressions/activity of CTSD and the administration of CTSD inhibitor Pepstatin A (PepA) to mice with obesity/MASLD
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of CTSD levels and activity in individuals and mice with obesity/MASLD versus controls
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Significant increases in CTSD levels and activity associated with HIR; inhibition of CTSD with PepA improved HIR and glucose metabolism
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Increased CTSD suppresses hepatic insulin sensitivity via activating the IL2RG-JAK2-STAT3 axis, highlighting CTSD inhibition as a preventative strategy for prediabetes
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study focuses on a specific molecular mechanism and does not provide a comprehensive dietary intervention plan; results are from cellular models and animal studies, which may not directly translate to humans
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study provides insights into the molecular mechanisms of hepatic insulin resistance, readers should focus on the broader implications for diet and lifestyle changes to manage prediabetes. It's important to consult with a healthcare provider for personalized advice.

## English Plain-Language Version

Hepatic insulin resistance is an early sign of prediabetes, critical for maintaining glucose balance. Recent research has shed light on the role of cathepsin D in this process.

Increased cathepsin D activity is associated with hepatic insulin resistance, suggesting that targeting this enzyme could prevent prediabetes.

Diet modifications, such as choosing low-fat options and increasing fiber, may help reduce cathepsin D activity.

Obesity and related conditions like NAFLD heighten the risk of prediabetes, emphasizing the importance of maintaining a healthy weight. Sustainable lifestyle changes, not drastic diets, are key.

Adequate sleep is essential for glucose metabolism. Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night to support blood sugar management.

Personalized dietary and lifestyle changes are crucial for managing prediabetes. Consulting with healthcare providers ensures tailored strategies.
