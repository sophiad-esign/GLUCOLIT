## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Does maternal consumption of interesterified palm oil affect metabolic health in offspring?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Female C57BL/6J mice
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Consumption of a normocaloric diet containing interesterified palm oil before and during pregnancy
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison between mice fed palm oil and those fed interesterified palm oil
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Metabolic profile of dams, glucose and insulin tolerance, tissue-specific insulin signaling, inflammatory profile, and hypothalamic neuropeptide expression in offspring
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Maternal INP exposure led to hyperphagia, increased body weight, and reduced energy expenditure in dams. Offspring showed sex-specific metabolic impairments, with female offspring exhibiting glucose intolerance and impaired insulin sensitivity, and both sexes showing a pro-inflammatory profile.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and results may not directly translate to humans. The study's findings are specific to interesterified palm oil and may not apply to all types of dietary fats.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While this study suggests that maternal diet can influence offspring's metabolic health, it is important to consider the broader context of a healthy diet and consult with healthcare providers for personalized dietary advice.

## 原文精华摘要

怀孕期间的饮食可以对孩子的健康产生持久影响。 改性棕榈油，作为食品工业中常见的脂肪，可能会导致后代胰岛素抵抗和炎症。

在一项研究中，雌性小鼠在怀孕前和怀孕期间被喂食这种油。 这些母亲表现出食欲增加和体重增加，这影响了她们后代的代谢。

雌性后代在处理葡萄糖方面有困难，而雄性后代显示出胰岛素抵抗的分子迹象。 两性都显示出炎症，表明这种油对代谢健康的影响。

了解这些影响可以帮助指导预防胰岛素抵抗和相关疾病的策略，特别是对于孕妇和计划怀孕的女性。

## English Plain-Language Version

Diet during pregnancy can have lasting effects on a child's health. Interesterified palm oil, a common food industry fat, may lead to insulin resistance and inflammation in offspring.

In a study, female mice were fed this oil before and during pregnancy. The mothers showed increased appetite and weight, which affected their offspring's metabolism.

Female offspring struggled with glucose processing, while males showed molecular signs of insulin resistance. Both sexes displayed inflammation, indicating the oil's impact on metabolic health.

Understanding these effects can guide strategies to prevent insulin resistance and related disorders, especially for pregnant women and those planning to conceive.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the impact of maternal diet on offspring's metabolic health is crucial for developing strategies to prevent insulin resistance and related metabolic disorders.

This study provides insights into how specific dietary fats can program long-term health outcomes, which is particularly relevant for pregnant women and those planning to conceive.

Practical Takeaways

- Maternal diet can influence offspring's metabolic health.
- Interesterified palm oil in maternal diet may lead to insulin resistance and inflammation in offspring.
- Effects of maternal diet on offspring can differ between males and females.
- A balanced diet with consideration of fat types is important for pregnant women.
- It's crucial to discuss dietary choices with healthcare providers for personalized advice.
