## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the association between the Planetary Health Diet score and cardiometabolic risk factors among Korean adults?
- **研究对象 / Population:** The study population consisted of 25,336 Korean adults aged 19 years and older from the 2016-2020 Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** The Planetary Health Diet score was calculated based on the Lancet Commission guidelines, and participants were categorized into quartiles accordingly.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Multivariable logistic regression models were used to examine associations with various cardiometabolic risk factors.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** A higher Planetary Health Diet score was significantly associated with lower odds of hypercholesterolemia in women and reduced prevalence of certain metabolic issues.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Higher adherence to the Planetary Health Diet was associated with selected cardiometabolic markers, particularly among Korean women.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's findings were more pronounced in women, and no significant associations were observed in men. Further research is needed to confirm the applicability of the Planetary Health Diet score in Korean dietary contexts.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests potential benefits of the Planetary Health Diet in managing metabolic risks, particularly for Korean women, it's important to approach these findings with caution and not overclaim causation. The diet provides a framework for discussions with healthcare providers about sustainable dietary choices to manage metabolic risks.
