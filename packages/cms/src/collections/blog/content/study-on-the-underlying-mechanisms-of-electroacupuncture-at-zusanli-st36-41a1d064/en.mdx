## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can electroacupuncture at 'Zusanli' (ST36) restore blood glucose homeostasis in prediabetes mice, and what are the underlying mechanisms?
- **研究对象 / Population:** C57BL/6J mice were used to model acute hyperglycemia, low-risk prediabetes, and high-risk prediabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Electroacupuncture (EA) at 'Zusanli' (ST36) was applied with parameters of 1 mA, 10 Hz for 20 minutes.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Mice were divided into normal, model, and EA groups for comparison.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Blood glucose, glucose tolerance, serum HbA1c, insulin, and glucagon levels were measured. Pancreatic morphology and gene/protein expressions related to β-cell function were also assessed.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** EA lowered blood glucose and increased insulin levels in prediabetes mice, with the most significant effect in the low-risk stage. Changes in gene/protein expressions related to β-cell function and endoplasmic reticulum stress were observed.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study was conducted in mice, and the results may not directly translate to humans. The mechanisms require further investigation.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While the study suggests that electroacupuncture may help manage prediabetes, readers should be cautious about applying these findings to human health without further research.
