## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What is the impact of circadian gene variants on glycemic parameters and insulin resistance in type 2 diabetes?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Populations with, or at risk for, type 2 diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Circadian gene variants or expression.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Glycemic parameters and insulin resistance indices.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Fasting plasma glucose, HOMA-IR, T2DM risk.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Certain circadian gene variants are associated with impaired glycemic control and insulin resistance.
- **局限性 / Limits:** Observational and heterogeneous evidence; no meta-analysis or formal heterogeneity metric was performed.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** While genetic factors play a role, lifestyle factors like sleep can be modified to manage the risk of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes.

## English Plain-Language Version

Biological rhythms are essential for maintaining health, and disruptions can lead to metabolic issues.

Research indicates that specific gene variants can affect our circadian rhythms, impacting glucose metabolism.

Establishing a regular sleep schedule and ensuring adequate rest can support better metabolic health.

Consulting with healthcare providers can help address sleep issues and related metabolic risks.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Understanding the genetic basis of circadian rhythms is important for diabetes management.

Lifestyle modifications, including sleep habits, can mitigate the risks associated with certain gene variants.

Practical Takeaways

- Circadian gene variants may influence glucose metabolism and diabetes risk.
