- **研究问题 / Research question:** Can local heat therapy improve glycemic control and decrease frailty risk in older adults with prediabetes?

- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Local heat therapy (LHT) applied as a standalone intervention and as a precondition for high-intensity interval training (HIIT).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Sham-controlled groups and HIIT group.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Glycemic control, skeletal muscle architecture, frailty risk, and exercise adaptations.

## English Plain-Language Version

### Study Background Glycemic dysregulation in older adults can lead to muscle loss and increased risk of frailty, potentially escalating into type 2 diabetes.

The HEAT trial explores local heat therapy (LHT) as an alternative to high-intensity interval training (HIIT) for improving muscle health and glycemic control.

### Core Findings The HEAT trial is a randomized, sham-controlled study investigating the effects of LHT on glycemic control and frailty risk in older adults with prediabetes.

It includes two phases: LHT as a standalone intervention and LHT as a precondition for HIIT.

### Your Interpretation and Critique LHT may improve muscle architecture and precondition muscles for exercise, enhancing HIIT adherence and effectiveness.

The trial's outcomes will determine the practicality and scalability of this approach.

### Clinical/Business Insights Incorporating heat therapy into lifestyle could complement glycemic management for prediabetes. The potential of LHT for home use could lead to innovative health tech products.

### Why This Matters for Prediabetes

This study is relevant as it explores a novel, non-invasive approach to manage glycemic control and reduce frailty risk in older adults with prediabetes, which could have significant implications for diabetes prevention and health

technology innovation.

### Practical Takeaways

- Local heat therapy may offer a practical approach to improve glycemic control in prediabetes.
- The HEAT trial is a significant step towards understanding the effects of LHT on muscle health and frailty.
- Exercise adherence is a challenge, and LHT could precondition muscles to enhance HIIT effectiveness.
- The outcomes of the HEAT trial will be instrumental in evaluating the scalability of LHT interventions.
