## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How does the nutritional landscape for cystic fibrosis patients change with the advent of highly effective modulator therapy (HEMT), and what are the associated metabolic complications?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Cystic fibrosis patients
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Highly effective modulator therapy (HEMT)
- **对照 / Comparison:** Traditional high-calorie diets and nutritional supplementation
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Overweight, obesity, metabolic complications
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** With HEMT, there is a rising prevalence of overweight and obesity in CF patients, associated with metabolic complications such as hypertension, dyslipidemia, insulin resistance, hepatic steatosis, and obstructive sleep apnea.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study is a review and does not provide new experimental data. The evidence specific to pharmacologic weight management in CF patients is limited.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** CF patients and healthcare providers should consider the shift from traditional high-calorie diets to personalized nutritional plans that prioritize metabolic health, recognizing the limitations of current evidence and the need for further research.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，尽管睡了一个完整的夜晚，醒来时却感到疲倦，而且发现你的血糖水平比平时高。 这可能是胰岛素抵抗的迹象，对于囊性纤维化（CF）患者来说，这是一个常见问题。 随着治疗的进步，许多CF患者现在活得更长，但这种长寿带来了新的挑战，比如管理体重和代谢健康。

鉴于CF人群中超重和肥胖的流行率日益增加，传统的高热量饮食以对抗营养不良的方法正在被重新评估。 相反，一个更个性化的营养计划，考虑到代谢健康，正变得至关重要。 这种转变至关重要，因为超重与一系列并发症有关，包括高血压、高胆固醇和睡眠呼吸暂停。 这不仅仅是计算卡路里的问题；

而是选择支持肺功能和代谢健康的正确食物。 例如，用复杂碳水化合物和瘦蛋白替换高糖零食可以帮助管理血糖水平。 即使只是每天散步，定期进行体育活动也可以提高胰岛素敏感性和整体健康。 谈到睡眠时，建立一致的睡前例行程序和创造有利于睡眠的环境可以帮助改善休息的质量和持续时间。

这些变化不是一刀切的； 它们需要个性化的规划和医疗监督。 但通过专注于均衡饮食、定期锻炼和良好的睡眠卫生，CF患者可以更好地管理他们的代谢健康，提高他们的生活质量。

## English Plain-Language Version

Imagine waking up feeling tired despite a full night's sleep, only to find that your blood sugar levels are higher than usual. This could be a sign of insulin resistance, a common issue for those with cystic fibrosis (CF).

As treatments improve, many CF patients are now living longer, but this longevity comes with new challenges, such as managing weight and metabolic health.

The traditional approach of high-calorie diets to combat malnutrition is being reevaluated in light of the increasing prevalence of overweight and obesity in the CF population.

Instead, a more personalized nutritional plan that considers metabolic health is becoming essential.

This shift is crucial because excess weight is linked to a range of complications, including hypertension, high cholesterol, and sleep apnea. It's not just about counting calories;

it's about choosing the right foods that support both lung function and metabolic health. For example, swapping out high-sugar snacks for complex carbohydrates and lean proteins can help manage blood sugar levels.

Incorporating regular physical activity, even if it's just a daily walk, can also improve insulin sensitivity and overall health.

And when it comes to sleep, establishing a consistent bedtime routine and creating a sleep-conducive environment can help improve both the quality and duration of rest. These changes are not one-size-fits-all;

they require individualized planning and medical supervision. But by focusing on a balanced diet, regular exercise, and good sleep hygiene, CF patients can better manage their metabolic health and improve their quality of life.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

Cystic fibrosis patients face a unique set of challenges when it comes to nutrition and metabolic health.

As life expectancy increases, the focus shifts from preventing undernutrition to managing the complications associated with overweight and obesity.

Understanding these changes is crucial for patients to make informed decisions about their diet and lifestyle.

Practical Takeaways

- A balanced diet with complex carbohydrates and lean proteins can help manage blood sugar levels.
- Regular physical activity, even a daily walk, can improve insulin sensitivity.
- Establishing a consistent bedtime routine and sleep-conducive environment can enhance sleep quality.
- Individualized nutritional strategies are essential for managing metabolic health in CF patients.
- Consult with healthcare providers to develop personalized plans that consider both pulmonary and metabolic health.
