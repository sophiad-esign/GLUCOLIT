## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** How can lifestyle modifications prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes in patients with chronic kidney disease who are at risk?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Patients with chronic kidney disease who are prediabetic or at risk for incident type 2 diabetes.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Lifestyle modifications including diet, exercise, and sleep habits.
- **对照 / Comparison:** Real-world adherence compared to trial conditions.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Prevention of type 2 diabetes, impact on cardiorenal health.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Lifestyle modification is the foundation for diabetes prevention, supported by decades of evidence. Adaptation to dietary and functional constraints of kidney disease is necessary. Metformin is a reasonable pharmacologic addition in selected patients.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The evidence is narrative in approach, drawing on current guidelines and pivotal trials selected for topical relevance rather than through a predefined systematic search. Real-world adherence falls short of what trial conditions achieved.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with chronic kidney disease and prediabetes, making lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes and benefit cardiorenal health. It's important to adapt these changes to personal dietary and functional realities and to consult with healthcare providers for a tailored approach.

## English Plain-Language Version

Choosing the right path to health starts with daily meals and activities. For those with prediabetes and kidney issues, making the right choices can protect the heart and kidneys. Lifestyle changes are not just about managing blood sugar;

they're a strategy that benefits the heart and kidneys. It's about what, how much, and when you eat. Small changes like choosing brown rice over white or adding vegetables can make a difference.

Exercise should fit into your life, and sleep quality matters. These steps can prevent a serious condition affecting many.

Prediabetes is a warning sign that can be managed with lifestyle changes. It's about organ health, not just individual health. Making these changes early can alter the disease's course.

Remember, lifestyle changes are the first step, but they don't replace medical advice or treatment. Always consult with a healthcare provider.

Taking control of your health means making small, sustainable changes to your diet and activity levels, reducing the risk of developing type 2 diabetes. It's a long-term commitment to a healthier lifestyle.

Why This Matters for Prediabetes

The convergence of cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney disease, and type 2 diabetes affects a significant portion of the population.

Understanding how lifestyle modifications can prevent the progression to type 2 diabetes is essential for individuals at risk, particularly those with chronic kidney disease, as it can have a profound impact on both heart and kidney health.

Practical Takeaways

- Lifestyle changes are crucial in preventing type 2 diabetes, especially for those with chronic kidney disease.
- Small dietary swaps and regular exercise can significantly impact your health.
- Prediabetes is a warning sign that can be managed with early lifestyle interventions.
- Always consult with a healthcare provider to discuss the best plan for your situation.
- Preventing type 2 diabetes is a long-term commitment to a healthier lifestyle.
