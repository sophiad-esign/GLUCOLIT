## 审核用证据卡片

- **研究问题 / Research question:** What are the temporal trends in the incidence and prevalence of prediabetes in Ontario, and how can diet and lifestyle changes help in managing this condition?
- **研究对象 / Population:** Individuals aged 20-105 years in Ontario with valid glucose testing, excluding results in the diabetes range.
- **干预或暴露 / Intervention or exposure:** Prediabetes defined using laboratory criteria: glycated hemoglobin 6.0% and 6.4%, impaired glucose tolerance (2-hour glucose of 7.8-11.0 mmol/L after a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test), or impaired fasting glucose (6.1-6.9 mmol/L).
- **对照 / Comparison:** Comparison of incidence and prevalence rates of prediabetes between 2015 and 2024.
- **观察指标 / Outcomes:** Age-standardized incidence increased by 20% from 15.2 per 1,000 persons in 2015 to 18.2 per 1,000 in 2024, while prevalence increased by 25% from 11% in 2015 to 14% in 2024.
- **主要发现 / Main findings:** Prediabetes incidence and prevalence increased substantially in Ontario between 2015 and 2024, with a disproportionate burden in lower socioeconomic and urban communities.
- **局限性 / Limits:** The study's limitations include its retrospective nature and reliance on laboratory criteria for prediabetes diagnosis. It also highlights the need for more targeted prevention strategies, especially in socioeconomically disadvantaged and urban communities where the burden is higher.
- **读者怎么理解 / Reader meaning:** For individuals with prediabetes, the study suggests the importance of early intervention through diet and lifestyle changes to reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes. It emphasizes the need for targeted prevention strategies, especially in communities with higher burdens of prediabetes.

## 原文精华摘要

想象一下，当你醒来时发现自己的健康正处于十字路口——糖尿病前期已经进入了你的生活。 这是一个警示信号，一个在2型糖尿病成为现实之前改变方向的机会。 来自安大略省的研究表明，糖尿病前期的患病率正在上升，每年都有更多的人受到影响。 这是一个采取行动的呼吁，以了解饮食和生活方式如何能够产生差异。

糖尿病前期是一种代谢状态，如果不加以控制，可能会导致2型糖尿病。 这是一种血糖水平高于正常但不足以被归类为糖尿病的状况。 该研究分析了安大略省的数据，发现2015年至2024年间，糖尿病前期的发病率和患病率分别增加了20%和25%。 这一趋势不仅仅是一个数字；

它反映了需要针对性预防策略的需求。

研究结果强调了早期干预的重要性。 这不仅仅是为了避免糖尿病； 这是为了改善整体健康和生活质量。 对于糖尿病前期患者来说，改变饮食和增加体力活动可以显著降低发展为2型糖尿病的风险。

虽然研究没有提供具体的饮食建议，但它确实表明，关注更健康的饮食习惯至关重要。 这可能意味着将更多的全谷物、瘦蛋白和大量水果和蔬菜纳入你的饮食中。 它也是关于饮食控制和正念饮食——意识到你正在消费什么以及消费多少。

锻炼是另一个关键组成部分。 定期的体力活动可以帮助改善胰岛素敏感性，降低患糖尿病的风险。 研究暗示需要有结构的锻炼方法，这可能涉及有氧运动和力量训练的混合。

睡眠经常被忽视，但在代谢健康中扮演着重要角色。 一致的睡眠时间表、足够的持续时间和良好的睡眠质量是必不可少的。 睡眠模式的中断会影响葡萄糖代谢和胰岛素敏感性。

研究的局限性包括其回顾性性质和依赖实验室标准来诊断糖尿病前期。 它还强调了需要更多的针对性预防策略，特别是在社会经济弱势和城市社区，那里的负担更高。

从实际意义上讲，这意味着糖尿病前期患者应该与医疗保健提供者合作，制定一个包括饮食变化、定期锻炼和良好的睡眠卫生的个性化计划。 这不是一个放之四海而皆准的方法，而是一个考虑到个人需求和情况的量身定制的策略。

关键的收获是明确的：糖尿病前期是一个需要关注的严重健康问题。 通过做出明智的生活方式改变，个人可以控制自己的健康，并降低发展为2型糖尿病的风险。

为什么和糖尿病前期有关？

糖尿病前期是一个关键的健康问题，如果不加以解决，可能会导致2型糖尿病等更严重的状况。 理解早期干预和生活方式改变的重要性，对于糖尿病前期患者控制自己的健康和预防糖尿病的发展至关重要。

- 包括饮食和锻炼在内的生活方式改变可以显著降低发展为2型糖尿病的风险。

- 良好的睡眠卫生，包括一致的睡眠时间表和质量，在代谢健康中扮演着重要角色。

Prediabetes is a metabolic state that, if left unchecked, can lead to type 2 diabetes. It's a condition where blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not high enough to be classified as diabetes.

The study analyzed data from Ontario and found that the incidence and prevalence of prediabetes increased by 20% and 25% respectively between 2015 and 2024. This trend is not just a number;

it's a reflection of the need for targeted prevention strategies.

The study's findings underscore the importance of early intervention. It's not just about avoiding diabetes; it's about improving overall health and quality of life.

For those with prediabetes, making changes to diet and increasing physical activity can significantly reduce the risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes.

While the study doesn't provide specific dietary recommendations, it does suggest that a focus on healthier eating habits is crucial.

This could mean incorporating more whole grains, lean proteins, and plenty of fruits and vegetables into your diet. It's also about portion control and mindful eating—being aware of what and how much you're consuming.

Exercise is another key component. Regular physical activity can help improve insulin sensitivity and reduce the risk of diabetes.

The study hints at the need for a structured approach to exercise, which could involve a mix of aerobic activities and strength training.

Sleep is often overlooked but plays a significant role in metabolic health. A consistent sleep schedule, adequate duration, and good quality sleep are essential.

Disruptions in sleep patterns can affect glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity.

The study's limitations include its retrospective nature and the reliance on laboratory criteria for prediabetes diagnosis.

It also highlights the need for more targeted prevention strategies, especially in socioeconomically disadvantaged and urban communities where the burden is higher.

In practical terms, this means that individuals with prediabetes should work with healthcare providers to develop a personalized plan that includes dietary changes, regular exercise, and good sleep hygiene.

It's not a one-size-fits-all approach but a tailored strategy that considers individual needs and circumstances.

The key takeaways are clear: prediabetes is a serious health concern that requires attention. By making informed lifestyle changes, individuals can take control of their health and reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
